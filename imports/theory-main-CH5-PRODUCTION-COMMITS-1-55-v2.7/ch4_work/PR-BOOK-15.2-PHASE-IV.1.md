# PR-BOOK-15.2 - Phase IV.1: Advanced Partial Derivatives

## Scope

This enhancement completes the mathematical-completeness pass for Section 2.4, `Partial Derivatives`, bringing it to the same pedagogical depth as the expanded derivatives section.

## Changes

- Added rigorous limit definitions for coordinate partial derivatives.
- Added first-principles worked derivations and multivariable examples.
- Distinguished continuity, existence of partial derivatives, and full differentiability.
- Derived the tangent plane as the best local affine approximation.
- Developed the total differential, error approximation, and measurement uncertainty.
- Added the multivariable chain rule and total/material derivatives.
- Introduced directional derivatives and their relation to the gradient.
- Expanded mixed partial derivatives and the Clairaut--Schwarz theorem.
- Developed the Jacobian matrix, determinant, and local area/volume scaling.
- Derived polar, cylindrical, and spherical coordinate volume elements.
- Developed the Hessian matrix, eigenvalue interpretation, and classification of extrema.
- Added applications in thermodynamics, electromagnetism, fluid mechanics, and quantum mechanics.
- Added TikZ illustrations, common mistakes, exercises, summary, and forward transition.
- Synchronized canonical and compatibility source trees.
