\section{Repository Integration and Chapter Dependencies}
\label{sec:ch5-integration}

Chapter 5 now uses the canonical notation
\[
z=x+iy,\qquad \bar z=x-iy,\qquad z=re^{i\theta},\qquad f(z)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y).
\]
The terms \gls{complexnumber}, \gls{argument}, \gls{holomorphic}, \gls{cauchyriemann}, \gls{contourintegral}, \gls{laurentseries}, \gls{singularity}, and \gls{residue} are integrated into the global glossary. The notation table records the conventions for argument, complex conjugation, contour orientation, residues, and branches.
