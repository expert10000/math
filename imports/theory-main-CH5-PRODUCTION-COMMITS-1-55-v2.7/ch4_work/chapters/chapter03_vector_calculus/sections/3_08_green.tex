\section{Green's Theorem}
\label{sec:greens-theorem}
For a positively oriented boundary $C=\partial D$ in the plane,
\[
\oint_C (P\,dx+Q\,dy)=\iint_D\left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)dA.
\]
Green's theorem relates circulation around a planar boundary to curl distributed through its interior. It is the two-dimensional precursor of Stokes' theorem.
