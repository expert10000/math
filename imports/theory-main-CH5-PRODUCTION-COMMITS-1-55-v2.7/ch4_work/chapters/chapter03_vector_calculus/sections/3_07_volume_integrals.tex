\section{Volume Integrals}
\label{sec:volume-integrals}
\subsection{Definition}
For a scalar density $\rho$ over a volume $V$,
\[
Q=\iiint_V \rho\,dV.
\]
This converts a local density into a total quantity such as mass, charge, or probability.
\subsection{Coordinate Dependence}
The volume element carries the Jacobian of the chosen coordinates; for example, $dV=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$ in spherical coordinates.
