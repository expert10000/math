\section{Surface Integrals}
\label{sec:surface-integrals}
\subsection{Introduction}
A surface integral accumulates a scalar field over an area or measures the flux of a vector field through an oriented surface.
\subsection{Flux Definition}
\[
\Phi=\iint_S \mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf A,
\qquad d\mathbf A=\hat{\mathbf n}\,dA.
\]
The sign depends on the chosen orientation of the unit normal.
\subsection{Applications}
Surface integrals describe electric and magnetic flux, fluid flow through membranes, and probability-current flow through boundaries.
