\section{Gauss' Theorem}
\label{sec:gauss-theorem}
For a volume $V$ bounded by the closed surface $\partial V$,
\[
\iint_{\partial V}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf A
=\iiint_V \nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV.
\]
Gauss' theorem converts outward flux through a closed boundary into the total divergence contained inside. It is fundamental to electrostatics, gravitation, fluid continuity, and conservation laws.
