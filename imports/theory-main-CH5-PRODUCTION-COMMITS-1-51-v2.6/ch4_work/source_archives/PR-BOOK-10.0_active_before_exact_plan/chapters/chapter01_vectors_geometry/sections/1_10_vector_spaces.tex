\section{Vector Spaces}
\label{sec:vector-spaces}
\subsection{Introduction}
The familiar arrows of Euclidean geometry are examples of a more general structure: a vector space.
\subsection{Axiomatic Structure}
A vector space is closed under vector addition and scalar multiplication and satisfies the standard associativity, commutativity, identity, inverse, and distributive axioms.
\subsection{Beyond Geometric Arrows}
Polynomials, matrices, functions, and quantum states can all form vector spaces. This abstraction is the bridge from elementary vector geometry to Hilbert space.
\subsection{Looking Ahead}
Later chapters will add inner products, norms, linear operators, eigenvalue problems, and complex vector spaces.
