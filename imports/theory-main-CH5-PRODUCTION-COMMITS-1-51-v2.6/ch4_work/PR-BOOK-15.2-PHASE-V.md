# PR-BOOK-15.2 Phase V - The Gradient

## Scope

This phase expands Section 2.5 from a concise introduction into a graduate-level treatment of the gradient and its physical applications.

## Main changes

- Motivation through height, temperature, pressure, and electric-potential fields.
- Formal definition of the gradient and the del operator.
- Derivation of directional derivatives from the chain rule.
- Proof of steepest ascent and orthogonality to level sets.
- Tangent-plane relation and optimization interpretation.
- Applications to Fourier heat flow, conservative forces, gravitation, electrostatics, and fluid pressure.
- Curvilinear-coordinate formulas.
- Introduction of the Laplacian and quantum momentum and kinetic-energy operators.
- Fully solved examples, exercises, common mistakes, summary, and transition to divergence and curl.
