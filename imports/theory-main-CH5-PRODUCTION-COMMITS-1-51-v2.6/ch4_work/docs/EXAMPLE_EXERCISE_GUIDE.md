# Worked Example and Exercise Guide

## Worked-example structure

1. **Problem.** State the task precisely.
2. **Given.** Collect known quantities and assumptions.
3. **Strategy.** Explain the method before calculating.
4. **Solution.** Show complete, readable steps.
5. **Interpretation.** Explain the physical or mathematical meaning.
6. **Common mistake.** Identify a likely error where useful.

## Exercise levels

- **Basic:** direct use of definitions or one-step calculations.
- **Intermediate:** combines several ideas or requires interpretation.
- **Advanced:** multi-stage derivation or unfamiliar application.
- **Challenge:** synthesis, proof, modeling, or open-ended extension.

## Recommended chapter distribution

- 40% basic.
- 30% intermediate.
- 20% advanced.
- 10% challenge.

## Exercise categories

- Conceptual questions.
- Derivations.
- Numerical calculations.
- Graphical interpretation.
- Proofs.
- Computational exercises.
- Physical applications.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Solvable from chapter material.
- [ ] Difficulty label is appropriate.
- [ ] Variables and units are unambiguous.
- [ ] No hidden assumptions.
- [ ] Selected solutions are complete and verifiable.
