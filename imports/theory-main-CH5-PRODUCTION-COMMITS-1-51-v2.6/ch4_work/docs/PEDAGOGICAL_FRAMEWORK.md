# Pedagogical Framework

## Mission

Teach readers to think like physicists: connect observation, mathematical structure, interpretation, and prediction.

## Core sequence

Why?

↓

What?

↓

How?

↓

Where is it used?

## Layered explanation

Each major concept should be explained at three levels:

1. Visual or geometric.
2. Qualitative or physical.
3. Quantitative or formal.

## Success criteria

The reader should be able to:

- explain the concept intuitively,
- state its formal definition,
- reproduce the central derivation,
- interpret the result physically,
- solve representative problems,
- recognize where the concept reappears later.
