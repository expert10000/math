# PR-BOOK-15.2 - Phase III: Derivatives

## Scope

This phase continues the mathematical-completeness pass for Chapter 2 by expanding Section 2.3, `Derivatives`.

## Changes

- Rebuilt the section around the progression from finite change to instantaneous change.
- Added the secant-to-tangent limiting construction and a new TikZ figure.
- Added a precise definition of differentiability and the theorem that differentiability implies continuity.
- Added complete first-principles derivations for `x^2`, `x^3`, `1/x`, and `sqrt(x)`.
- Added tangent-line equations, local linearization, error interpretation, and differentials.
- Expanded physical applications to velocity, acceleration, current, power, and force.
- Added derivations and explanations of the linearity, power, product, quotient, and chain rules.
- Added derivatives of common elementary functions and the role of radian measure.
- Added implicit differentiation, higher derivatives, concavity, notation comparison, common errors, and diagnostic checks.
- Added a forward bridge to the Schrodinger equation, momentum operator, and kinetic-energy operator.
- Synchronized canonical and compatibility source trees.
