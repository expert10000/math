\section{Cartesian Coordinate System}

Define three mutually perpendicular axes.

\[
x,
\qquad
y,
\qquad
z.
\]

Their associated unit vectors are

\[
\hat{\mathbf x},
\qquad
\hat{\mathbf y},
\qquad
\hat{\mathbf z}.
\]

Each has unit length

\[
|\hat{\mathbf x}|=
|\hat{\mathbf y}|=
|\hat{\mathbf z}|=1.
\]

They satisfy

\[
\hat{\mathbf x}\cdot\hat{\mathbf y}=0,
\]

etc.

Therefore they are orthonormal.
