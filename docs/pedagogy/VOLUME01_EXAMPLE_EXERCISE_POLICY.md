# Volume I expansion policy

## Main text

Each enriched chapter receives three substantial worked examples positioned
immediately after concept sections. Each example must:

1. introduce or consolidate a method;
2. show the calculation or proof in full;
3. interpret the result;
4. include an internal check when a natural check exists.

The three existing brief examples remain untouched.

## Exercises

Each enriched chapter keeps its existing eight exercise/hint/solution triads
and receives sixteen additional graded triads. The supplementary layer contains:

- five standard computations;
- four proofs;
- three counterexample / hypothesis-testing tasks;
- two applications or investigations;
- two challenge problems.

Thus an enriched chapter has approximately 24 exercises total, within the
series target of 20–35.

## Non-reduction contract

The expansion process may add text only. Existing source is protected by a
baseline SHA-256 hash after removing only marked expansion blocks. The audit
fails if protected text changes.
