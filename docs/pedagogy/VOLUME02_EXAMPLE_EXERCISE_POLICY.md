# Volume II example/exercise expansion policy

## Worked examples

New examples must be placed immediately after the concept or theorem mechanism
they teach. They must contain an explicit estimate, calculation, proof
strategy, or verification; one-line illustrations do not count as enrichment.

Real-analysis examples should expose quantifier structure and hypothesis use.
Whenever possible, the example includes a check:

- sharp upper/lower bound;
- epsilon choice;
- residual estimate;
- derivative/Jacobian verification;
- compactness or connectedness hypothesis check;
- supremum-norm error;
- Darboux-gap estimate.

## Exercises

The supplementary set per enriched chapter is graded by role:

1. Standard computations
2. Proofs
3. Counterexamples and hypothesis testing
4. Applications and investigations
5. Challenge problems

Every new exercise is followed by a mathematical hint and a complete solution.

## Expansion only

Existing theorem statements, proofs, worked examples, solved dossiers,
exercises, hints, solutions, labels, learning outcomes, and chapter summaries
must not be reduced or rewritten by this package.
