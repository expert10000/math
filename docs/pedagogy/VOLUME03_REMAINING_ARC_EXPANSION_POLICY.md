# Volume III remaining-arc example/exercise expansion

This package completes the pedagogical expansion of III/15–III/28 while preserving all
existing source.

Each remaining chapter receives three concept-placed worked examples and sixteen new
exercise/hint/solution triads: five standard computations, four proofs, three
counterexamples/hypothesis tests, two applications, and two challenges.

The new examples emphasize concrete distribution actions, singular support, weak
derivatives, Sobolev membership and energy estimates, mollifier density, Lax--Milgram
coercivity, fundamental/Green kernels, Sturm--Liouville jumps, maximum-principle
comparisons, and transform/spectral PDE solution formulas.

All additions use VOL03-EXPANSION markers. Removing those blocks must reproduce the
Commit-1 protected-source hashes exactly.
