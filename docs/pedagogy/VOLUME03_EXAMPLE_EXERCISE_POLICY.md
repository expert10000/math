# Volume III example/exercise expansion policy

Volume III additions are theorem- and estimate-driven. Each enriched chapter receives three
concept-placed worked examples and sixteen graded exercise/hint/solution triads. Existing
theorems, proofs, examples, dossiers, exercises, hints, solutions, labels, outcomes, and
summaries are preserved.

The sixteen added exercises are grouped as five standard computations, four proofs, three
counterexamples/hypothesis tests, two applications, and two challenges.

All additions are bracketed by VOL03-EXPANSION markers. Stripping those blocks must reproduce
the Commit-1 protected source exactly by SHA-256.
