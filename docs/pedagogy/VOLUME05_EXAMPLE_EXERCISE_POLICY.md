# Volume V example/exercise expansion policy

Volume V additions are algebra- and construction-driven. Each enriched chapter receives three
concept-placed worked examples and sixteen graded exercise/hint/solution triads. Existing
theorems, proofs, examples, dossiers, exercises, hints, solutions, labels, outcomes, and
summaries are protected.

The sixteen added exercises are grouped as five standard computations, four proofs, three
counterexamples or hypothesis tests, two applications or investigations, and two challenges.

All additions are bracketed by VOL05-EXPANSION markers. Stripping those blocks must reproduce
the Commit-1 protected source exactly by SHA-256.

From Commit 2 onward, generated prose is scanned for unsafe raw TeX special characters and the
actual generated blocks must pass an isolated pdflatex probe before any live chapter is modified.
