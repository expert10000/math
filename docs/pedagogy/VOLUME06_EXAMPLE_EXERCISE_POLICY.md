# Volume VI example/exercise expansion policy

Volume VI additions are geometry- and construction-driven. Each enriched chapter receives three
substantial concept-placed worked examples and sixteen graded exercise/hint/solution triads.
Existing theorems, proofs, examples, legacy dossiers, exercises, hints, solutions, figures,
labels, and chapter summaries are protected.

The sixteen added exercises are grouped as five standard computations, four proofs, three
counterexamples or hypothesis tests, two applications or investigations, and two challenges.

All additions are bracketed by VOL06-EXPANSION markers. Stripping those blocks must reproduce
the Commit-1 protected source exactly by SHA-256.

From Commit 2 onward, generated prose is scanned for unsafe raw TeX special characters and the
actual generated blocks must pass an isolated pdflatex probe before any live chapter is modified.
