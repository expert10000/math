# Volume II remaining-arc pedagogical expansion

- II/13–II/15: uniform series control, Fourier/trigonometric structure, and
  scale-dependent pathology;
- II/16–II/19: contraction estimates, topological fixed points, integral
  equations, Picard iteration, Gronwall stability, and ODE continuation;
- II/20–II/25: interpolation/remainders, Bernstein approximation,
  Chebyshev/minimax structure, alternation certificates, quadrature error, and
  continued-fraction approximation.

Each chapter receives 16 new triads grouped as:

- 5 Standard computations
- 4 Proofs
- 3 Counterexamples and hypothesis testing
- 2 Applications and investigations
- 2 Challenge problems

All previous examples, dossiers, exercises, hints, solutions, theorem text,
proofs, labels, outcomes, and summaries remain protected.
