# Volume I late-arc example and exercise expansion

The I/13–I/18 enrichment preserves all prior content and adds mathematical
working depth at the point where the method is first needed.

The late arc emphasizes:

- I/13: weighted geometry, Cauchy–Schwarz, orthogonal complements, polarization;
- I/14: Gram–Schmidt, projection matrices, QR, least squares and residual checks;
- I/15: orthogonal/unitary tests, Householder reflections, planar rotation/reflection geometry;
- I/16: self-adjoint/normal tests, orthonormal diagonalization, spectral projectors and functional calculus;
- I/17: matrix extraction, congruence, definiteness, inertia and principal axes;
- I/18: full SVD construction, pseudoinverse, least squares, conditioning and low-rank approximation.

The supplementary exercise layer per chapter contains:

- 5 standard computations;
- 4 proofs;
- 3 counterexample / hypothesis-testing tasks;
- 2 applications / investigations;
- 2 challenge problems.

The original eight exercises remain intact and continue to provide short
definition checks and foundational calculations.
