\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and Further Reading}

This guide records primary or authoritative sources relevant to \emph{Volume II: Real Analysis and Topological Foundations}.
It is a reading and attribution guide; the volume's own hypotheses and conventions remain authoritative.

\section*{Chapter guide}
\begin{description}
\item[II/01--II/06.] Completeness, Euclidean/normed structures, metrics, and topological foundations.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Dedekind; Cantor; Rudin.

\item[II/07--II/12.] Sequences, continuity, differentiation, compactness, and convergence.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Cauchy; Weierstrass; Rudin.

\item[II/13--II/18.] Integration and the transition from Riemann to measure-theoretic viewpoints.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Riemann; Lebesgue; Folland.

\item[II/19--II/25.] Function spaces, convergence principles, approximation, and later real-analysis structures.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Baire; Banach; Rudin.

\end{description}

\section*{Selected bibliographic notes}
\begin{description}
\item[Rudin.] Modern rigorous real analysis.

\item[Lebesgue (1902); Folland.] Primary and modern references for measure and Lebesgue integration.

\end{description}

\section*{Reference policy}
Historical attribution and modern theorem verification serve different roles.
Where precise hypotheses matter, use an authoritative modern source in addition to historical attribution.
The maintained series bibliography is stored at \texttt{references/series\_primary\_sources.bib}.
