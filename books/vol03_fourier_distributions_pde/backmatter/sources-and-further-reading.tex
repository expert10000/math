\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and Further Reading}

This guide records primary or authoritative sources relevant to \emph{Volume III: Measure, Fourier Analysis, Distributions and PDE}.
It is a reading and attribution guide; the volume's own hypotheses and conventions remain authoritative.

\section*{Chapter guide}
\begin{description}
\item[III/01--III/08.] Measure, measurable functions, integration, and convergence theorems.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Lebesgue; Radon; Folland.

\item[III/09--III/17.] Fourier series/transforms, orthogonality, spectral viewpoints, and harmonic analysis.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Fourier; Plancherel; Stein--Shakarchi.

\item[III/18--III/23.] Distributions, weak derivatives, convolution, and Fourier analysis of generalized functions.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Schwartz; Hörmander.

\item[III/24--III/28.] Weak formulations, fundamental PDE examples, and analytic estimates.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Evans; Hörmander.

\end{description}

\section*{Selected bibliographic notes}
\begin{description}
\item[Schwartz; Hörmander.] Distributions and generalized-function analysis.

\item[Evans.] Modern PDE reference.

\end{description}

\section*{Reference policy}
Historical attribution and modern theorem verification serve different roles.
Where precise hypotheses matter, use an authoritative modern source in addition to historical attribution.
The maintained series bibliography is stored at \texttt{references/series\_primary\_sources.bib}.
