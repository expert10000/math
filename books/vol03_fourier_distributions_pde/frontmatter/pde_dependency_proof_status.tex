\chapter*{PDE Dependency and Proof-Status Map}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{PDE Dependency and Proof-Status Map}
\label{front:vol03-pde-dependency-map}

The final part of Volume III uses several advanced results whose roles differ:
some are proved in the volume, some are proved only in model cases, and some
are standard inputs with full proofs deferred. The dependency chain is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{test functions and distributions}
\\ \downarrow \\
\text{weak derivatives}
\\ \downarrow \\
W^{1,p},\ H^1,\ H_0^1
\\ \downarrow \\
\text{density + trace + Poincare}
\\ \downarrow \\
\text{Lax--Milgram / weak BVP}
\\ \downarrow \\
\text{fundamental and Green solutions}
\\ \downarrow \\
\text{elliptic comparison / maximum principles}
\\ \downarrow \\
\text{spectral and transform PDE methods}.
\end{array}}
\]

\paragraph{Proof-status convention.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{proved}: a proof sufficient for the stated theorem appears here;
\item \textbf{model-case proved}: the mechanism is proved in the principal
setting used later, while broader variants are not claimed;
\item \textbf{proof sketch}: the main argument is shown but a substantial
standard theorem is imported;
\item \textbf{deferred}: the theorem is used with its full hypotheses stated,
but its full proof lies outside this volume.
\end{itemize}
