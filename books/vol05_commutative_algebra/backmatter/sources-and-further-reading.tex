\phantomsection
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and Further Reading}

This guide records primary or authoritative sources relevant to \emph{Volume V: Commutative Algebra and Homological Methods}.
It is a reading and attribution guide; the volume's own hypotheses and conventions remain authoritative.

\section*{Chapter guide}
\begin{description}
\item[V/01--V/14.] Ideals, localization, modules, Noetherian methods, dimension, and structural commutative algebra.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Noether; Atiyah--Macdonald; Matsumura.

\item[V/15--V/21.] Finiteness, primary methods, dimension, and local algebra.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Hilbert; Artin--Rees; Matsumura.

\item[V/22--V/28.] Chain complexes, exactness, derived functors, Ext, Tor, and foundations of homological algebra.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Cartan--Eilenberg; Grothendieck 1957; Mac Lane.

\end{description}

\section*{Selected bibliographic notes}
\begin{description}
\item[Atiyah--Macdonald; Matsumura.] Commutative algebra.

\item[Cartan--Eilenberg (1956).] Foundational systematic homological algebra.

\item[Grothendieck (1957).] Derived-functor/abelian-category foundations; DOI \texttt{10.2748/tmj/1178244839}.

\end{description}

\section*{Reference policy}
Historical attribution and modern theorem verification serve different roles.
Where precise hypotheses matter, use an authoritative modern source in addition to historical attribution.
The maintained series bibliography is stored at \texttt{references/series\_primary\_sources.bib}.
