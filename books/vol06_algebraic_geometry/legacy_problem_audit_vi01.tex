\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Legacy Problem Recovery Audit for VI/01}

The canonical migration map identifies
\texttt{theory-of-differential-geometry-4.tex} as the primary legacy family for the
classical affine-algebraic-set material.  Its first affine problem block contains thirteen
substantive problems.  A problem-level audit shows that none has VI/01 as its best primary
home: the problems already assume topology, irreducibility, coordinate rings, morphisms,
families, or tangent-space ideas.  They are therefore retained in the later chapter where
the required machinery actually belongs.

\textbf{Result of the audit.}
No substantive legacy problem is deleted.  VI/01 therefore uses newly designed Problems
VI.01.P1--P8 and Challenges VI.01.C1--C3 to teach the foundational algebraic-set material
at the correct level, while every legacy problem remains assigned to its stronger canonical
home.  Duplicate legacy bodies are not copied merely to increase the local problem count.
