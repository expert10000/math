% Problem dossier: VI.05.P7
\subsection*{Problem VI.05.P7 --- Disjoint algebraic pieces and the Chinese remainder theorem}
\label{prob:vi05-p7}
\paragraph{Problem.}
Assume \(k\) is algebraically closed.  Let \(Y,Z\subseteq\mathbb A_k^n\) be nonempty
algebraic sets.  Prove that
\[
Y\cap Z=\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
I(Y)+I(Z)=(1).
\]
If these conditions hold, prove
\[
k[Y\cup Z]\cong k[Y]\times k[Z],
\]
and construct the idempotent that is \(1\) on \(Y\) and \(0\) on \(Z\).
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the general form of the ``two parallel lines'' calculation.  It explains exactly
why a disjoint algebraic decomposition becomes a direct product of function rings.
\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use \(V(I(Y)+I(Z))=Y\cap Z\), the Nullstellensatz, and the Chinese remainder theorem.
\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\textbf{Hint 1.} Empty zero set means the radical of \(I(Y)+I(Z)\) is \((1)\).
\textbf{Hint 2.} For radical ideals of algebraic sets, \(I(Y\cup Z)=I(Y)\cap I(Z)\).
\textbf{Hint 3.} Choose \(f\in I(Y)\), \(g\in I(Z)\) with \(f+g=1\).
\paragraph{Solution.}
We have
\[
V(I(Y)+I(Z))=V(I(Y))\cap V(I(Z))=Y\cap Z.
\]
If the intersection is empty, the Nullstellensatz gives
\[
\sqrt{I(Y)+I(Z)}=(1),
\]
which forces \(I(Y)+I(Z)=(1)\).  The converse is immediate after applying \(V\).
Now
\[
I(Y\cup Z)=I(Y)\cap I(Z).
\]
Since the ideals are comaximal, CRT gives
\[
k[Y\cup Z]
=\frac{k[x_1,\dots,x_n]}{I(Y)\cap I(Z)}
\cong
\frac{k[x_1,\dots,x_n]}{I(Y)}\times
\frac{k[x_1,\dots,x_n]}{I(Z)}
=k[Y]\times k[Z].
\]
Choose \(f\in I(Y)\), \(g\in I(Z)\) with \(f+g=1\).  The class \(e=[g]\) is \(1\) on
\(Y\) and \(0\) on \(Z\), so \(e^2=e\) in \(k[Y\cup Z]\).
\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Disjointness becomes comaximality, and comaximality becomes a product ring.  The idempotent
is the algebraic characteristic function of one clopen component.
\paragraph{Extensions.}
Generalize to finitely many pairwise disjoint algebraic subsets and construct a complete
set of pairwise orthogonal idempotents summing to \(1\).
\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical problem synthesizing the connectedness/idempotent legacy material with the
Chinese remainder theorem.
