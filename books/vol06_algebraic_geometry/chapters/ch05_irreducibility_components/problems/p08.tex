% Problem dossier: VI.05.P8
\subsection*{Problem VI.05.P8 --- Squarefree plane curves and their component graph}
\label{prob:vi05-p8}
\paragraph{Problem.}
Assume \(k\) is algebraically closed and let
\[
f=f_1\cdots f_r\in k[x,y]
\]
where the \(f_i\) are pairwise nonassociate irreducible polynomials.  Prove that the
irreducible components of \(V(f)\) are exactly \(V(f_i)\).  Show that \(V(f)\) is connected
if and only if the graph joining \(i\) and \(j\) whenever \(V(f_i,f_j)\neq\varnothing\) is
connected.  Apply this to
\[
f=xy(x-1)(y-1).
\]
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Factorization is often the fastest route to the irreducible decomposition of a plane
hypersurface.  The second part then turns connectedness into finite combinatorics.
\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
For an irreducible polynomial \(f_i\), the ideal \((f_i)\) is prime in \(k[x,y]\).  Use
\(V(fg)=V(f)\cup V(g)\) and the component-graph criterion.
\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\textbf{Hint 1.} The factorization immediately gives a union of the \(V(f_i)\).
\textbf{Hint 2.} None of these irreducible curves contains another, because containment
would reverse to divisibility of irreducible defining equations.
\textbf{Hint 3.} For the application there are two vertical and two horizontal lines.
\paragraph{Solution.}
Since \(f=\prod_i f_i\),
\[
V(f)=\bigcup_{i=1}^r V(f_i).
\]
Each \((f_i)\) is prime, so each \(V(f_i)\) is irreducible.  If
\(V(f_i)\subseteq V(f_j)\), the Nullstellensatz gives
\((f_j)\subseteq(f_i)\), hence \(f_i\) divides \(f_j\); by irreducibility and the
nonassociate assumption this forces \(i=j\).  Thus the displayed sets are exactly the
irreducible components.  The connectedness statement is precisely
\cref{thm:vi05-intersection-graph}.
For
\[
xy(x-1)(y-1)=0,
\]
the components are \(x=0\), \(x=1\), \(y=0\), \(y=1\).  The two vertical lines do not
meet each other, nor do the two horizontal lines, while every vertical line meets every
horizontal line.  The component graph is \(K_{2,2}\), hence connected.  Therefore the
curve is reducible with four components but connected.
\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Squarefree factorization determines the irreducible branches; intersection data determines
which branches glue into connected pieces.  These are separate layers of structure.
\paragraph{Extensions.}
Study arrangements of affine lines and conics.  For a line arrangement, determine how
parallelism changes the component graph and therefore connectedness.
\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis problem based on the factorization and component themes already
present in the legacy affine-geometry corpus.
