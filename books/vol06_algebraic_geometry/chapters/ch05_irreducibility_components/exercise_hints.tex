% VI/05 exercise hints.  Problems carry their own hints inside their dossier files.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.1}
Compute the three coordinate rings and ask whether each is a domain.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.2}
Every nonempty open subset of an irreducible space is dense.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.3}
The function \(f-g\) vanishes on a dense subset.  Its zero set is closed.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.4}
The affine line is irreducible.  Use continuity of \(\phi\), then take the closure of its
image.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.5}
Factor the defining polynomial into four distinct linear factors.  All four lines meet at
the origin.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.6}
The equation \(xy=0\) is equivalent to \(x=0\) or \(y=0\).

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.7}
For idempotents, use the fact that the two irreducible components meet at the origin, or
evaluate an idempotent on each component and compare at the intersection.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.8}
The ideals \((x)\) and \((x-1)\) are comaximal in \(k[x,y]\).

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.9}
A union of two connected subsets with nonempty intersection is connected; iterate this
observation.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.10}
If \(f(X)=A\cup B\) with \(A,B\) closed in the image, pull the decomposition back to \(X\).

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.11}
Use the open-set criterion for irreducibility.

\subsection*{Hint to Exercise VI.05.12}
Use:
\[
\text{irreducible}\Longleftrightarrow k[X]\text{ is a domain},
\]
and, for connectedness over an algebraically closed field, use nontrivial idempotents.
