\begin{problem}[Field-valued points as a disjoint union]\label{chal:vi21-c01}
Prove a natural set-level decomposition
\[
X(K)\cong\coprod_{x\in X}\Hom_{\mathrm{Fields}}(\kappa(x),K),
\]
where only field homomorphisms compatible with any specified base structure are retained in the relative case.  Explain exactly where locality of stalk maps enters.
\end{problem}
