\subsection*{VI.21.P15: Factorization through characteristic $p$}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi21-p15}
Prove that $\pi_X$ factors through
$\Spec\mathbb F_p\to\Spec\mathbb Z$ iff $p\cdot1=0$ in $\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$.
\par\textbf{Solution.} By the universal property, a map $X\to\Spec\mathbb F_p$ is equivalent to a
ring map $\mathbb F_p\to\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$.  Such a unital map exists exactly when $p1=0$, and then
its composite with $\mathbb Z\to\mathbb F_p$ is the unique absolute structure map.
\end{problem}
