\subsection*{VI.21.P19: Naturality of the structure morphism}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi21-p19}
For every scheme morphism $f:X\to Y$, prove that
\[
\pi_X=\pi_Y\circ f,
\]
and interpret this as a commutative square over the absolute base.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Naturality turns the structure morphisms into a coherent global base structure on the whole category of schemes.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Translate point statements through local rings and residue fields; when the target is affine, use the VI/19 universal property and then verify the pointwise interpretation.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Identify the relevant canonical ring or stalk homomorphism.
\item Track maximal ideals or prime contractions to identify the image point.
\item Use locality to pass to residue fields when needed.
\item Check independence, uniqueness, or functoriality explicitly rather than only on points.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The morphism $\pi_X$ is, by definition, the unique morphism from $X$ to the final object
$\Spec\mathbb Z$.  The composite
\[
X\xrightarrow{f}Y\xrightarrow{\pi_Y}\Spec\mathbb Z
\]
is another morphism with the same source and target.  Finality therefore forces
\[
\pi_X=\pi_Y\circ f.
\]
Equivalently, every scheme morphism fits into the commutative diagram
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
X&\xrightarrow{f}&Y\\
\downarrow\pi_X&&\downarrow\pi_Y\\
\Spec\mathbb Z&=&\Spec\mathbb Z.
\end{array}
\]
Thus every scheme morphism is automatically a morphism over the absolute base.  On affine charts
this is simply the fact that a unital ring homomorphism preserves the canonical image of
$\mathbb Z$.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Naturality turns the structure morphisms into a coherent global base structure on the whole category of schemes.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use it to show that residue characteristic can only move along specialization in $\Spec\mathbb Z$.
