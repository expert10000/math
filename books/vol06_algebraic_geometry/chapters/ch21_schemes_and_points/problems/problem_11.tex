\subsection*{VI.21.P11: Closed does not mean rational}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi21-p11}
Give a closed point of $\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb R}$ that is not $\mathbb R$-rational.
\par\textbf{Solution.} The maximal ideal $(t^2+1)$ is closed, but its residue field is
$\mathbb R[t]/(t^2+1)\cong\mathbb C$.  An $\mathbb R$-rational point would have residue field
admitting the specified identity-compatible map to $\mathbb R$; this one does not.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Provenance.} Canonical VI/21 derivative/application; not an additional legacy migration.
