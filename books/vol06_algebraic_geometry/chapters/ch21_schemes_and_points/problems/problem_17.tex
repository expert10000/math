\subsection*{VI.21.P17: Topological points do not determine a scheme}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi21-p17}
Compare $X=\Spec k$ and $Y=\Spec k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$.  Show their point spaces are homeomorphic but the schemes are not isomorphic.
\par\textbf{Solution.} Both rings have exactly one prime ideal, so both spectra are one-point spaces.
But their global section rings are not isomorphic: the second contains a nonzero nilpotent.  An
isomorphism of affine schemes would induce an isomorphism of rings, impossible.
\end{problem}
