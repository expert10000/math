\subsection*{VI.21.P18: Why local stalk maps are required}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi21-p18}
Explain why the local-homomorphism condition is essential in the definition of a scheme morphism.
\par\textbf{Solution.} Without locality, a stalk map need not carry the closed point of the source
local scheme to the prescribed image point and need not induce a residue-field map.  Locality makes
$f_x^{\#-1}(\mathfrak m_x)=\mathfrak m_{f(x)}$, which is exactly the compatibility tying the
continuous map of points to the sheaf map.
\end{problem}
