\begin{problem}[Equal principal opens and equal localizations]\label{prob:vi18-p06}
Suppose $\sqrt{(f)}=\sqrt{(g)}$.  Prove that $D(f)=D(g)$ and that there is a canonical
$A$-algebra isomorphism $A_f\cong A_g$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Equal radicals imply $D(f)=D(g)$.  They also imply $f^m\in(g)$ and $g^n\in(f)$ for some
$m,n$.  Hence $g$ becomes a unit in $A_f$ and $f$ becomes a unit in $A_g$.  The universal
property of localization gives $A$-algebra maps in both directions, and uniqueness shows
that their composites are identities.
\end{solution}
