\begin{problem}[Topology of the basic-open identification]\label{prob:vi18-p03}
Prove the homeomorphism $\Spec A_f\cong D(f)$ using only the prime correspondence and bases
of distinguished opens.  Show also that replacing $f$ by $f^n$ does not change the
identification.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Extension--contraction gives the point bijection.  The equality
$D(g/1)\leftrightarrow D(fg)$ identifies topological bases, hence gives a homeomorphism.
Since $D(f)=D(f^n)$ and $A_f\cong A_{f^n}$ canonically, the constructions agree.
\end{solution}
