\begin{problem}[Affine cover does not imply affine]\label{prob:vi18-p20}
Explain precisely why the standard two-affine cover of $\mathbb P^1$ does not contradict its non-affineness, and identify which global invariant fails to match a single spectrum.
\end{problem}

This problem consolidates the local versus global aspect of affine geometry and turns a structural statement into a reusable calculation.

Use the distinction between locally affine and globally affine, and the global-section calculation.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}

\item Reduce the statement to a ring-theoretic property whenever possible.

\begin{solution}
The statement that a scheme has an affine open cover is local: it says each point lies in some open subset isomorphic to a spectrum. Affineness itself is global: it asks for the entire scheme to be isomorphic to one spectrum. There is no logical reason for the first condition alone to imply the second.

For $\mathbb P^1_k$, the standard cover
\[
\mathbb P^1_k=U_0\cup U_1,
\qquad
U_0\cong U_1\cong\mathbb A^1_k
\]
shows that it is locally affine. On the overlap the coordinates satisfy $u=t^{-1}$. A global regular function is a pair $f(t)\in k[t]$ and $g(u)\in k[u]$ satisfying $f(t)=g(t^{-1})$ in $k[t,t^{-1}]$. Therefore
\[
\Gamma(\mathbb P^1_k,\mathcal O)=k.
\]

If $\mathbb P^1_k$ were affine, affine reconstruction would force
\[
\mathbb P^1_k\cong\Spec\Gamma(\mathbb P^1_k,\mathcal O)=\Spec k.
\]
But $\Spec k$ has one point, whereas $\mathbb P^1_k$ has many points (in particular the two distinct points $[1:0]$ and $[0:1]$). The global invariant that fails is therefore the global section ring: it is too small to reconstruct the projective line as one affine spectrum. The affine cover is perfectly compatible with non-affineness because the obstruction appears only after trying to glue the local charts into a single global coordinate ring.
\end{solution}


\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The calculation illustrates that global affine geometry is controlled by commutative algebra once the structure sheaf is kept in view.
