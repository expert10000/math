\begin{problem}[Affine schemes are quasi-compact]\label{prob:vi18-p05}
Give a proof of quasi-compactness of $\Spec A$ beginning with an arbitrary open cover and
ending with an explicit finite distinguished-open refinement.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Refine the cover by distinguished opens $D(f_j)$.  Since these cover, the $f_j$ generate
the unit ideal; otherwise a maximal ideal containing all of them is uncovered.  Write
$1=\sum_{i=1}^r a_i f_{j_i}$.  Then $D(f_{j_1}),\dots,D(f_{j_r})$ cover $\Spec A$, and
choosing their parent members of the original cover gives a finite subcover.
\end{solution}
