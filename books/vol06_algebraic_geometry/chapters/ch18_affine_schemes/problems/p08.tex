\begin{problem}[Nilpotent thickening is not detected by topology]\label{prob:vi18-p08}
Let $A=k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$.  Show that $\Spec A$ and $\Spec k$ are homeomorphic
but not isomorphic as affine schemes.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Both spectra have one point.  But
\[
\Gamma(\Spec A,\OO)=A
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\Gamma(\Spec k,\OO)=k,
\]
and these rings are not isomorphic because the first has a nonzero nilpotent.  An isomorphism
of affine schemes would induce an isomorphism on global sections, contradiction.
\end{solution}
