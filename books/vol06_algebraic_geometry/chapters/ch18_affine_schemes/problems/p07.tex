\begin{problem}[A clopen decomposition from a product ring]\label{prob:vi18-p07}
Show that
\[
\Spec(A\times B)\cong\Spec A\sqcup\Spec B
\]
as affine schemes, identifying the two pieces as distinguished opens cut out by the
idempotents $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Every prime of $A\times B$ contains exactly one of the complementary idempotents, so it is
of the form $\mfp\times B$ or $A\times\mfq$.  The corresponding subsets are clopen.
Localizing at $(1,0)$ kills the $B$ factor and gives $A$, while localizing at $(0,1)$ gives
$B$.  The basic-open affine theorem therefore identifies the two pieces with $\Spec A$ and
$\Spec B$ including their structure sheaves.
\end{solution}
