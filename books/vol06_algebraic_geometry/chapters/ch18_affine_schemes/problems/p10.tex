\begin{problem}[One-point affine schemes]\label{prob:vi18-p10}
Characterize rings whose spectra have exactly one point and give three nonisomorphic examples with the same one-point topology.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This problem consolidates the one-point spectra aspect of affine geometry and turns a structural statement into a reusable calculation.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
A unique prime must be both the nilradical and the unique maximal ideal.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A unique prime must be both the nilradical and the unique maximal ideal.
\item Reduce the statement to a ring-theoretic property whenever possible.
\item Translate the result back into topology, sheaves, or local rings so that all affine-scheme layers are visible.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The spectrum has exactly one point iff $A$ has exactly one prime ideal. Suppose the unique prime is $\mathfrak p$. Since the nilradical is the intersection of all prime ideals,
\[
\sqrt{(0)}=\mathfrak p.
\]
Every maximal ideal is prime, so $\mathfrak p$ is also the unique maximal ideal. Conversely, if the nilradical $\sqrt{(0)}$ is maximal, every prime contains it, and maximality forces every prime to equal it. Thus
\[
\Spec A\text{ has one point}\iff\sqrt{(0)}\text{ is a maximal ideal}.
\]

For three nonisomorphic examples over a field $k$, take
\[
k,
\qquad
k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2),
\qquad
k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^3).
\]
Each has a unique prime ideal: respectively $(0)$, $(\varepsilon)$, and $(\varepsilon)$. Their spectra are therefore homeomorphic one-point spaces. The rings are pairwise nonisomorphic: the first is reduced, the second has nonzero nilpotents all killed by the second power of $(\varepsilon)$, while in the third the class of $\varepsilon$ has nilpotence index $3$. Since global sections recover the coordinate ring, the corresponding affine schemes are pairwise nonisomorphic.
\end{solution}


\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The calculation illustrates that global affine geometry is controlled by commutative algebra once the structure sheaf is kept in view.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Vary the ring by products, quotients, localizations, arithmetic examples, or nilpotent thickenings and repeat the same dictionary.
