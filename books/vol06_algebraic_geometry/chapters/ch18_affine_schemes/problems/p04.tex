\begin{problem}[Stalks under a principal-open chart]\label{prob:vi18-p04}
For $\mfp\in D(f)$ prove explicitly that
\[
(A_f)_{\mfp A_f}\cong A_{\mfp}
\]
and that the isomorphism identifies maximal ideals and residue fields.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Both localizations invert exactly the images of elements of $A\setminus\mfp$: localizing
first at $f$ adds nothing because $f\notin\mfp$.  The universal properties give inverse
maps.  The unique maximal ideals both identify with $\mfp A_{\mfp}$, and quotienting gives
the same residue field $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mfp)$.
\end{solution}
