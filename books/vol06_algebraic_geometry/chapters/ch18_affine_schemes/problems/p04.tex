\begin{problem}[Principal opens give an affine basis]\label{prob:vi18-p04}
Using the principal-open basis theorem from VI/08 together with VI.18.P1, prove that every affine scheme has a basis of affine open subschemes of the form
\[
D(f)\cong\Spec A_f.
\]
\end{problem}

\begin{solution}
By the principal-open basis theorem proved in VI/08, the distinguished opens $D(f)$ form a basis for the Zariski topology on $\Spec A$.  VI.18.P1 now upgrades each member of that already-established topological basis to an affine open subscheme:
\[
(D(f),\OO_X|_{D(f)})\cong\Spec A_f.
\]
Hence the Zariski topology has a basis consisting entirely of affine open subschemes.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The calculation illustrates that global affine geometry is controlled by commutative algebra once the structure sheaf is kept in view.
