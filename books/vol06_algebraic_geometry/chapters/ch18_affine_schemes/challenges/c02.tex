\begin{problem}[Challenge: A finite basic union need not be principal]\label{chal:vi18-c02}
In $\Spec k[x,y]$, let
\[
U=D(x)\cup D(y)=\mathbb A^2_k\setminus V(x,y).
\]
Prove that there is no $f\in k[x,y]$ with $U=D(f)$.  Interpret the obstruction in terms of
the radical ideal $(x,y)$.
\end{problem}
