\begin{problem}[Challenge: Multiplicative-set generalization]\label{chal:vi18-c01}
Let $S\subseteq A$ be multiplicatively closed.  Identify $\Spec S^{-1}A$ with the subspace
of $\Spec A$ consisting of primes disjoint from $S$, and prove the corresponding statement
for the restricted structure sheaf.
\end{problem}
