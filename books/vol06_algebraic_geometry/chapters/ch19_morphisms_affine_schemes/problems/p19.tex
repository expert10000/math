\begin{problem}[Recover the point map from global pullback]\label{prob:vi19-p19}
Let $f:X\to\Spec A$ be a morphism of locally ringed spaces and let $\alpha:A\to\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$ be its global pullback. Prove
\[
f(x)=\{a\in A:\alpha(a)_x\in\mfm_x\}.
\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the uniqueness heart of the AG3 Problem 12 universal property.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use locality of the stalk homomorphism $A_{f(x)}\to\OO_{X,x}$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item An element $a$ belongs to $f(x)$ exactly when $a/1$ lies in the maximal ideal of $A_{f(x)}$.
\item Pull this condition across the local stalk map.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The stalk map
\[
f_x^\sharp:A_{f(x)}\longrightarrow\OO_{X,x}
\]
is local.  Therefore the inverse image of $\mfm_x$ is the maximal ideal $f(x)A_{f(x)}$.  For $a\in A$, the germ $\alpha(a)_x$ is the image of $a/1$.  Hence
\[
\alpha(a)_x\in\mfm_x
\iff a/1\in f(x)A_{f(x)}
\iff a\in f(x).
\]
This proves the formula.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The local condition on stalks forces the continuous point map once the pullback of global functions is known.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use the formula to reprove uniqueness in VI.19.P1.
