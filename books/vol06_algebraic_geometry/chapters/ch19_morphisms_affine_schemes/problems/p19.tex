\begin{problem}[Recover the point map from global pullback]\label{prob:vi19-p19}
Let $f:X\to\Spec A$ be a morphism of locally ringed spaces and let $\alpha:A\to\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$ be its global pullback. Prove
\[
f(x)=\{a\in A:\alpha(a)_x\in\mfm_x\}.
\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the uniqueness heart of the AG3 Problem 12 universal property.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use locality of the stalk homomorphism $A_{f(x)}\to\OO_{X,x}$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item An element $a$ belongs to $f(x)$ exactly when $a/1$ lies in the maximal ideal of $A_{f(x)}$.
\item Pull this condition across the local stalk map.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
% VI19 full-solutions refinement v1
Let \(f:X\to\Spec A\) be a morphism of locally ringed spaces and let
\[
\alpha:A=\Gamma(\Spec A,\OO)\to\Gamma(X,\OO_X)
\]
be the induced map on global sections.  Fix \(x\in X\).  The stalk homomorphism
\[
f_x^\sharp:A_{f(x)}\longrightarrow\OO_{X,x}
\]
is local.  Therefore
\[
(f_x^\sharp)^{-1}(\mathfrak m_x)=f(x)A_{f(x)},
\]
the maximal ideal of the source local ring.

For \(a\in A\), the germ \(\alpha(a)_x\) is exactly the image under \(f_x^\sharp\) of the fraction \(a/1\in A_{f(x)}\).  Hence
\[
\begin{aligned}
\alpha(a)_x\in\mathfrak m_x
&\iff a/1\in f(x)A_{f(x)}\\
&\iff a\in f(x).
\end{aligned}
\]
The last equivalence is the usual membership criterion in the local ring \(A_{f(x)}\): a numerator \(a\) lies in its maximal ideal precisely when \(a\in f(x)\).  Therefore
\[
\boxed{f(x)=\{a\in A:\alpha(a)_x\in\mathfrak m_x\}.}
\]
Thus the global pullback determines the point map.  Together with localization on principal opens, this is the uniqueness mechanism in the universal property VI.19.P1.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The local condition on stalks forces the continuous point map once the pullback of global functions is known.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use the formula to reprove uniqueness in VI.19.P1.
