\begin{problem}[Factorization through a distinguished open]\label{prob:vi19-p16}
Let $\alpha:A\to B$ and $f\in A$. Prove the morphism $\Spec B\to\Spec A$ factors through $D(f)=\Spec A_f$ if and only if $\alpha(f)$ is a unit in $B$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the geometric form of the localization universal property.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Translate the factorization to a ring map out of $A_f$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Use VI.18.P1 to identify $D(f)$ with $\Spec A_f$.
\item Apply the universal property of localization.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
By VI.18.P1, $D(f)=\Spec A_f$.  A factorization
\[
\Spec B\to\Spec A_f\to\Spec A
\]
corresponds to a ring factorization
\[
A\to A_f\to B.
\]
The localization universal property says this exists uniquely exactly when the image $\alpha(f)$ is invertible in $B$.  Hence the geometric factorization criterion is precisely invertibility of the pulled-back function.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
A map lands in the nonvanishing locus of $f$ exactly when the pullback of $f$ is everywhere invertible.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Prove the analogous statement for a general locally ringed space using VI.19.P1.
