\begin{problem}[Polynomial maps and substitution]\label{prob:vi19-p06}
Given polynomials $p_1,\dots,p_m\in k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$, construct the affine morphism $\A^n_k\to\A^m_k$ and prove that composition corresponds to substitution of polynomials.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Polynomial coordinate formulas are the classical face of the affine Hom correspondence.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Send the target coordinate $y_i$ to $p_i$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Build $k[y_1,\dots,y_m]\to k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$.
\item Apply $\Spec$.
\item Compose two such ring maps and inspect the images of coordinates.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
% VI19 full-solutions refinement v1
Let the source coordinates be \(x_1,\ldots,x_n\) and the target coordinates be \(y_1,\ldots,y_m\).  Given \(p_i\in k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\), the polynomial-ring universal property gives a unique \(k\)-algebra homomorphism
\[
\varphi:k[y_1,\ldots,y_m]\to k[x_1,\ldots,x_n],
\qquad y_i\longmapsto p_i.
\]
Applying \(\Spec\) gives
\[
f=\Spec(\varphi):\mathbb A_k^n\to\mathbb A_k^m.
\]
If \(k\) is algebraically closed and \(a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\) is a closed point, contraction of the maximal ideal \((x_1-a_1,\ldots,x_n-a_n)\) is
\[
(y_1-p_1(a),\ldots,y_m-p_m(a)),
\]
so \(f(a)=(p_1(a),\ldots,p_m(a))\).

Suppose a second polynomial map \(\mathbb A_k^m\to\mathbb A_k^r\) is defined by \(q_j(y_1,\ldots,y_m)\).  Its coordinate-ring map sends \(z_j\mapsto q_j(y)\).  The composite ring map therefore sends
\[
z_j\longmapsto q_j(p_1(x),\ldots,p_m(x)).
\]
By contravariant functoriality this ring composite corresponds to the composite geometric morphism.  Thus composition of affine polynomial maps is exactly substitution of the coordinate polynomials.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Contravariance turns coordinate pullback into the usual forward polynomial map on points.
