\begin{problem}[Maps to the affine line are global functions]\label{prob:vi19-p10}
For a locally ringed space $X$, prove $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{LRS}}(X,\Spec\mathbb Z[t])\cong\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$ and identify explicitly the function corresponding to a morphism.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This gives a geometric interpretation of a global regular function.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Ring maps from $\mathbb Z[t]$ are determined by the image of $t$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Apply VI.19.P1 with $A=\mathbb Z[t]$.
\item Use the universal property of the polynomial ring.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
% VI19 full-solutions refinement v1
Apply the universal property VI.19.P1 with \(A=\mathbb Z[t]\).  It yields a natural bijection
\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{LRS}}(X,\Spec\mathbb Z[t])
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{Ring}}(\mathbb Z[t],\Gamma(X,\OO_X)).
\]
A unital ring homomorphism out of \(\mathbb Z[t]\) is uniquely determined by the image of \(t\), because the integers have a fixed image and every polynomial is obtained from \(t\) by ring operations.  Conversely each section \(s\in\Gamma(X,\OO_X)\) defines the evaluation homomorphism \(p(t)\mapsto p(s)\).  Hence
\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathrm{LRS}}(X,\Spec\mathbb Z[t])
\cong\Gamma(X,\OO_X).
\]

If \(f:X\to\Spec\mathbb Z[t]\), the corresponding section is exactly
\[
s=f^\sharp_{\Spec\mathbb Z[t]}(t),
\]
the pullback of the universal coordinate \(t\).  Conversely, the morphism associated to \(s\) is constructed pointwise by
\[
x\longmapsto\{p(t):p(s)_x\in\mathfrak m_x\},
\]
and on a principal open \(D(p)\) its sheaf map is localization with \(p(t)\) sent to \(p(s)\).  Thus a global regular function is literally a morphism to the universal affine line.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
A regular function is literally a morphism to the universal affine line.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Generalize to tuples and affine $n$-space.
