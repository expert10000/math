\begin{problem}[Projection $\A^2\to\A^1$]\label{prob:vi19-p05}
Let $k[t]\to k[x,y]$ send $t$ to $x$. Describe the induced map of spectra, its action on closed $k$-points, the inverse image of $D(t)$, and the map on the stalk at $(x-a,y-b)$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This connects the abstract spectrum construction to a familiar coordinate projection.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use contraction and the principal-open formula.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Contract $(x-a,y-b)$.
\item Pull back $D(t)$.
\item Localize at the corresponding primes.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
% VI19 full-solutions refinement v1
Let \(\varphi:k[t]\to k[x,y]\) send \(t\mapsto x\), and put \(f=\Spec(\varphi)\).  For a prime \(\mathfrak p\subset k[x,y]\),
\[
f(\mathfrak p)=\{q(t)\in k[t]:q(x)\in\mathfrak p\}.
\]
If \(\mathfrak p=(x-a,y-b)\) is a closed \(k\)-point, then \(q(x)\in\mathfrak p\) exactly when \(q(a)=0\).  Thus
\[
f(x-a,y-b)=(t-a),
\]
so on closed \(k\)-points the morphism is the classical projection \((a,b)\mapsto a\).

For the distinguished open \(D(t)\subseteq\Spec k[t]\),
\[
f^{-1}(D(t))=D(\varphi(t))=D(x).
\]
The pullback on sections there is
\[
k[t,t^{-1}]\to k[x,y,x^{-1}],
\qquad t\longmapsto x.
\]

At \(\mathfrak p=(x-a,y-b)\), its image is \(\mathfrak q=(t-a)\).  The induced local homomorphism is
\[
k[t]_{(t-a)}\longrightarrow k[x,y]_{(x-a,y-b)},
\qquad
\frac{r(t)}{s(t)}\longmapsto\frac{r(x)}{s(x)}.
\]
Because \(s\notin(t-a)\) implies \(s(a)\neq0\), the image denominator is outside \((x-a,y-b)\).  Moreover the preimage of the target maximal ideal is \((t-a)k[t]_{(t-a)}\), so the stalk map is local.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Classical polynomial maps are recovered exactly from coordinate-ring homomorphisms.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Replace $t\mapsto x$ by $t\mapsto p(x,y)$.
