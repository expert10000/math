\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.19.C1 -- The affine anti-equivalence as an equivalence of categories]\label{chal:vi19-c01}
Build the two functors
\[
\Spec:(\mathbf{CRing})^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{AffSch},
\qquad
\Gamma:\mathbf{AffSch}\to(\mathbf{CRing})^{\mathrm{op}},
\]
and construct the natural isomorphisms showing they are quasi-inverse.  Your argument must identify the unit/counit maps explicitly on objects and morphisms.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Solution sketch.}
Use VI/18 global recovery and VI.19.P1.  On an affine $X$, compare $X$ with $\Spec\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$; on a ring $A$, compare $A$ with $\Gamma(\Spec A,\OO)$.  Naturality follows from the Hom correspondence.
\fi
