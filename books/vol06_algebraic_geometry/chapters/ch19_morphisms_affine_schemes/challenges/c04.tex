\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.19.C4 -- Global functions as maps and equations]\label{chal:vi19-c04}
Let $X$ be a locally ringed space and $s_1,\dots,s_n\in\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$. Construct the associated map $X\to\Spec\mathbb Z[t_1,\dots,t_n]$.  For an ideal $I\subseteq\mathbb Z[t_1,\dots,t_n]$, prove that the map factors through $\Spec(\mathbb Z[t_1,\dots,t_n]/I)$ exactly when every polynomial in $I$ vanishes after substituting the sections $s_i$.
\end{problem}
