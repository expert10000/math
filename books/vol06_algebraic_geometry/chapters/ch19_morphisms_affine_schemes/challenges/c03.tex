\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.19.C3 -- Image closure and nilpotent kernels]\label{chal:vi19-c03}
Prove that for every ring map $\varphi:A\to B$, the induced spectrum map is dense exactly when $\ker\varphi\subseteq\sqrt{(0)_A}$.  Construct three examples: injective dense, noninjective dense, and nondense.
\end{problem}
