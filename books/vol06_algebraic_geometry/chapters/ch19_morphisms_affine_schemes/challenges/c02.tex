\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.19.C2 -- Classify endomorphisms of the dual-number point]\label{chal:vi19-c02}
Let $A=k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$. Classify all $k$-algebra endomorphisms of $A$, hence all affine-scheme endomorphisms of $\Spec A$ over $k$ in the pullback-on-$k$ sense.  Determine which are automorphisms and explain why every one has the same continuous point map.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Solution sketch.}
A $k$-algebra map is determined by the image of $\varepsilon$, which must square to zero.  In $A$ every such element is $c\varepsilon$.  It is invertible as an endomorphism exactly when $c\ne0$.  The spectrum has one point.
\fi
