\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.19.C5 -- From affine compatibility to the gluing boundary]\label{chal:vi19-c05}
Let $X=D(f)\cup D(g)\subseteq\Spec A$ and let $Y=\Spec B$. Suppose morphisms $D(f)\to Y$ and $D(g)\to Y$ correspond to ring maps $B\to A_f$ and $B\to A_g$ whose composites to $A_{fg}$ agree.  Prove there is a unique ring map $B\to\Gamma(X,\OO_X)$ producing both local morphisms, and explain precisely what additional theorem VI/20 will use to turn this into a gluing statement for general schemes.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Solution sketch.}
Use the sheaf equalizer $\Gamma(X,\OO_X)\to A_f\times A_g\rightrightarrows A_{fg}$.  Agreement puts each image pair in the equalizer, producing the unique global ring map.  VI/20 generalizes this from one target affine scheme and an existing sheaf to gluing spaces and sheaves themselves.
\fi
