\begin{problem}[Restriction commutes with quotient]\label{prob:vi15-p12}
Prove $(\mathcal F/\mathcal F')|_U\cong(\mathcal F|_U)/(\mathcal F'|_U)$ for every open $U\subseteq X$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Quotient sheaves are local constructions.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Translate the sheaf statement to local representatives or stalks whenever possible.  Keep track of whether the construction is sectionwise or requires sheafification.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Compare stalks on $U$.

\begin{solution}
Restrict the quotient morphism $q:\mathcal F\to\mathcal F/\mathcal F'$ to $U$. It kills $\mathcal F'|_U$, so the quotient universal property gives a natural map
\[
\alpha:(\mathcal F|_U)/(\mathcal F'|_U)\longrightarrow(\mathcal F/\mathcal F')|_U.
\]
For $x\in U$,
\[
\bigl((\mathcal F|_U)/(\mathcal F'|_U)\bigr)_x
\cong\mathcal F_x/\mathcal F'_x
\cong
\bigl((\mathcal F/\mathcal F')|_U\bigr)_x.
\]
Under these identifications $\alpha_x$ is the identity on the quotient group. Therefore $\alpha$ is an isomorphism on every stalk and hence an isomorphism of sheaves.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Quotient sheaves are local constructions.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Formulate the same statement for sheaves of modules.  Where meaningful, test the statement on a discrete space and then on the exponential-map example to see the difference between sectionwise and local behavior.
