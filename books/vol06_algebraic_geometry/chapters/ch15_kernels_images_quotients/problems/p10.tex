\begin{problem}[Epimorphisms and local lifts]\label{prob:vi15-p10}
Prove that $\varphi$ is an epimorphism of sheaves of abelian groups iff $\im\varphi=\mathcal G$, iff every $\varphi_x$ is surjective.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Epimorphism is a local notion in sheaf theory, not sectionwise surjectivity.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Translate the sheaf statement to local representatives or stalks whenever possible.  Keep track of whether the construction is sectionwise or requires sheafification.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
For the categorical direction, use the cokernel: $\varphi$ is epi iff $\coker\varphi=0$.

\begin{solution}
First prove
\[
\varphi\text{ is epi}\iff\coker\varphi=0.
\]
If $\varphi$ is epi, let $q:\mathcal G\to\coker\varphi$ be the cokernel map. Since $q\varphi=0=0\varphi$, epimorphy gives $q=0$. The cokernel is generated locally by the image of $q$, so it must be the zero sheaf. Conversely, if $\coker\varphi=0$ and $a,b:\mathcal G\to\mathcal H$ satisfy $a\varphi=b\varphi$, then $(a-b)\varphi=0$. By the cokernel universal property, $a-b$ factors through the zero sheaf, so $a=b$.

Now
\[
\coker\varphi\cong\mathcal G/\im\varphi,
\]
so the cokernel vanishes exactly when $\im\varphi=\mathcal G$. Finally,
\[
(\im\varphi)_x=\im\varphi_x.
\]
Thus $\im\varphi=\mathcal G$ iff every $\im\varphi_x=\mathcal G_x$, i.e. iff every stalk map is surjective.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Epimorphism is a local notion in sheaf theory, not sectionwise surjectivity.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Formulate the same statement for sheaves of modules.  Where meaningful, test the statement on a discrete space and then on the exponential-map example to see the difference between sectionwise and local behavior.
