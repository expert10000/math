\begin{problem}[Kernel universal property]\label{prob:vi15-p3}
Prove that the sectionwise kernel together with its inclusion is the categorical kernel in $\Sh(X)$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
The construction is sectionwise because a kernel is defined by an equation, and equations glue.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Translate the sheaf statement to local representatives or stalks whenever possible.  Keep track of whether the construction is sectionwise or requires sheafification.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Factor every morphism killed by $\varphi$ through the subgroup kernels on each open set.

\begin{solution}
Let $\mathcal K(U)=\ker(\varphi_U)$ and let $i:\mathcal K\hookrightarrow\mathcal F$ be the inclusion. Since $\varphi_U i_U=0$ on every open set, $\varphi i=0$.

Now suppose $h:\mathcal H\to\mathcal F$ satisfies $\varphi h=0$. For $a\in\mathcal H(U)$,
\[
\varphi_U(h_U(a))=(\varphi h)_U(a)=0,
\]
so $h_U(a)$ lies in $\mathcal K(U)$. Define
\[
\widetilde h_U:\mathcal H(U)\to\mathcal K(U),\qquad a\mapsto h_U(a).
\]
Naturality of $h$ shows these maps commute with restrictions, hence define a sheaf morphism $\widetilde h:\mathcal H\to\mathcal K$, and by construction $i\widetilde h=h$.

If $u:\mathcal H\to\mathcal K$ is another factorization, then $i_Uu_U=h_U=i_U\widetilde h_U$ for every $U$. Since $i_U$ is injective, $u_U=\widetilde h_U$ for every $U$. Thus $u=\widetilde h$. This is the kernel universal property.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The construction is sectionwise because a kernel is defined by an equation, and equations glue.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Formulate the same statement for sheaves of modules.  Where meaningful, test the statement on a discrete space and then on the exponential-map example to see the difference between sectionwise and local behavior.
