\begin{problem}[VI.37.P9: A nonreduced projective line]\label{prob:vi37-09}
Describe \(X=\Proj k[x_0,x_1,x_2]/(x_2^2)\) and compare it with \(V_+(x_2)\).
\begin{solution}
The underlying topological space is the projective line \(x_2=0\).  On \(x_0\ne0\), the coordinate \(u=x_2/x_0\) satisfies \(u^2=0\); similarly on the other chart.  Thus \(X\) is a doubled projective line, not the reduced line itself.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
