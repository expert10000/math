\begin{problem}[VI.37.P12: A saturated zero-dimensional scheme]\label{prob:vi37-12}
In \(\mathbb P^2\), let \(I=(x_2,x_0x_1)\). Describe \(\Proj S/I\), and show \(I\) is saturated.
\begin{solution}
Inside the line \(x_2=0\cong\mathbb P^1\), the equation \(x_0x_1=0\) gives the two reduced points \([1:0:0]\) and \([0:1:0]\).  The ideal is the intersection \((x_1,x_2)\cap(x_0,x_2)\) of two saturated point ideals, hence is saturated.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
