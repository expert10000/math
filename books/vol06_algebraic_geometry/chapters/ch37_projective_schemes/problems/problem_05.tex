\begin{problem}[VI.37.P5: Saturation removes vertex torsion]\label{prob:vi37-05}
Let \(I=(x^2,xy,xz)\subset k[x,y,z]\). Compute \(I^{\rm sat}\) with respect to \((x,y,z)\) and compare the associated projective schemes.
\begin{solution}
Since \((x,y,z)x\subset I\), one has \(x\in I^{\rm sat}\), so \((x)\subseteq I^{\rm sat}\).  Conversely \(I\subseteq(x)\), and \((x)\) is saturated, hence \(I^{\rm sat}=(x)\).  Both ideals define the same projective line, although their affine cones differ at the origin.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
