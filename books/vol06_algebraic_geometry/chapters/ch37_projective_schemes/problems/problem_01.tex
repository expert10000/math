\begin{problem}[VI.37.P1: Quotient chart theorem]\label{prob:vi37-01}
Let \(S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]\), let \(I\) be homogeneous, and let \(f\) be homogeneous of positive degree. Prove the chart identity
\[
D_+(\bar f)\subseteq\Proj(S/I)\cong\Spec(S_{(f)}/I_{(f)}).
\]
\begin{solution}
Localization commutes with quotient, so \((S/I)_f\cong S_f/IS_f\).  The maps preserve the grading.  Taking degree-zero parts gives \((S/I)_{(f)}\cong S_{(f)}/I_{(f)}\).  VI/35 identifies the standard open with the spectrum of that degree-zero ring.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
