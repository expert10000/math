\begin{problem}[VI.37.P10: Components from minimal primes]\label{prob:vi37-10}
Determine the irreducible components of \(X=\Proj k[x_0,x_1,x_2]/(x_0x_1x_2)\).
\begin{solution}
The minimal primes are \((x_0),(x_1),(x_2)\).  None contains the irrelevant ideal.  Hence \(X\) has three irreducible components, the three coordinate lines of \(\mathbb P^2\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
