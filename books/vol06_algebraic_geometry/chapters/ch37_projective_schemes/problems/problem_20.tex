% VI37-DETAILED-PROBLEM-20
\begin{problem}[VI.37.P20: An embedded irrelevant associated prime]\label{prob:vi37-20}
Let
\[
A=k[x,y]/(x^2,xy)
\]
with the standard grading. Determine a minimal prime and an embedded associated prime, and explain which one contributes to \(\Proj A\).

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/37 extension.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The defining ideal factors as
\[
(x^2,xy)=x(x,y).
\]
Its radical is
\[
(x),
\]
so
\[
\mathfrak p=(x)
\]
is the unique minimal prime.

The class of \(x\in A\) is nonzero and its annihilator is
\[
\operatorname{Ann}_A(x)=(x,y)=A_+.
\]
Indeed \(x^2=xy=0\), while a polynomial with nonzero constant term cannot annihilate the nonzero class \(x\). Hence
\[
\mathfrak m=A_+=(x,y)
\]
is an associated prime. Since
\[
\mathfrak p\subsetneq\mathfrak m,
\]
it is embedded, not minimal.

The prime \(\mathfrak m\) contains the irrelevant ideal and therefore is not a point of Proj. The minimal prime \(\mathfrak p\) does not contain \(A_+\), because \(y\notin(x)\), and it supplies the unique projective component.

On \(D_+(y)\), \(y\) is invertible and \(xy=0\) forces \(x=0\), so the chart ring is \(k\). Thus
\[
\boxed{\Proj A\cong\Spec k}.
\]
The embedded associated prime at the irrelevant vertex disappears under Proj.
\end{solution}
\fi
