\begin{problem}[VI.37.P6: Projective closure of a hyperbola]\label{prob:vi37-06}
Find the projective closure of \(xy=1\subseteq\mathbb A^2\) and its intersection with the line at infinity.
\begin{solution}
With coordinates \([x_0:x_1:x_2]\) and \(x=x_1/x_0,y=x_2/x_0\), homogenization gives \(x_1x_2-x_0^2=0\).  At infinity \(x_0=0\), hence \(x_1x_2=0\), giving \([0:1:0]\) and \([0:0:1]\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
