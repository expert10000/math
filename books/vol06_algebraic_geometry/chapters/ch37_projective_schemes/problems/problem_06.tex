\begin{problem}[VI.37.P6: Projective closure of a hyperbola]\label{prob:vi37-06}
Find the projective closure of \(xy=1\subseteq\mathbb A^2\) and its intersection with the line at infinity.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Use homogeneous coordinates
\[
[x_0:x_1:x_2]
\]
with
\[
x=x_1/x_0,\qquad y=x_2/x_0.
\]
The degree-two homogenization of \(xy-1\) is
\[
F=x_1x_2-x_0^2.
\]
Since the affine ideal is principal, this homogeneous polynomial gives the closure:
\[
\boxed{
\overline Y
=
\Proj k[x_0,x_1,x_2]/(x_1x_2-x_0^2).
}
\]

The line at infinity is \(x_0=0\). Scheme-theoretic intersection is defined by
\[
(x_1x_2-x_0^2,x_0)
=
(x_0,x_1x_2).
\]
Inside \(\mathbb P^1=V_+(x_0)\), the equation \(x_1x_2=0\) defines two reduced points:
\[
\boxed{
[0:1:0],\qquad[0:0:1].
}
\]
\end{solution}
\fi
