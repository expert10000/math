\begin{problem}[VI.37.P4: A tangent line creates a fat intersection]\label{prob:vi37-04}
Intersect the conic \(XZ-Y^2=0\) with its tangent line \(Z=0\) at \([1:0:0]\).
\begin{solution}
The sum ideal is
\[
  (Z,XZ-Y^2)=(Z,Y^2).
\]
Hence the intersection is
\[
  \Proj\bigl(k[X,Y,Z]/(Z,Y^2)\bigr).
\]
On the chart \(X\ne0\), with \(y=Y/X\), it is \(\Spec k[y]/(y^2)\): a length-two nonreduced point.  Scheme structure records tangency.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
