\begin{problem}[VI.37.P11: Same Proj, different graded rings]\label{prob:vi37-11}
Let \(A=k[x_0,x_1,z]/(z^2,x_0z,x_1z)\). Prove \(\Proj A\cong\mathbb P^1_k\).
\begin{solution}
The standard opens \(D_+(x_0)\) and \(D_+(x_1)\) cover Proj because any homogeneous prime avoiding the irrelevant ideal must avoid at least one of \(x_0,x_1\); the element \(z\) cannot rescue a prime since it is nilpotent.  On \(D_+(x_i)\), the relation \(x_i z=0\) and invertibility of \(x_i\) force \(z=0\).  The chart rings are therefore exactly those of \(\mathbb P^1\), with the same overlap.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
