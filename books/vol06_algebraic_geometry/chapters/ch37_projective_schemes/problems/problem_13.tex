% VI37-DETAILED-PROBLEM-13
\begin{problem}[VI.37.P13: Union exact sequence]\label{prob:vi37-13}
Let \(I,J\subseteq S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]\) be homogeneous. Prove the exact sequence
\[
0\to S/(I\cap J)\longrightarrow S/I\oplus S/J\longrightarrow S/(I+J)\to0,
\]
and explain its projective scheme meaning.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/37 extension.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Define
\[
\alpha:S/(I\cap J)\longrightarrow S/I\oplus S/J,
\qquad
\bar s\longmapsto(s+I,s+J),
\]
and
\[
\beta:S/I\oplus S/J\longrightarrow S/(I+J),
\qquad
(a+I,b+J)\longmapsto a-b+(I+J).
\]
These are degree-zero maps of graded modules.

The kernel of \(\alpha\) consists of classes represented by elements lying in both \(I\) and \(J\), hence is zero.
The map \(\beta\) is surjective because \(c+(I+J)\) is the image of \((c+I,0+J)\).
Finally,
\[
\beta(a+I,b+J)=0
\]
means \(a-b=i+j\) with \(i\in I,j\in J\). Then
\[
a-i=b+j=:s,
\]
and \((a+I,b+J)=\alpha(s+(I\cap J))\). Thus
\[
\ker\beta=\operatorname{im}\alpha.
\]
Hence the sequence is exact.

After projective sheafification, exactness from VI/35 gives the familiar union-intersection sequence of structure sheaves. Algebraically,
\[
\Proj S/(I\cap J)
\]
is the scheme-theoretic union,
\[
\Proj S/(I+J)
\]
is the scheme-theoretic intersection, and the middle terms record the two pieces separately.
\end{solution}
\fi
