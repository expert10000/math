\begin{problem}[VI.37.P8: Empty projective scheme]\label{prob:vi37-08}
Show that \(\Proj k[x_0,x_1]/(x_0,x_1)^2\) is empty although the quotient ring is nonzero.
\begin{solution}
The irrelevant ideal of the quotient is nilpotent: its square is zero.  Every prime contains every nilpotent element, hence contains the irrelevant ideal.  No prime qualifies as a point of Proj.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
