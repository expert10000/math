\begin{problem}[Challenge 3: Length-two tangency]\label{chal:vi37-03}
For a smooth conic in \(\mathbb P^2\), choose a rational point and compute the scheme-theoretic intersection with its tangent line on an affine chart. Show that the local intersection is isomorphic to \(\Spec k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\) under suitable characteristic assumptions.
\end{problem}
