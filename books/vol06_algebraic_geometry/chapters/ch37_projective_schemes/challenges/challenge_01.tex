\begin{problem}[Challenge 1: Saturation and sheaf ideals]\label{chal:vi37-01}
Let \(I\subseteq S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]\) be homogeneous. Prove directly from standard charts that the quasi-coherent ideal sheaves associated to \(I\) and \(I^{\rm sat}\) on \(\mathbb P^n\) are equal. Explain why this is stronger than equality of underlying closed sets.
\end{problem}
