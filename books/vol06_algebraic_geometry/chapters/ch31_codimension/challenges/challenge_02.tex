\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.31.C2: Codimension through generic points]\label{chal:vi31-02}
Let $X$ be a scheme and $Y\subseteq X$ irreducible closed. Starting only from the specialization order on points, reconstruct the proof that $\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim\OO_{X,\eta_Y}$. Identify exactly where the scheme-theoretic sobriety/generic-point property enters.
\end{problem}
