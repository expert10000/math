\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.31.C3: Failure modes of additivity]\label{chal:vi31-03}
Compare three settings: varieties over a field, a non-equidimensional disjoint union, and a general Noetherian spectrum. Determine which steps in the proof of $\dim Y+\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim X$ survive and which require extra hypotheses.
\end{problem}
