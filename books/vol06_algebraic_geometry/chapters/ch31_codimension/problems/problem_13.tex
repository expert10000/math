\begin{problem}[VI.31.P13: Reduction invariance]\label{prob:vi31-13}
Let \(X_{\mathrm{red}}\to X\) be reduction and let \(Y\subseteq X\) be irreducible closed. Compare codimensions of the corresponding subsets.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Reduction induces a homeomorphism
\[
|X_{\mathrm{red}}|\xrightarrow{\sim}|X|.
\]
Under this homeomorphism, irreducible closed subsets correspond bijectively and strict inclusions are preserved.
Therefore every chain
\[
Y=Z_n\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq Z_0
\]
in \(X\) corresponds to a chain of exactly the same length in \(X_{\mathrm{red}}\), and conversely.

Hence, if \(Y_{\mathrm{red}}\) denotes the corresponding irreducible closed subset of \(X_{\mathrm{red}}\),
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{codim}(Y_{\mathrm{red}},X_{\mathrm{red}})
=
\operatorname{codim}(Y,X).
}
\]
Codimension, like global dimension, is insensitive to nilpotent thickening.
\end{solution}
\fi
