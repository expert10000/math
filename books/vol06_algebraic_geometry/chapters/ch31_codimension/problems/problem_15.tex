\begin{problem}[VI.31.P15: Infimum over a closed set]\label{prob:vi31-15}
Let $W$ be a closed subset with finitely many irreducible components $W_i$. Show $\inf_{w\in W}\dim\OO_{X,w}=\min_i\operatorname{codim}(W_i,X)$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
On each $W_i$, the infimum of local-ring dimensions is attained at its generic point and equals $\operatorname{codim}(W_i,X)$. Taking the infimum over the union gives the minimum over components.
\end{solution}
