\begin{problem}[VI.31.P2: AG8 Problem 3(c): failure of dimension--codimension additivity]\label{prob:vi31-02}
Let $X=\Spec k\sqcup\mathbb A^1_k$ and let $Y=\Spec k$ be the zero-dimensional component. Show that
\[
\dim Y+\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)\neq\dim X.
\]

\textbf{Provenance.} Legacy: AG8-P3c, the exact part-(c) counterexample split from AG8 Problem 3. Part (d) is VI.30.P2; part (a) remains reserved for VI/32.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The subset $Y$ is an irreducible component of $X$, hence has codimension zero. Its dimension is zero, while $\dim X=\max\{0,1\}=1$. Thus the left side is $0+0=0$, not $1$.
\end{solution}
