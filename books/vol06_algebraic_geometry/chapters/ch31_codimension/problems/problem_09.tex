\begin{problem}[VI.31.P9: The diagonal]\label{prob:vi31-09}
Compute the codimension of the diagonal $\Delta\subseteq\mathbb A^n_k\times_k\mathbb A^n_k$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The ambient affine space has dimension $2n$ and the diagonal is isomorphic to $\mathbb A^n_k$, so variety additivity gives codimension $n$. Equivalently its prime ideal is generated by the regular sequence $x_i-y_i$.
\end{solution}
