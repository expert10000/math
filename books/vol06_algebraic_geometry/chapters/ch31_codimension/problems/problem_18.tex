\begin{problem}[VI.31.P18: Codimension-one center]\label{prob:vi31-18}
Let $A$ be a Noetherian domain and $f\neq0$ a nonunit. If $\mathfrak p$ is minimal over $(f)$, show $V(\mathfrak p)$ has codimension one in $\Spec A$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The principal ideal theorem gives $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)\le1$. Since $A$ is a domain and $f\neq0$, $\mathfrak p\neq(0)$, while $(0)$ is prime; hence height is at least one. Therefore it is exactly one.
\end{solution}
