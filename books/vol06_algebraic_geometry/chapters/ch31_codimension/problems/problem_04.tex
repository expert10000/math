\begin{problem}[VI.31.P4: Codimension from the generic stalk]\label{prob:vi31-04}
Let $Y\subseteq X$ be irreducible closed with generic point $\eta$. Prove $\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim\OO_{X,\eta}$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Choose an affine neighborhood $U=\Spec A$ of $\eta$, corresponding to $\mathfrak p$. Every generalization of $\eta$ lies in $U$, so all codimension chains are visible in $U$. Apply the affine dictionary and $\OO_{X,\eta}=A_{\mathfrak p}$.
\end{solution}
