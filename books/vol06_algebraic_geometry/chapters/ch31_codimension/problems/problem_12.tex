\begin{problem}[VI.31.P12: Codimension zero]\label{prob:vi31-12}
Prove that an irreducible closed subset $Y\subseteq X$ has codimension zero exactly when it is an irreducible component of $X$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Codimension zero means there is no strictly larger irreducible closed subset containing $Y$. That is exactly maximality among irreducible closed subsets, the definition of irreducible component.
\end{solution}
