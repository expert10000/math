\begin{problem}[VI.31.P6: Closed points of affine space]\label{prob:vi31-06}
Show that every $k$-rational point of $\mathbb A^n_k$ has codimension $n$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Its maximal ideal is generated, after translation, by $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ and has height $n$. Equivalently the point has dimension zero inside the $n$-dimensional variety and additivity gives codimension $n$.
\end{solution}
