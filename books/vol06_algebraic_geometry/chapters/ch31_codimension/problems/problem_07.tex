\begin{problem}[VI.31.P7: Irreducible hypersurface]\label{prob:vi31-07}
Let $0\neq f\in k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ be irreducible and nonconstant. Compute the codimension of $V(f)$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The principal prime $(f)$ is nonzero. By the principal ideal theorem its height is at most one, and in the domain polynomial ring a nonzero prime cannot have height zero. Hence its height, and therefore the codimension, is one.
\end{solution}
