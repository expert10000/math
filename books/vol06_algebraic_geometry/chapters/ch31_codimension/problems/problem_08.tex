\begin{problem}[VI.31.P8: Redundant equations]\label{prob:vi31-08}
Compute the codimension of $V(x,xy)\subseteq\mathbb A^2_k$ and explain why counting equations fails.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The ideal $(x,xy)$ equals $(x)$, so the closed set is a line and has codimension one. The second equation is algebraically redundant.
\end{solution}
