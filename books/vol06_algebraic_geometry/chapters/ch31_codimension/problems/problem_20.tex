% VI31-DETAILED-PROBLEM-20
\begin{problem}[VI.31.P20: VI/31 routing audit]\label{prob:vi31-20}
Explain why AG8 Problem 3 contributes only part (c) to VI/31 and why part (a) must remain for VI/32.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The source problem contains counterexamples to several different dimension statements, and the reconstruction
splits them by mathematical ownership.

Part (c) asks for failure of the identity
\[
\dim Y+\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim X.
\]
That is exactly a codimension statement. VI/31 therefore owns AG8-P3c and gives the explicit
non-equidimensional example
\[
X=\Spec k\sqcup\mathbb A^1_k,
\qquad
Y=\Spec k.
\]

Part (a), by contrast, concerns a statement about the local dimension at a closed point. Its natural invariant is
\[
\dim\mathcal O_{X,x},
\]
viewed as local geometry at \(x\), not merely as the generic-point bridge used to define codimension here.
VI/32 is the chapter devoted to local dimension, tangent spaces, embedding dimension, and regularity, so AG8-P3a
must remain there.

Keeping the parts separate avoids assigning one substantive legacy subproblem to two chapters and preserves the
conceptual boundary between relative codimension and local geometry.
\end{solution}
\fi
