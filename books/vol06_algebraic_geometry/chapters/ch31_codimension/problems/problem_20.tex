\begin{problem}[VI.31.P20: VI/31 routing audit]\label{prob:vi31-20}
Explain why AG8 Problem 3 contributes only part (c) to VI/31 and why part (a) must remain for VI/32.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Part (c) is exactly the failure of $\dim Y+\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim X$, hence codimension material. Part (a) asks for failure of the closed-point local-dimension equality and belongs to the local geometry chapter. Splitting them avoids assigning one substantive source subproblem twice.
\end{solution}
