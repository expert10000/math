\begin{problem}[VI.31.P16: Concatenation inequality]\label{prob:vi31-16}
For irreducible closed $Z\subseteq Y\subseteq X$ of finite codimension, prove the concatenation inequality for codimensions.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Choose chains from $Z$ to $Y$ and from $Y$ upward whose lengths are arbitrarily close to the two suprema; concatenation produces a chain from $Z$ upward in $X$. For finite integer codimensions, maximal-length chains may be concatenated directly.
\end{solution}
