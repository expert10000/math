\begin{problem}[VI.31.P3: Codimension equals height on affine schemes]\label{prob:vi31-03}
Let $A$ be a ring and $\mathfrak p\in\Spec A$. Prove $\operatorname{codim}(V(\mathfrak p),\Spec A)=\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Prime chains ending at $\mathfrak p$ correspond, with arrows reversed, exactly to chains of irreducible closed subsets beginning at $V(\mathfrak p)$. Taking suprema of their lengths proves the equality.
\end{solution}
