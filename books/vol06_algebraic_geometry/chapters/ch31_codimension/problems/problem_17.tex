\begin{problem}[VI.31.P17: Additivity for nested subvarieties]\label{prob:vi31-17}
Let $Z\subseteq Y\subseteq X$ be irreducible closed subvarieties of a variety. Prove exact codimension additivity.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Use $\operatorname{codim}(Z,X)=\dim X-\dim Z$, and similarly for the other two pairs. Then $\dim X-\dim Z=(\dim X-\dim Y)+(\dim Y-\dim Z)$.
\end{solution}
