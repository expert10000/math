\begin{problem}[VI.31.P1: AG8 Problem 2: codimension statements]\label{prob:vi31-01}
Let $X$ be a variety over $k$ and let $Y\subseteq X$ be a closed irreducible subvariety. Prove
\[
\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\inf_{y\in Y}\dim\OO_{X,y}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\dim Y+\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim X.
\]

\textbf{Provenance.} Legacy: AG8-P2b, the codimension portion of Problem 2. Global/open dimension is VI/30; the deeper local-dimension portion remains VI/32.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Let $\eta$ be the generic point of $Y$. By the affine height dictionary, $\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim\OO_{X,\eta}$. Every $y\in Y$ is a specialization of $\eta$, so the height of the prime at $y$ is at least that at $\eta$; hence the infimum is attained at $\eta$. Choose an affine chart $\Spec A$ meeting $Y$, with $Y$ represented by $\mathfrak p$. The finite-type domain dimension formula gives $\dim A=\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)+\dim A/\mathfrak p$, which globalizes to the second equality.
\end{solution}
