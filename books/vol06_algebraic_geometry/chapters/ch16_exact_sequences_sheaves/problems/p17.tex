\begin{problem}[A point-evaluation short exact sequence]\label{prob:vi16-p17}
For a closed point $x$ of a reasonable space, let $\mathcal I_x\subseteq\mathcal C^0_X$ be the sheaf of continuous functions whose germ vanishes at $x$.  Prove $0\to\mathcal I_x\to\mathcal C^0_X\to i_x(\mathbb R)\to0$ is exact.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
It is a concrete short exact sequence supported at one point and previews ideal-sheaf quotients.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Compute the stalk map at $x$ and away from $x$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Away from $x$, the target stalk is zero.
\item At $x$, evaluation on the germ is onto constants and the kernel is $\mathcal I_{x,x}$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Away from $x$ the target stalk vanishes and the inclusion is an isomorphism locally, so exactness is immediate.  At $x$, the map sends a germ of a continuous function to its value at $x$; it is surjective because constant functions realize every real value, and its kernel is precisely the germ ideal of functions vanishing at $x$.  The stalkwise criterion gives exactness.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
A quotient supported at one point is controlled entirely by one local condition.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Replace continuous functions by smooth or holomorphic functions.

\paragraph{Related problems.}
VI.22 later uses analogous ideal-sheaf quotient sequences for closed subschemes.
