\begin{problem}[Direct sums of exact sequences]\label{prob:vi16-p14}
Prove that arbitrary direct sums of short exact sequences of sheaves of abelian groups are short exact.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is a useful stability property for constructions built from many local pieces.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Take stalks and use exactness of direct sums in abelian groups.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Stalks commute with direct sums.
\item Direct sums preserve kernels and surjections in $\mathbf{Ab}$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
At each point $x$, the stalk of the direct-sum sequence is the direct sum of the stalk sequences.  Direct sums of short exact sequences of abelian groups are short exact.  Hence every stalk sequence is exact, and the sheaf sequence is exact.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Again the stalkwise theorem reduces sheaf algebra to ordinary algebra.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Compare direct products and filtered colimits.
