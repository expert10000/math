\begin{problem}[Skyscraper exact sequences]\label{prob:vi16-p15}
Let $i_x$ be the skyscraper functor at a closed point $x$.  Show that an exact sequence of abelian groups gives an exact sequence of skyscraper sheaves and conversely the exactness at $x$ recovers the group sequence.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the point-supported test case for exactness.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Compute stalks at $x$ and away from $x$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Away from $x$ all stalks vanish.
\item At $x$ the stalk is the original group.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
For $y\neq x$, every skyscraper stalk is zero, so the induced sequence is trivially exact.  At $x$, the stalk sequence is the given group sequence.  Hence exactness of the group sequence implies sheaf exactness.  Conversely, if the skyscraper sequence is exact, taking the stalk at $x$ recovers exactness of the original group sequence.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Skyscraper sheaves embed ordinary group exactness into sheaf theory at one point.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use several skyscraper points to build finite-support examples.

\paragraph{Related problems.}
VI.15 computed kernels and quotients of skyscraper sheaves.
