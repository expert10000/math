\begin{problem}[Exactness of sheafification]\label{prob:vi16-p12}
Let $0\to\mathcal P'\to\mathcal P\to\mathcal P''\to0$ be objectwise exact presheaves of abelian groups.  Prove their sheafifications form a short exact sequence.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
It shows sheafification is not merely a repair operation but an exact functor in the abelian-group setting.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Take stalks before and after sheafification; use exactness of filtered colimits.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A presheaf stalk is a filtered colimit over neighborhoods.
\item Filtered colimits of abelian groups are exact.
\item Sheafification preserves stalks.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
At every $x$, taking the filtered colimit of the objectwise short exact sequences gives a short exact sequence of presheaf stalks.  Sheafification leaves each stalk unchanged, so the stalk sequence of the sheafified objects is short exact.  The stalkwise criterion proves the sheafified sequence is short exact.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Sheafification can fix gluing without disturbing the local algebra detected by stalks.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Ask which statements remain true for nonabelian categories.

\paragraph{Related problems.}
VI.14 gives the universal property; VI.16.P1 supplies the exactness test.
