\begin{problem}[Locally constant exact sequences]\label{prob:vi16-p16}
Starting from $0\to A'\to A\to A''\to0$, prove the associated locally constant sheaves form a short exact sequence on a locally connected space.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
It shows that constant algebraic data produces exact local systems.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
All stalks are the original groups.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Identify the stalk of each locally constant sheaf.
\item Apply the stalkwise criterion.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Each point has a connected neighborhood on which the locally constant sheaf is constant, so its stalk is the corresponding group.  Therefore the stalk sequence is canonically the original short exact group sequence at every point.  It follows that the sheaf sequence is short exact.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Exactness is insensitive to global monodromy when tested stalkwise, though morphisms of nontrivial local systems can encode monodromy.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Generalize to local systems with compatible group representations.

\paragraph{Related problems.}
VI.16.P15 is the point-supported analogue.
