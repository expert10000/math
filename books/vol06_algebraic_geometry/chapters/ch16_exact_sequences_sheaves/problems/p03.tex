\begin{problem}[Local lifting criterion for a sheaf epimorphism]\label{prob:vi16-p03}
Let $f:\F\to\G$.  Prove that the following are equivalent: $f$ is a sheaf epimorphism; every $f_x$ is surjective; and every target section is locally liftable.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This isolates the exact local content of sheaf surjectivity.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Use the image-sheaf description from VI/15.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Translate epimorphism into $\im f=\G$.
\item Take stalks.
\item Represent a preimage germ locally.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The conditions $\im f=\G$ and $(\im f)_x=\G_x$ for all $x$ are equivalent because an inclusion of sheaves is an isomorphism iff it is so on all stalks.  VI/15 gives $(\im f)_x=\im f_x$, so this is exactly stalkwise surjectivity.  If $f_x$ is onto and $g\in\G(U)$, lift $g_x$, represent the lift by a local section, and shrink until it maps to $g$.  Conversely any local lift gives a germ lift.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
A categorical epimorphism is encoded by local solvability, not global solvability.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Apply the criterion to exponential and derivative morphisms.

\paragraph{Related problems.}
VI.16.P4 and P5 provide analytic examples.
