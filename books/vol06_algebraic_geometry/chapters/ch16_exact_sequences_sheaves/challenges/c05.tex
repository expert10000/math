\begin{problem}[Challenge: From local lifts to the connecting obstruction]\label{chal:vi16-05}
For a short exact sequence, organize the overlap differences of local lifts into a Cech-style cocycle and prove that changing local lifts changes it by a coboundary-style term.  Explain why vanishing of the resulting class would be equivalent to existence of a global lift once Cech cohomology is available.
\end{problem}
