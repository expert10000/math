\begin{problem}[Challenge: Exactness of the de Rham complex]\label{chal:vi16-03}
On a smooth manifold, prove the sheaf de Rham complex is exact in positive degrees using the Poincare lemma, and identify its degree-zero kernel.
\end{problem}
\begin{hint}
% PEDAGOGY-ENRICHED-VI
Use local contractible coordinate balls and the stalkwise criterion.  The degree-zero kernel consists of locally constant functions. Test exactness on stalks; for a connecting or lifting question, identify the local preimages first and then measure the obstruction to gluing them.
\end{hint}
