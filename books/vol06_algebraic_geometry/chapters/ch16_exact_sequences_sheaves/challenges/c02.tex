\begin{problem}[Challenge: Snake lemma for sheaves]\label{chal:vi16-02}
Formulate and prove the snake lemma for a commutative diagram of sheaves with exact rows, constructing the connecting morphism from the kernel on the right to the cokernel on the left.
\end{problem}
\begin{hint}
Apply the ordinary snake lemma to every stalk.  Then explain why the stalkwise connecting maps are induced by a unique sheaf morphism; alternatively construct the connecting map locally by lifting and chasing.
\end{hint}
