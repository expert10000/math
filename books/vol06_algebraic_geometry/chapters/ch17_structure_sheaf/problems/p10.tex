\begin{problem}[Gluing on two principal opens]\label{prob:vi17-p10}
Let $U=D(f)\cup D(g)$.  Prove
\[\OO_X(U)\cong A_f\times_{A_{fg}}A_g.\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the sheaf axiom translated into explicit commutative algebra.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Use the cover by two principal opens and identify the overlap with $D(fg)$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A section produces a compatible pair by restriction.
\item A compatible pair glues uniquely by the sheaf axiom.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Restriction gives a map
\[\OO_X(U)\to A_f\times A_g.\]
Its image consists precisely of pairs with equal restrictions to $D(fg)$, hence equal images in $A_{fg}$.  Conversely, a compatible pair is a pair of sections on the cover $D(f),D(g)$ agreeing on their overlap.  The sheaf gluing axiom produces a unique section on $U$.  This establishes the fiber-product description.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The abstract sheaf equalizer becomes a concrete localization equalizer.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Generalize to a finite principal-open cover and write the full equalizer diagram.
