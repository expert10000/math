\begin{problem}[The structure sheaf of $\Spec\mathbb Z$]\label{prob:vi17-p07}
Compute $\OO(D(n))$, the stalk at $(p)$, the stalk at $(0)$, and the corresponding residue fields for $X=\Spec\mathbb Z$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
The arithmetic line shows in one example how localization, generic points, closed points, and varying residue fields interact.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Use $\mathbb Z_n=\mathbb Z[1/n]$ and localize at prime ideals.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item At $(p)$ invert all integers not divisible by $p$.
\item At $(0)$ invert every nonzero integer.
\item Then quotient the local rings by their maximal ideals.
\end{enumerate}

% VI17_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINED
% VI17_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINED
\begin{solution}
For $X=\Spec\mathbb Z$, the principal-open formula gives
\[
\OO_X(D(n))=\mathbb Z_n=\mathbb Z[1/n].
\]
At the closed point $(p)$, the stalk is obtained by inverting all integers outside $(p)$:
\[
\OO_{X,(p)}=\mathbb Z_{(p)}
=\left\{\frac ab\in\mathbb Q:p\nmid b\right\}.
\]
Its maximal ideal is $p\mathbb Z_{(p)}$, and reduction modulo that ideal gives
\[
\kappa(p)=\mathbb Z_{(p)}/p\mathbb Z_{(p)}\cong\mathbb F_p.
\]

At the generic point $(0)$, every nonzero integer is inverted, so
\[
\OO_{X,(0)}=\mathbb Q.
\]
Because this stalk is already a field, its maximal ideal is zero and its residue field is also
\[
\kappa(0)=\mathbb Q.
\]
Thus the same global integer $m$ has value $m\in\mathbb Q$ at the generic point and value $m\bmod p\in\mathbb F_p$ at the closed point $(p)$.
\end{solution}



\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
One sheaf simultaneously records rational and modular arithmetic.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Describe $D(n)$ as the arithmetic line with the prime divisors of $n$ removed.
