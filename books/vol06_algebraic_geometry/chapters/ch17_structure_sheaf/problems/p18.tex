\begin{problem}[Reconstruct the sheaf from principal-open data]\label{prob:vi17-p18}
Suppose a sheaf of rings $\mathcal F$ on $\Spec A$ is equipped with compatible isomorphisms $\mathcal F(D(f))\cong A_f$ for every $f\in A$.  Prove $\mathcal F\cong\OO_{\Spec A}$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Principal opens form a basis, so the structure sheaf is determined by its localization data there.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
A sheaf is determined by its restriction to a basis if the restriction maps and overlap identifications agree.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every open set is covered by principal opens.
\item Map sections locally using the given isomorphisms.
\item Compatibility on basis overlaps lets the local images glue.
\end{enumerate}

% VI17_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINED
% VI17_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINED
\begin{solution}
Write $X=\Spec A$.  Principal opens form a basis of the topology and are stable under finite intersection.  By hypothesis, for every $f\in A$ we have a compatible isomorphism
\[
\phi_f:\mathcal F(D(f))\xrightarrow{\sim}A_f=\OO_X(D(f)),
\]
and compatibility means these isomorphisms commute with the corresponding restriction and localization maps.

Let $U\subseteq X$ be open and choose a principal-open cover $U=\bigcup_iD(f_i)$.  Given $s\in\mathcal F(U)$, restrict it to each $D(f_i)$ and apply $\phi_{f_i}$.  On $D(f_if_j)$ the resulting sections agree, because both compatibility of the $\phi_f$ and the presheaf restriction laws identify them with the image of the same restriction of $s$.  The sheaf axiom for $\OO_X$ therefore glues these local images to a unique section
\[
\Phi_U(s)\in\OO_X(U).
\]

This construction is independent of the chosen principal cover: two covers admit the common refinement by intersections $D(f_ig_j)$, and the two candidate sections agree on that refinement, hence globally by locality.  The maps $\Phi_U$ commute with restriction for the same reason, so they define a morphism of sheaves
\[
\Phi:\mathcal F\to\OO_X.
\]

Applying the inverses $\phi_f^{-1}$ on a principal cover and gluing in $\mathcal F$ constructs a morphism $\Psi:\OO_X\to\mathcal F$.  On every principal open, $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ are inverse by construction.  Since principal opens form a basis, locality implies they are inverse on every open.  Hence $\mathcal F\cong\OO_X$ canonically.
\end{solution}



\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The entire sheaf is encoded by its values on the distinguished basis plus compatible restriction maps.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use this principle in VI/18 to identify $\OO_X|_{D(f)}$ with the structure sheaf of $A_f$.
