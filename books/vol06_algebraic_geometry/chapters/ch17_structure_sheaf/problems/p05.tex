\begin{problem}[Residue fields from local rings]\label{prob:vi17-p05}
For $\mfp\in\Spec A$, prove
\[\kappa(\mfp)=\OO_{X,\mfp}/\mfp\OO_{X,\mfp}\cong\Frac(A/\mfp).\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Residue fields are the natural value fields of regular functions at scheme-theoretic points.

\paragraph{Useful ideas.}
Use the stalk formula and commute localization with quotient.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Identify $\OO_{X,\mfp}$ with $A_{\mfp}$.
\item Quotient first by $\mfp$ and identify the multiplicative set in $A/\mfp$.
\end{enumerate}

% VI17_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINED
% VI17_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINED
\begin{solution}
By the stalk theorem,
\[
\OO_{X,\mfp}\cong A_{\mfp},
\]
and the unique maximal ideal is $\mfp A_{\mfp}$.  Hence
\[
\kappa(\mfp)=A_{\mfp}/\mfp A_{\mfp}.
\]
Let $S=A\setminus\mfp$.  Localization commutes with quotient, so
\[
S^{-1}A/S^{-1}\mfp
\cong
S^{-1}(A/\mfp).
\]
The image of $S$ in $A/\mfp$ consists of all nonzero classes: an element maps to zero exactly when it lies in $\mfp$.  Since $\mfp$ is prime, $A/\mfp$ is a domain.  Therefore localizing $A/\mfp$ at the image of $S$ inverts every nonzero element, giving
\[
S^{-1}(A/\mfp)=\Frac(A/\mfp).
\]
Combining these identifications yields
\[
\kappa(\mfp)\cong\Frac(A/\mfp).
\]
The isomorphism is canonical: the value of a germ $a/s$ is the fraction of the classes of $a$ and $s$ in the fraction field of $A/\mfp$.
\end{solution}



\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Closed and generic points are treated uniformly: the only difference is the prime quotient whose fraction field is taken.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Compute residue fields for $\Spec\mathbb Z$, $\Spec\mathbb R[x]$, and finite-type algebras over nonclosed fields.
