\begin{problem}[Challenge: Affine rigidity from global sections]\label{chal:vi17-c05}
Suppose two affine locally ringed spaces $(\Spec A,\OO_A)$ and $(\Spec B,\OO_B)$ are isomorphic.  Prove $A\cong B$ by passing to global sections.  Explain why this statement does not yet prove the full anti-equivalence between rings and affine schemes.
\end{problem}
