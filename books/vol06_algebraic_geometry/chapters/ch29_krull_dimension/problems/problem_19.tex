\begin{problem}[VI.29.P19: AG8 Krull stream reconstruction]\label{prob:vi29-19}
Explain why the Krull-dimension definitions in AG8 are tracked as a theory stream rather than as a new numbered problem.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical reconstruction of AG8 T01 Krull-dimension theory; no numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
AG8 contains both general dimension theory and numbered problems.  The chain definition of Krull dimension
belongs to the unnumbered theory routed by the scope ledger to VI/29.  It is therefore preserved as
\[
\text{AG8-T01-KRULL-DIMENSION}.
\]

By contrast, AG8 Problem 2 is a numbered multi-part problem about dimension of varieties and is already split by
mathematical ownership: the function-field slice belongs to VI/27, while the global dimension, codimension, and
local-dimension parts are reserved for VI/30--VI/32.  Repackaging AG8's general theory as another numbered
problem would duplicate and falsify the corpus.

Thus VI/29 owns the AG8 Krull-dimension \emph{theory stream}, not a fabricated new legacy problem ID.
\end{solution}
\fi
