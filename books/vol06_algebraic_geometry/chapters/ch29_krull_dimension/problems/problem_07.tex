\begin{problem}[VI.29.P7: Height equals localized dimension]\label{prob:vi29-07}
Prove $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)=\dim A_{\mathfrak p}$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Primes of $A_{\mathfrak p}$ correspond inclusion-preservingly to primes $\mathfrak q\subseteq\mathfrak p$ of $A$.  The possible chain lengths are therefore exactly those used in the definition of height.
\end{solution}
