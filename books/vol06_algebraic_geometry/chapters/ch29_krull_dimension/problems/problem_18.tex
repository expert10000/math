\begin{problem}[VI.29.P18: AG6 dimension-preview reconstruction]\label{prob:vi29-18}
Explain how the dimension-preview material routed from AG6 is absorbed into VI/29 without creating a false numbered legacy problem.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical reconstruction of the AG6 T05 dimension-preview stream; no numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The source audit separates two different notions of provenance: numbered legacy problems and theory streams.
The audited AG6 numbered problems in this region are Problems 5--7.  None of them is a standalone problem whose
mathematical content is exactly the Krull-dimension definition or the affine prime-chain theory developed here.

Nevertheless AG6 contains dimension-preview material: prime chains, the idea that dimension records the depth of
the specialization order, and examples preparing the later dimension arc.  That material is therefore preserved
as the theory-stream record
\[
\text{AG6-T05-DIMENSION-PREVIEW}
\]
and developed canonically in VI/29.

Creating an invented ``AG6 Problem 8'' would corrupt the corpus ledger by converting unnumbered theory into a
numbered exercise that does not exist in the source.  The correct reconstruction is exactly what VI/29 does:
preserve the mathematics, preserve its source stream, and make no false numbered-problem claim.
\end{solution}
