\begin{problem}[VI.29.P13: A dimension-preserving localization]\label{prob:vi29-13}
Show $\dim k[x,y]_x=2$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The dimension is at most $2$.  The chain $(0)\subsetneq(y)\subsetneq(y,x-1)$ consists of primes disjoint from powers of $x$, so it survives localization and gives the lower bound $2$.
\end{solution}
