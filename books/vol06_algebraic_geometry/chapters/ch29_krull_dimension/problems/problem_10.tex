\begin{problem}[VI.29.P10: Dimension and reduction]\label{prob:vi29-10}
Prove $\dim A=\dim A_{\mathrm{red}}$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Every prime contains the nilradical, so primes of $A$ correspond bijectively and order-preservingly to primes of $A/\sqrt{(0)}$.
\end{solution}
