\begin{problem}[VI.29.P20: AG8 Problem 3 routing audit]\label{prob:vi29-20}
Why is no part of AG8 Problem 3 consumed by VI/29?

\textbf{Provenance.} Instance audit of AG8 Problem 3; the numbered problem remains reserved for VI/30--VI/32.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
AG8 Problem 3 asks for counterexamples to specific parts of AG8 Problem 2.  Those parts test statements about
local dimension, dimension--codimension relations, and behavior of dimension under passage to nonempty open
subsets of general schemes.

VI/29 has a narrower mandate: it develops the ring-theoretic chain definition, affine prime/closed-set dictionary,
height as \(\dim A_{\mathfrak p}\), and elementary operations such as quotient, localization, reduction, product,
and integral extension.  None of the Problem 3 counterexamples is uniquely a Krull-definition or prime-chain
exercise at this stage.

Therefore consuming a subpart in VI/29 would break the intended split.  AG8 Problem 3 remains intact for the
global dimension arc VI/30--VI/32, where the statements it challenges are actually formulated and analyzed.
\end{solution}
