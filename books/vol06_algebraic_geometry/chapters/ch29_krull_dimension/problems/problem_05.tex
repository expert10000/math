\begin{problem}[VI.29.P5: Dimension of the affine-line coordinate ring]\label{prob:vi29-05}
Show $\dim k[x]=1$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
A nonzero prime in the PID $k[x]$ is generated by an irreducible polynomial and is maximal.  Hence all nontrivial chains have the form $(0)\subsetneq(f)$ and length $1$.
\end{solution}
