\begin{problem}[VI.29.P15: Integral extensions preserve dimension]\label{prob:vi29-15}
Assuming going up and incomparability, prove that an integral extension $A\subseteq B$ has $\dim A=\dim B$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Going up lifts each chain in $A$ to one of the same length in $B$, giving $\dim B\ge\dim A$.  Contraction of a strict chain in $B$ remains strict by incomparability, giving $\dim B\le\dim A$.
\end{solution}
