\begin{problem}[VI.29.P16: Normalization preserves dimension]\label{prob:vi29-16}
Deduce that an integral domain and its normalization have the same Krull dimension.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Let \(A\) be a domain and \(\overline A\) its integral closure in \(\Frac(A)\).  By definition,
\[
A\subseteq\overline A
\]
is an integral extension.  Problem~15 therefore applies directly and gives
\[
\boxed{\dim\overline A=\dim A}.
\]
Geometrically, normalization may separate branches or alter local rings, but it does not change the maximal
length of prime-specialization chains.
\end{solution}
\fi
