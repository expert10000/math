\begin{problem}[VI.29.P2: Affine chain dictionary]\label{prob:vi29-02}
Prove that prime chains in $A$ and irreducible-closed chains in $\Spec A$ have the same possible lengths.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Every irreducible closed subset is $V(\mathfrak p)$ for a unique prime.  Since $\mathfrak p\subsetneq\mathfrak q$ iff $V(\mathfrak q)\subsetneq V(\mathfrak p)$, reversing a chain preserves its length.
\end{solution}
