\begin{problem}[VI.29.P11: Dimension of a product]\label{prob:vi29-11}
Prove $\dim(A\times B)=\max\{\dim A,\dim B\}$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Every prime of $A\times B$ is of the form $\mathfrak p\times B$ or $A\times\mathfrak q$, and a chain stays within one of these two families.  Thus the longest possible length is the maximum of the two dimensions.
\end{solution}
