\begin{problem}[VI.29.P17: Component dimensions]\label{prob:vi29-17}
Let $\mathfrak p_1,\ldots,\mathfrak p_r$ be the minimal primes of a ring with finitely many minimal primes.  Explain why $\dim A=\max_i\dim(A/\mathfrak p_i)$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/29 development.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Every prime contains a minimal prime.  Every chain therefore lies inside one component $V(\mathfrak p_i)$ after choosing a minimal prime below its first term, while every chain in $A/\mathfrak p_i$ is a chain in $A$ above $\mathfrak p_i$.
\end{solution}
