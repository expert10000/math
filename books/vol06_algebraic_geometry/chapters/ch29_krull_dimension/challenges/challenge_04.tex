\begin{problem}[Challenge 4: Integral extension chain lifting]\label{chal:vi29-04}
Work through a concrete integral extension, for example $k[t^2,t^3]\subset k[t]$, and compare all prime-chain lengths explicitly.

\textbf{Hint.} Use contraction, lying over, going up, and the fact both rings are one-dimensional domains.
\end{problem}
