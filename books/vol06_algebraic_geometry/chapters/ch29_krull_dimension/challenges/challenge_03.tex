\begin{problem}[Challenge 3: Localization design]\label{chal:vi29-03}
Starting with $k[x,y,z]$, choose multiplicative sets producing localizations of dimensions $3$, $2$, $1$, and $0$.

\textbf{Hint.} Select opens retaining prime chains of the desired lengths; for dimension $0$, pass to the fraction field.
\end{problem}
