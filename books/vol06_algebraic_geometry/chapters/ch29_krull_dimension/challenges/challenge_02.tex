\begin{problem}[Challenge 2: Mixed-dimensional components]\label{chal:vi29-02}
Construct a finite-type $k$-algebra whose spectrum has one irreducible component of dimension $2$ and another of dimension $1$, and compute the ring dimension.

\textbf{Hint.} Use a product such as $k[x,y]\times k[t]$ or a connected reducible example if desired; dimension is the maximum component dimension.
\end{problem}
