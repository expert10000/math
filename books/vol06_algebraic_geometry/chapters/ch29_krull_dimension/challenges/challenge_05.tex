\begin{problem}[Challenge 5: Infinite dimension]\label{chal:vi29-05}
Analyze the coordinate-prime chains in $k[x_1,x_2,\ldots]$ and explain precisely why every finite $n$ occurs as a chain length even though no single displayed finite chain proves an infinite value by itself.

\textbf{Hint.} For each $n$, exhibit a separate length-$n$ chain; then take the supremum over all $n$.
\end{problem}
