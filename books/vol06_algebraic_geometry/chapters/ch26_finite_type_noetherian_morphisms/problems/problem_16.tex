\begin{problem}[The quasi-compactness obstruction]\label{prob:vi26-p16}
Explain precisely why \(\coprod_{n\ge1}\Spec k\to\Spec k\) fails to be finite type even though every component is finite type.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Let
\[
X=\coprod_{n\ge1}\Spec k.
\]
Every point has the affine open neighborhood given by its own component, and the coordinate ring
\(k\) is certainly finitely generated over \(k\).  Hence the structure morphism \(X\to\Spec k\)
is locally finite type.

However, the components \(X_n=\Spec k\) are pairwise disjoint open subsets and
\[
X=\bigcup_{n\ge1}X_n.
\]
No finite subfamily covers \(X\).  Thus \(X\) is not quasi-compact.  Since the target is affine,
finite type would require \(X\) itself to be quasi-compact, equivalently to possess a finite
finite-type affine cover.  The failure is therefore purely the missing quasi-compactness condition,
not a failure of finite algebra generation on individual neighborhoods.
\end{solution}
\fi
