\begin{problem}[Finite-type schemes over a field]\label{prob:vi26-p12}
Let $X$ be quasi-compact and locally of finite type over a field $k$.  Show that $X\to\Spec k$ is finite type.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The target is affine and the source is quasi-compact.  Choose an affine cover of $X$ by spectra of finitely generated $k$-algebras; quasi-compactness gives a finite subcover.  Hence the structure morphism is finite type.
\end{solution}
