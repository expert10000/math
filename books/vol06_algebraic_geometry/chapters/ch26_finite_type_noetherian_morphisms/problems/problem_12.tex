\begin{problem}[Finite-type schemes over a field]\label{prob:vi26-p12}
Let \(X\) be quasi-compact and locally of finite type over a field \(k\).  Show that \(X\to\Spec k\) is finite type.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Because the target is the single affine scheme \(\Spec k\), local finite type gives an affine open
cover
\[
X=\bigcup_{\lambda}U_\lambda,\qquad
U_\lambda=\Spec B_\lambda,
\]
with every \(B_\lambda\) finitely generated over \(k\).  Since \(X\) is quasi-compact, finitely many
of these opens cover:
\[
X=U_{\lambda_1}\cup\cdots\cup U_{\lambda_r}.
\]
Thus over the affine target \(\Spec k\), the inverse image admits a finite affine cover by
finite-type \(k\)-algebras.  This is exactly the definition of a finite-type structure morphism.
\end{solution}
\fi
