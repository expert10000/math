\begin{problem}[Locally finite type over locally Noetherian]\label{prob:vi26-p13}
Prove the first assertion of Theorem~\ref{thm:vi26-noetherian-consequence}.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Choose affine charts $V=\Spec A$ on the locally Noetherian base with $A$ Noetherian and affine source charts $U=\Spec B$ above them with $B$ finitely generated over $A$.  Hilbert's basis theorem and passage to quotients show $B$ Noetherian.  These $U$ cover $X$, so $X$ is locally Noetherian.
\end{solution}
