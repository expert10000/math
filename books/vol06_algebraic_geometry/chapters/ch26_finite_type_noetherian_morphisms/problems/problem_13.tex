\begin{problem}[Locally finite type over locally Noetherian]\label{prob:vi26-p13}
Prove the first assertion of Theorem~\ref{thm:vi26-noetherian-consequence}.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Let \(Y\) be locally Noetherian and \(f:X\to Y\) locally finite type.  Choose a Noetherian affine
cover
\[
Y=\bigcup_iV_i,\qquad V_i=\Spec A_i.
\]
By local finite type, each \(f^{-1}(V_i)\) has an affine cover
\[
U_{ij}=\Spec B_{ij}
\]
with \(B_{ij}\) finitely generated over \(A_i\).  Choose a surjection
\[
A_i[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\twoheadrightarrow B_{ij}.
\]
Hilbert's basis theorem makes the polynomial ring Noetherian, and a quotient of a Noetherian ring is
Noetherian.  Hence every \(B_{ij}\) is Noetherian.  The \(U_{ij}\) form an affine Noetherian cover
of \(X\), so \(X\) is locally Noetherian.
\end{solution}
