\begin{problem}[Finite type over Noetherian]\label{prob:vi26-p14}
If $Y$ is Noetherian and $X\to Y$ is finite type, prove $X$ Noetherian.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Problem 13 gives that $X$ is locally Noetherian.  A finite-type morphism is quasi-compact, and a Noetherian scheme $Y$ is quasi-compact; therefore $X=f^{-1}(Y)$ is quasi-compact.  Hence $X$ is Noetherian.
\end{solution}
