\begin{problem}[Closed subschemes of affine space]\label{prob:vi26-p11}
Let $Z\hookrightarrow\mathbb A^n_S$ be a closed subscheme.  Show $Z\to S$ is finite type.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Over $V=\Spec A$, the closed subscheme is affine with coordinate ring $A[x_1,\dots,x_n]/I$ for some ideal $I$.  The images of the variables generate this quotient as an $A$-algebra, regardless of whether $I$ is finitely generated.  Hence $Z\to S$ is finite type.
\end{solution}
