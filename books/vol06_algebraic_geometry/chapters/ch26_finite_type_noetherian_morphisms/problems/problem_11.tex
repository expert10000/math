\begin{problem}[Closed subschemes of affine space]\label{prob:vi26-p11}
Let \(Z\hookrightarrow\mathbb A^n_S\) be a closed subscheme.  Show \(Z\to S\) is finite type.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Work over an affine open \(V=\Spec A\subseteq S\).  Then
\[
\mathbb A^n_V=\Spec A[x_1,\ldots,x_n].
\]
The restriction \(Z_V\) is a closed subscheme of this affine scheme, so
\[
Z_V=\Spec\bigl(A[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/I\bigr)
\]
for some ideal \(I\).  The images of \(x_1,\ldots,x_n\) generate the quotient as an \(A\)-algebra,
regardless of whether \(I\) is finitely generated.  Thus \(Z_V\to V\) is finite type.  Since finite
type is local on the target, \(Z\to S\) is finite type.

This proof also highlights why finite type is weaker than finite presentation: the equations defining
the closed subscheme need not be finitely generated when the base is non-Noetherian.
\end{solution}
\fi
