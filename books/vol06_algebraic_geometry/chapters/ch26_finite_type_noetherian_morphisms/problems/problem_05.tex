\begin{problem}[Affine criterion for finite type]\label{prob:vi26-p05}
Let \(A\to B\) be a ring map.  Show that \(\Spec B\to\Spec A\) is finite type if \(B\) is finitely generated as an \(A\)-algebra.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Choose generators \(b_1,\ldots,b_n\) of \(B\) over \(A\).  Then
\[
A[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\twoheadrightarrow B,\qquad x_i\mapsto b_i.
\]
Thus the single affine source chart \(\Spec B\) is a finite-type affine chart over the affine target
\(\Spec A\).  Moreover \(\Spec B\) is quasi-compact because every affine scheme is
quasi-compact.  Hence the morphism is locally finite type and quasi-compact, therefore finite type.

Equivalently, the displayed surjection gives a factorization
\[
\Spec B\hookrightarrow\mathbb A^n_A\longrightarrow\Spec A,
\]
where the first map is a closed immersion and the second is affine \(n\)-space over the base.
No finite-generation hypothesis on the kernel is needed for finite type.
\end{solution}
\fi
