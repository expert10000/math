\begin{problem}[Legacy AG6-P6e: the three finiteness counterexamples]\label{prob:vi26-p03}
Over a field \(k\), verify: (a) \(\Spec k[x_1,x_2,\ldots]\) is not Noetherian; (b) its structure morphism to \(\Spec k\) is not locally of finite type; (c) \(\coprod_{n\ge1}\Spec k\to\Spec k\) is locally of finite type but not of finite type.
\par\smallskip\begingroup\emergencystretch=2em\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Preserves the finite-type/\allowbreak Noetherian subparts grouped in legacy row AG6-P6e.\par\endgroup
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Put \(R=k[x_1,x_2,\ldots]\).

For (a), consider
\[
I_n=(x_1,\ldots,x_n).
\]
Then \(I_n\subsetneq I_{n+1}\), because \(x_{n+1}\notin I_n\).  Hence \(R\) has a strictly
ascending infinite chain of ideals and is not Noetherian.

For (b), let \(\eta=(0)\) be the generic point of \(\Spec R\).  If the structure morphism were
locally of finite type, \(\eta\) would possess an affine neighborhood finite type over \(k\).
Any open neighborhood of \(\eta\) contains a principal open \(D(f)\) with \(f\ne0\).  If that
neighborhood were finite type, then by the affine-open criterion the smaller affine open
\[
D(f)=\Spec R_f
\]
would also be finite type over \(k\).  But \(f\) involves only finitely many variables, while
\(R_f\) still has fraction field
\[
k(x_1,x_2,\ldots),
\]
of infinite transcendence degree over \(k\).  A finitely generated \(k\)-algebra has fraction fields
of finite transcendence degree on its integral components.  Thus \(R_f\) cannot be finitely
generated over \(k\), contradiction.

For (c), every component \(\Spec k\) is itself a finite-type affine neighborhood, so
\[
\coprod_{n\ge1}\Spec k\longrightarrow\Spec k
\]
is locally of finite type.  However, the components form an open cover with no finite subcover.
The source is not quasi-compact, so the morphism is not finite type.  This example isolates exactly
the global quasi-compactness condition missing from local finite type.
\end{solution}
\fi
