\begin{problem}[Quasi-compact source over an affine target]\label{prob:vi26-p19}
Let \(X\to\Spec A\) be locally finite type and assume \(X\) is quasi-compact.  Show the morphism is finite type.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Local finite type gives an affine open cover
\[
X=\bigcup_\lambda U_\lambda,\qquad U_\lambda=\Spec B_\lambda,
\]
with each \(B_\lambda\) finitely generated over \(A\).  Since \(X\) is quasi-compact, finitely many
members cover:
\[
X=U_{\lambda_1}\cup\cdots\cup U_{\lambda_r}.
\]
The target \(\Spec A\) is affine, and we have produced exactly the finite affine finite-type cover
required in the definition.  Hence \(X\to\Spec A\) is finite type.  Equivalently, the structure
morphism is locally finite type and quasi-compact.
\end{solution}
\fi
