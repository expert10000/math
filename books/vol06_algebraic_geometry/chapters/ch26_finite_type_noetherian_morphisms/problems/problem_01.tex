\begin{problem}[Legacy AG5-P4: every affine open of a locally Noetherian scheme is Noetherian]\label{prob:vi26-p01}
Let $X$ be a scheme.  Prove the legacy theorem that $X$ is locally Noetherian if and only if every affine open $U=\Spec A\subseteq X$ has $A$ Noetherian.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Recovered numbered legacy Problem AG5-P4, previously routed by VI-SCOPE-041 but absent from the ledger.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The reverse implication is immediate.  Assume $X$ has an affine open cover $V_i=\Spec B_i$ with $B_i$ Noetherian, and fix an affine open $U=\Spec A$.  For each $x\in U$, choose $i$ with $x\in V_i$.  By simultaneous distinguished shrinking, there is a neighborhood $W_x$ of $x$ that is $D(f_x)$ in $U$ and $D(g_x)$ in $V_i$.  Hence $A_{f_x}\cong (B_i)_{g_x}$ is Noetherian.  Since $U$ is quasi-compact, finitely many $D(f_1),\dots,D(f_r)$ cover $U$.  The standard finite-localization criterion now implies $A$ is Noetherian: for any ideal $I\subseteq A$, each $I_{f_j}$ is finitely generated; lift finitely many local generators to elements of $I$, let $J$ be the ideal they generate, and use $(I/J)_{f_j}=0$ on a finite distinguished cover to conclude $I/J=0$.  Thus every ideal of $A$ is finitely generated.
\end{solution}
