\begin{problem}[Affine space is finite type]\label{prob:vi26-p10}
Show that \(\mathbb A^n_S\to S\) is finite type for every scheme \(S\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Take an affine open \(V=\Spec A\subseteq S\).  The inverse image of \(V\) in relative affine
\(n\)-space is
\[
\mathbb A^n_V=\Spec A[x_1,\ldots,x_n].
\]
This is a single affine chart, and its coordinate ring is generated by the \(n\) variables over
\(A\).  Thus the projection is locally finite type.  Because each inverse image of an affine target
open is itself affine, the projection is quasi-compact.  Hence
\[
\mathbb A^n_S\longrightarrow S
\]
is finite type.
\end{solution}
