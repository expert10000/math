\begin{problem}[Finite type is local on the target]\label{prob:vi26-p18}
Suppose $Y=\bigcup_iV_i$ is an open cover and each restricted morphism $f^{-1}(V_i)\to V_i$ is finite type.  Show $f$ is finite type.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/26 dossier developed from the audited AG5/AG6 finiteness theory streams; no additional numbered legacy problem is claimed.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Local finite type is local on the target.  Quasi-compactness of a morphism can also be checked locally on the target: for any quasi-compact open $W\subseteq Y$, choose finitely many $V_i$ covering $W$ after refinement by quasi-compact opens, and use quasi-compactness of each restricted preimage.  Hence $f$ is locally finite type and quasi-compact, so finite type.
\end{solution}
