% VI26-DETAILED-PROBLEM-02
\begin{problem}[Legacy AG6-P5 with AG5-P5 merged: locally finite type on every affine open]\label{prob:vi26-p02}
Prove that \(f:X\to Y\) is locally of finite type if and only if for every affine open \(V=\Spec B\subseteq Y\), the inverse image \(f^{-1}(V)\) has an affine open cover \(\Spec A_j\) with each \(A_j\) finitely generated over \(B\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Legacy AG6-P5; AG5-P5 is a duplicate/variant merged here, not a second canonical problem.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The ``every affine open'' condition plainly implies the definition, so only the converse requires
proof.  Assume \(f\) is locally of finite type, witnessed by an affine cover
\[
Y=\bigcup_i V_i,\qquad V_i=\Spec B_i,
\]
such that each \(f^{-1}(V_i)\) has an affine cover \(U_{ij}=\Spec C_{ij}\) with \(C_{ij}\) finitely
generated over \(B_i\).

Fix an arbitrary affine open \(V=\Spec B\subseteq Y\), and let \(x\in f^{-1}(V)\), with
\(y=f(x)\).  Choose \(i,j\) with \(y\in V_i\) and \(x\in U_{ij}\).  Inside the overlap
\(V\cap V_i\), choose a common distinguished neighborhood of \(y\):
\[
W=D(b)\subseteq V,\qquad W=D(c)\subseteq V_i.
\]
Then \(B_b\cong (B_i)_c\).  The open
\[
U_{ij}\cap f^{-1}(W)
\]
is the distinguished open \(D(\varphi(c))\) in \(U_{ij}\), where \(\varphi:B_i\to C_{ij}\) is the
structure map.  Shrink once more, if necessary, to a distinguished affine neighborhood
\(D(a)\) of \(x\) contained in this open.  Its coordinate ring is a localization of \(C_{ij}\),
hence is finitely generated over \((B_i)_c\cong B_b\).

Finally,
\[
B_b\cong B[t]/(bt-1)
\]
is finitely generated over \(B\).  By transitivity of finite generation, the chosen source
neighborhood of \(x\) is finitely generated over \(B\).  As \(x\) was arbitrary, such affine
neighborhoods cover \(f^{-1}(V)\).  Thus the finite-type condition can be checked over every affine
open of the target, not just the original witnessing cover.
\end{solution}
\fi
