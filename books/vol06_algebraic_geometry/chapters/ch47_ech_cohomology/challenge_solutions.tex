% VI47-DETAILED-CHALLENGE-01
\subsection*{Challenge VI.47.1}
\begin{solution}
For two refinement maps, the standard prism operator alternates between their chosen indices.  Expanding the faces gives \(\delta K+K\delta=\tau^*-\sigma^*\); all interior terms cancel in pairs.  Therefore different refinement choices induce the same map on cohomology.
\end{solution}

% VI47-DETAILED-CHALLENGE-02
\subsection*{Challenge VI.47.2}
\begin{solution}
On the standard cover of \(\mathbb P^r\), finite intersections have multivariable Laurent coordinate rings.  Cech cochains are indexed by Laurent exponent vectors, and the one-dimensional missing exponent interval from \(\mathbb P^1\) becomes a lattice-region problem in \(\mathbb Z^r\).  Surviving monomials are those not absorbed by the polynomial orthants contributed by the affine charts.
\end{solution}

% VI47-DETAILED-CHALLENGE-03
\subsection*{Challenge VI.47.3}
\begin{solution}
For a good cyclic cover of \(S^1\), the nerve is homotopy equivalent to the circle and the constant-sheaf Cech complex gives \(\check H^1\cong\mathbb Z\).  The one-set cover has no positive cochains and gives zero fixed-cover \(H^1\).  Refinement explains how the intrinsic class reappears.
\end{solution}

% VI47-DETAILED-CHALLENGE-04
\subsection*{Challenge VI.47.4}
\begin{solution}
Take a flabby resolution and form the double complex \(C^p(\mathfrak U,\mathcal I^q)\).  Horizontal positive cohomology vanishes by flabbiness; vertical positive cohomology on every finite intersection vanishes by the Leray assumption.  The two spectral sequences collapse to sheaf cohomology and Cech cohomology respectively, identifying them.
\end{solution}

% VI47-DETAILED-CHALLENGE-05
\subsection*{Challenge VI.47.5}
\begin{solution}
The computed dimensions satisfy
\[
h^0(\mathcal O(n))=\max(n+1,0),\qquad h^1(\mathcal O(n))=\max(-n-1,0).
\]
Thus \(\chi(\mathcal O(n))=n+1\) for all integers \(n\), exactly the genus-zero Riemann--Roch formula once the standard cover is known to be Leray.
\end{solution}
