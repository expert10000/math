\begin{problem}[Legacy AG6-P7b: function field and generic stalk]\label{prob:vi27-p03}
Let $X$ be integral with generic point $\eta$.  Prove that $\OO_{X,\eta}$ is a field and that for every nonempty affine open $U=\Spec A\subseteq X$ one has $\OO_{X,\eta}\cong\Frac(A)$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Function-field half of split legacy AG6-P7; the generic-point half was already merged into VI/09.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
By VI/09, $\eta$ is unique.  In the affine chart $U=\Spec A$, integrality makes $A$ a domain and $\eta$ corresponds to $(0)$.  Therefore $\OO_{X,\eta}\cong A_{(0)}$.  Localizing a domain at the complement of $(0)$ inverts every nonzero element, so $A_{(0)}=\Frac(A)$, a field.  Since the stalk is intrinsic to $X$, this identifies the fraction fields of all nonempty affine charts canonically.
\end{solution}
