\begin{problem}[Local rings inside the function field]\label{prob:vi27-p10}
For $x\in X$ integral, construct the canonical injection $\OO_{X,x}\hookrightarrow K(X)$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Choose an affine neighborhood $U=\Spec A$ with $x\leftrightarrow\mathfrak p$.  Then $\OO_{X,x}=A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $K(X)=\Frac(A)$.  The localization map $A_{\mathfrak p}\to\Frac(A)$ is injective because $A$ is a domain.  Independence of the chart follows from the generic-stalk identification.
\end{solution}
