\begin{problem}[Dominant morphism gives field embedding]\label{prob:vi27-p12}
Deduce that a dominant morphism $f:X\to Y$ of integral schemes induces $K(Y)\hookrightarrow K(X)$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The morphism of locally ringed spaces gives a local homomorphism $\OO_{Y,\eta_Y}\to\OO_{X,\eta_X}$.  These are fields by integrality, and a unital homomorphism of fields is injective.  This is $f^*:K(Y)\hookrightarrow K(X)$.
\end{solution}
