\begin{problem}[Dominant morphism gives field embedding]\label{prob:vi27-p12}
Deduce that a dominant morphism \(f:X\to Y\) of integral schemes induces \(K(Y)\hookrightarrow K(X)\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
By Problem~11, \(f(\eta_X)=\eta_Y\). The morphism of locally ringed spaces gives
\[
\mathcal O_{Y,\eta_Y}\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{X,\eta_X}.
\]
These stalks are \(K(Y)\) and \(K(X)\). A unital homomorphism of fields is injective, because its kernel is an
ideal and cannot contain \(1\). Therefore
\[
f^*:K(Y)\hookrightarrow K(X).
\]
Functoriality of stalk maps gives \((g\circ f)^*=f^*\circ g^*\) for a composite of dominant morphisms.
\end{solution}
\fi
