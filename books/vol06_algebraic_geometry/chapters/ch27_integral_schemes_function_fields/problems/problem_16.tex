\begin{problem}[A local-domain counterexample to global integrality]\label{prob:vi27-p16}
Show that all local rings of \(\Spec k\amalg\Spec k\) are domains although the scheme is not integral.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Each of the two points has an open neighborhood equal to its component \(\Spec k\), so its local ring is \(k\),
a field and hence a domain. Nevertheless
\[
\Spec k\amalg\Spec k
\]
is the union of two disjoint nonempty open-and-closed components and is reducible. Thus it is not integral. This
shows why domain local rings do not replace the global irreducibility condition.
\end{solution}
\fi
