\begin{problem}[A local-domain counterexample to global integrality]\label{prob:vi27-p16}
Show that all local rings of $\Spec k\amalg\Spec k$ are domains although the scheme is not integral.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Each point has local ring $k$, a field.  But the space has two disjoint nonempty open-and-closed components and is reducible.  Integrality therefore requires global irreducibility in addition to local domain behavior.
\end{solution}
