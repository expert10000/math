\begin{problem}[Affine criterion for integrality]\label{prob:vi27-p05}
Prove that a nonempty scheme is integral iff it admits an affine open cover by spectra of domains and is irreducible.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
One direction is immediate.  Conversely, an affine cover by domains makes the scheme reduced because reducedness is local; irreducibility is assumed, so the scheme is integral.
\end{solution}
