\begin{problem}[Nonempty opens have the same function field]\label{prob:vi27-p06}
If \(U\subseteq X\) is nonempty open in an integral scheme, prove \(U\) integral and \(K(U)=K(X)\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The open subscheme \(U\) is reduced, and a nonempty open of an irreducible space is irreducible. Hence \(U\)
is integral. If \(\eta\) is the generic point of \(X\), then \(\eta\in U\), and
\[
\overline{\{\eta\}}^{\,U}=U\cap\overline{\{\eta\}}^{\,X}=U,
\]
so \(\eta\) is also the generic point of \(U\). Therefore
\[
K(U)=\mathcal O_{U,\eta}=\mathcal O_{X,\eta}=K(X).
\]
\end{solution}
\fi
