\begin{problem}[Equality on a dense open]\label{prob:vi27-p08}
Let $s,t\in\OO_X(U)$ on a nonempty open of an integral scheme.  Show that if they agree on some nonempty open $V\subseteq U$, then $s=t$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Their images in $K(X)$ are determined by the germ at $\eta$.  Since $\eta\in V$, the two germs agree; injectivity of $\OO_X(U)\hookrightarrow K(X)$ yields $s=t$.
\end{solution}
