\begin{problem}[Equality on a dense open]\label{prob:vi27-p08}
Let \(s,t\in\mathcal O_X(U)\) on a nonempty open of an integral scheme. Show that if they agree on some nonempty open \(V\subseteq U\), then \(s=t\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The nonempty open \(V\) contains the generic point \(\eta\). Hence \(s\) and \(t\) have the same germ at
\(\eta\), so they have the same image in \(K(X)\). Problem~7 gives an injection
\[
\mathcal O_X(U)\hookrightarrow K(X),
\]
therefore \(s=t\) on all of \(U\).
\end{solution}
\fi
