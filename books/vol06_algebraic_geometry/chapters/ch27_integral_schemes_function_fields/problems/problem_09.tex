\begin{problem}[Regular locus of a rational function]\label{prob:vi27-p09}
For $r\in K(X)$, prove that the points where $r$ is regular form a nonempty open subset.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Choose a nonempty affine $U=\Spec A$ and write $r=a/b\in\Frac(A)$ with $b\ne0$.  On $D(b)$ the element $b$ is invertible, so $r\in A_b=\OO_X(D(b))$.  Thus the regular locus contains nonempty open $D(b)$.  Regularity is local, so the union of all opens on which $r$ is represented by a section is itself open.
\end{solution}
