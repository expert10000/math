\begin{problem}[Rational function regularity in affine coordinates]\label{prob:vi27-p19}
Let \(A\) be a domain, \(x=\mathfrak p\in\Spec A\), and \(r\in\Frac(A)\). Prove \(r\) is regular at \(x\) iff \(r=a/b\) for some \(a,b\in A\) with \(b\notin\mathfrak p\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
By definition, \(r\) is regular at \(x\) iff
\[
r\in\mathcal O_{X,x}=A_{\mathfrak p}\subseteq\Frac(A).
\]
The elements of \(A_{\mathfrak p}\) are precisely the fractions \(a/b\) with \(b\notin\mathfrak p\). Hence
membership in the local ring is equivalent to such a representation. If \(r=a/b\) with \(b\notin\mathfrak p\),
then \(x\in D(b)\) and \(r\) is represented by the regular section \(a/b\in A_b\) on that neighborhood.
\end{solution}
\fi
