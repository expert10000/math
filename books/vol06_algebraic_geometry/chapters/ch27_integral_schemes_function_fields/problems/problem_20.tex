\begin{problem}[Why normalization needs $K(X)$]\label{prob:vi27-p20}
Explain why the normalization of an integral affine scheme naturally lives inside its function field.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical bridge to VI/28.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
For $X=\Spec A$ integral, $K(X)=\Frac(A)$.  The integral closure $\overline A$ is defined as the elements of this field integral over $A$.  Thus the common function field is the ambient algebraic object in which the local normalizations are constructed; the gluing is the subject of VI/28.
\end{solution}
