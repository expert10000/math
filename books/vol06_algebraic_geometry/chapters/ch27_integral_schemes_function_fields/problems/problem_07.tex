\begin{problem}[Sections inject into \(K(X)\)]\label{prob:vi27-p07}
Prove that \(\mathcal O_X(U)\to K(X)\) is injective for every nonempty open \(U\) of an integral scheme.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Let \(s\in\mathcal O_X(U)\) have zero generic germ. Then \(s\) vanishes on some neighborhood \(V\) of
\(\eta\). Take an affine \(W=\Spec A\subseteq U\). Since \(V\cap W\ne\varnothing\), choose a principal open
\(D(f)\subseteq V\cap W\) with \(f\ne0\). If \(s|_W\) corresponds to \(a\in A\), then \(a/1=0\) in \(A_f\),
so \(f^na=0\) for some \(n\). Because \(A\) is a domain and \(f\ne0\), \(a=0\). Thus \(s\) vanishes on every
affine chart and hence on \(U\). Therefore
\[
\mathcal O_X(U)\hookrightarrow K(X).
\]
\end{solution}
\fi
