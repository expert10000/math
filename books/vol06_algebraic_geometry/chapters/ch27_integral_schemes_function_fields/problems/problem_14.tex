\begin{problem}[Function field of affine space]\label{prob:vi27-p14}
Compute $K(\mathbb A^n_k)$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Canonical VI/27 dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The coordinate ring $k[t_1,\dots,t_n]$ is a domain, so the generic stalk is its field of fractions, namely $k(t_1,\dots,t_n)$.
\end{solution}
