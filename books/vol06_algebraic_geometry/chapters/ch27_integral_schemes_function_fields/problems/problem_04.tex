\begin{problem}[Legacy AG8-P2c: the function-field slice of the variety dimension problem]\label{prob:vi27-p04}
Let $X$ be an integral finite-type $k$-scheme and $U=\Spec A\subseteq X$ nonempty affine.  Show that $K(X)=\Frac(A)$ is a finitely generated field extension of $k$ and that $\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)$ is independent of $U$.  State how this interfaces with the later equality $\dim X=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)$.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} Function-field/transcendence-degree slice of split legacy AG8-P2; dimension and codimension slices remain reserved for VI/30--VI/32.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Since $A$ is a finitely generated $k$-domain, choose generators $a_1,\dots,a_n$.  Then $K(X)=\Frac(A)=k(a_1,\dots,a_n)$, so the field extension is finitely generated.  For any other nonempty affine chart $V=\Spec B$, both $\Frac(A)$ and $\Frac(B)$ identify with the same generic stalk $\OO_{X,\eta}$; hence their transcendence degrees over $k$ agree.  The numerical equality with Krull dimension is the dimension theorem and is reserved for VI/30; here we have established the function-field invariant used in that theorem.
\end{solution}
