\begin{problem}[Legacy AG6-P6c: a scheme that is not integral]\label{prob:vi27-p02}
Give an explicit scheme that is not integral and explain exactly which part of integrality fails.
\par\smallskip\noindent\textit{Provenance.} AG6 Problem 6, integral-scheme subpart; other subparts remain in their assigned chapters.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Take
\[
X=\Spec k[x,y]/(xy).
\]
The classes of \(x\) and \(y\) are nonzero but have product zero, so the coordinate ring is not a domain. More
precisely,
\[
(xy)=(x)\cap(y),
\]
an intersection of prime ideals, so \((xy)\) is radical and \(X\) is reduced. Geometrically
\[
X=V(x)\cup V(y)
\]
is the union of two proper closed subsets. Hence the failure is irreducibility, not reducedness.
\end{solution}
\fi
