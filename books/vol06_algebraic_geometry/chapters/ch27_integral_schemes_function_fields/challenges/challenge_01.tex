\begin{problem}[Challenge 1: Reconstruct a function field from an affine atlas]\label{chal:vi27-01}
Let $X$ be integral with a finite affine cover $U_i=\Spec A_i$.  Starting only from the rings and restriction maps on overlaps, construct canonical isomorphisms $\Frac(A_i)\cong\Frac(A_j)$ satisfying the cocycle condition and recover $K(X)$.
\end{problem}
