\subsection*{Challenge VI.03.C1 --- The Boolean cube as a product algebra}
\label{chall:vi03-c1}
\paragraph{Challenge.}
Let \(X=\{0,1\}^n\subseteq\mathbb A_k^n\).  Prove
\[
I(X)=(x_1^2-x_1,\dots,x_n^2-x_n)
\]
and
\[
k[X]\cong k^{2^n}.
\]
Construct explicit idempotents selecting individual vertices.
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Orientation.} Reduce every polynomial to a multilinear one, then use evaluation on the cube.
\paragraph{Guided hints.} Modulo \(x_i^2-x_i\), every exponent is \(0\) or \(1\).  For a vertex \(a\), use \(e_a=\prod_{a_i=1}x_i\prod_{a_i=0}(1-x_i)\).
\paragraph{Solution.}
The displayed generators vanish on the cube.  Division by them reduces every polynomial
modulo the ideal to a multilinear polynomial.  If a multilinear polynomial vanishes on all
vertices, induct on \(n\): write it as \(A+x_nB\); evaluating at \(x_n=0\) gives \(A=0\) on
the \((n-1)\)-cube, while evaluating at \(1\) gives \(A+B=0\), so \(B=0\) by induction.
Hence the ideal is exactly \(I(X)\).  The vertex selectors \(e_a\) are orthogonal idempotents
summing to \(1\), giving \(k[X]\cong\prod_{a\in X}k\cong k^{2^n}\).
\paragraph{Extension.} Interpret multilinear polynomials as the unique normal forms for functions on the Boolean cube.
\paragraph{Provenance.} New challenge.
\fi
