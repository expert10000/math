\begin{problem}[Challenge: Local systems from covering spaces]
Let $p:E\to X$ be a covering space with discrete fiber. Show that the sheaf of local sections of $p$ has stalk naturally identified with the fiber $p^{-1}(x)$ at each $x$. Analyze how continuation along paths can permute these stalks, and work out the $n$th-root covering of $S^1$.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
A germ of a local section is determined by the unique point of the fiber it takes at $x$ on an evenly covered neighborhood. For the circle, continuation once around the loop cyclically permutes the $n$ roots.
