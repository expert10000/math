\begin{problem}[Challenge: Sheaves on finite Alexandrov spaces]
Let $X$ be a finite $T_0$ Alexandrov space with specialization poset. Show that every point has a smallest open neighborhood $U_x$. Prove that a sheaf of abelian groups determines a diagram $x\mapsto\mathcal F(U_x)$ with maps along the specialization order, and formulate a converse construction. Identify precisely where the sheaf condition enters.
\end{problem}
