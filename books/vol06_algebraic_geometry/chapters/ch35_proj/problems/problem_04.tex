\begin{problem}[VI.35.P4: Stalk computation]\label{prob:vi35-04}
Prove \(\OO_{\Proj S,\mathfrak p}\cong S_{(\mathfrak p)}\) and identify its maximal ideal and residue field.
\begin{solution}
Choose \(f\notin\mathfrak p\) and compute the stalk inside \(\Spec S_{(f)}\). The resulting fractions are balanced homogeneous fractions \(a/b\) with \(b\notin\mathfrak p\). Those with \(a\in\mathfrak p\) form the maximal ideal; quotienting gives the residue field.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
