\begin{problem}[VI.35.P7: A two-chart prototype]\label{prob:vi35-07}
For \(S=k[x,y]\), compute both standard charts and their overlap, including the transition \(t=y/x\leftrightarrow t^{-1}=x/y\).
\begin{solution}
The charts are \(\Spec k[t]\) and \(\Spec k[u]\), with overlap \(\Spec k[t,t^{-1}]\cong\Spec k[u,u^{-1}]\) under \(u=t^{-1}\). This is the prototype glued scheme later called \(\mathbb P^1_k\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
