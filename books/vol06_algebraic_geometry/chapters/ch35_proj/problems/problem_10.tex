\begin{problem}[VI.35.P10: Veronese invariance on charts]\label{prob:vi35-10}
Give a chartwise proof that \(\Proj S\cong\Proj S^{(d)}\) in the standard setting.
\begin{solution}
Use standard opens indexed by homogeneous elements whose degrees are divisible by \(d\). The balanced degree-zero fractions are identical before and after Veronese regrading, so the affine chart rings agree. These isomorphisms agree on overlaps and glue.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
