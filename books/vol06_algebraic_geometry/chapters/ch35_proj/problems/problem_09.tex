\begin{problem}[VI.35.P9: Homogeneous quotient topology]\label{prob:vi35-09}
For homogeneous \(I\), prove that the natural prime correspondence identifies
\[
\Proj(S/I)\longrightarrow \Proj S
\]
homeomorphically with \(V_+(I)\).
\begin{solution}
Use the homogeneous prime correspondence for quotients. A prime of \(S/I\) avoids the irrelevant ideal exactly when its inverse image in \(S\) avoids \(S_+\). Closed sets correspond under quotient ideals, so the bijection is a homeomorphism.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
