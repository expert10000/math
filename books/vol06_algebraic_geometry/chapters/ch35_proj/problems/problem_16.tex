% VI35-DETAILED-PROBLEM-16
\begin{problem}[VI.35.P16: Veronese invariance of the structure sheaf]\label{prob:vi35-16}
Under the standard hypotheses of the Veronese theorem, prove that the chartwise homeomorphism
\[
\Proj S\cong\Proj S^{(d)}
\]
identifies the structure sheaves.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/35 extension.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
P10 gives matching affine covers. If \(f\in S_{md}\), then
\[
(S^{(d)})_{(f)}
\cong
S_{(f)}
\]
by identity on balanced fractions. Under the affine chart theorem, these are exactly the coordinate rings of the
corresponding standard opens on the two Proj spaces.

Now take two such chart elements \(f,g\). On the overlap, both structure sheaves restrict by homogeneous
localization. The chart ring isomorphism commutes with these localization maps because it is induced by the identity
on the same fractions. Hence the square of restriction maps commutes on every pairwise overlap.

Therefore the affine structure-sheaf isomorphisms satisfy the gluing cocycle and combine to a global isomorphism of
locally ringed spaces:
\[
\boxed{
(\Proj S,\OO_{\Proj S})
\cong
(\Proj S^{(d)},\OO_{\Proj S^{(d)}}).
}
\]
Thus Veronese invariance is genuinely scheme-theoretic, not merely a homeomorphism of homogeneous-prime sets.
\end{solution}
\fi
