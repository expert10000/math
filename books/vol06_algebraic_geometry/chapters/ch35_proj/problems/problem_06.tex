\begin{problem}[VI.35.P6: Exactness of the tilde construction]\label{prob:vi35-06}
Prove that a short exact sequence of graded modules with degree-zero maps remains exact after applying \(M\mapsto\widetilde M\).
\begin{solution}
Restrict to \(D_+(f)\). VI/34 gives exactness after ordinary localization and taking degree zero. Hence the associated affine sheaf sequence is exact on each chart, so the global sequence is exact.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
