\begin{problem}[VI.35.P11: Why the Spec sheaf restriction is wrong]\label{prob:vi35-11}
Give a concrete example showing that \(\OO_{\Spec S}(D(f))=S_f\) is too large for projective geometry, while \(S_{(f)}\) has the correct scaling-invariant functions.
\begin{solution}
Take \(S=k[x,y]\), \(f=x\). The affine localization \(S_x\) contains \(x\), \(x^{-1}\), and fractions of all degrees. Projective functions invariant under simultaneous scaling are the degree-zero expressions generated by \(y/x\), so the correct chart ring is \(S_{(x)}=k[y/x]\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
