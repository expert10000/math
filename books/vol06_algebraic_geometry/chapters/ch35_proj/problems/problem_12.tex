\begin{problem}[VI.35.P12: Boundary to projective morphisms]\label{prob:vi35-12}
Explain why a graded map \(S\to T\) can fail to define a morphism \(\Proj T\to\Proj S\) everywhere, and formulate the obstruction in terms of irrelevant ideals.
\begin{solution}
A point \(\mathfrak q\in\Proj T\) can contract to a homogeneous prime of \(S\) that contains \(S_+\), in which case the contracted prime is not a point of \(\Proj S\). Thus one needs hypotheses ensuring projectively relevant primes remain projectively relevant, or one obtains only a morphism on a suitable open subset. VI/38 treats the systematic functorial theory.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
