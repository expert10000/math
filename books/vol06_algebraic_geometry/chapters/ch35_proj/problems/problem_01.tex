\begin{problem}[VI.35.P1: The standard-open basis]\label{prob:vi35-01}
Prove from the definitions that the sets \(D_+(f)\) form a basis and give an explicit basis refinement inside an arbitrary \(V_+(I)^c\).
\begin{solution}
If \(\mathfrak p\notin V_+(I)\), choose a homogeneous element \(f\in I\setminus\mathfrak p\). Then \(\mathfrak p\in D_+(f)\subseteq V_+(I)^c\). Intersections of standard opens are standard by \(D_+(f)\cap D_+(g)=D_+(fg)\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
