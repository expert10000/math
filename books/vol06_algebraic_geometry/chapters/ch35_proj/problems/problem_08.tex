\begin{problem}[VI.35.P8: Reducible and nonreduced Proj]\label{prob:vi35-08}
Compare \(\Proj k[x,y]/(xy)\) and \(\Proj k[x,y]/(y^2)\) as topological spaces and schemes.
\begin{solution}
The first has two reduced points, detected by primes \((x)\) and \((y)\). The second has one underlying point \((y)\), but its x-chart is \(\Spec k[t]/(t^2)\), so it is nonreduced.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
