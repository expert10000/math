\begin{problem}[VI.35.P2: The affine chart correspondence]\label{prob:vi35-02}
Construct explicitly the prime correspondence in \(D_+(f)\cong\Spec S_{(f)}\) and verify that it respects basic opens.
\begin{solution}
Send \(\mathfrak p\) to \((\mathfrak pS_f)_0\). Conversely extend a prime of \(S_{(f)}\) to the homogeneous prime of \(S_f\) determined degreewise and contract to \(S\). Equal-degree powers turn homogeneous nonvanishing conditions into ordinary distinguished-open conditions in \(S_{(f)}\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
