\begin{problem}[VI.35.P3: Structure-sheaf gluing]\label{prob:vi35-03}
Using VI/20, prove that the affine schemes \(\Spec S_{(f)}\) glue along the standard overlaps to a scheme structure on \(\Proj S\).
\begin{solution}
On \(D_+(f)\cap D_+(g)\), after balancing degrees, both chart restrictions are the same localization, represented by \(S_{(fg)}\) or an equal-power variant. Localization in stages supplies the cocycle identities, so VI/20 applies.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
