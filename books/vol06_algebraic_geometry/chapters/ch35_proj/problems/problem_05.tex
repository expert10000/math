\begin{problem}[VI.35.P5: Graded modules become quasi-coherent sheaves]\label{prob:vi35-05}
Construct \(\widetilde M\) from the modules \(M_{(f)}\) and prove quasi-coherence.
\begin{solution}
On every standard affine chart use VI/33 to form the module sheaf associated to the \(S_{(f)}\)-module \(M_{(f)}\). Homogeneous localization in stages identifies these sheaves on overlaps, so they glue. Quasi-coherence is affine-local.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
