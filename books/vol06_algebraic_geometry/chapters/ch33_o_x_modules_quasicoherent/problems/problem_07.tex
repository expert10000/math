\begin{problem}[VI.33.P7: Tensor product and scheme-theoretic intersection]\label{prob:vi33-07}
For ideals $I,J\subseteq A$, prove $\widetilde{A/I}\otimes\widetilde{A/J}\cong\widetilde{A/(I+J)}$ and interpret the support.

\begin{solution}Use $(A/I)\otimes_A(A/J)\cong A/(I+J)$ and compatibility of tilde with tensor products. The support is $V(I+J)=V(I)\cap V(J)$, the underlying closed set of the scheme-theoretic intersection.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
