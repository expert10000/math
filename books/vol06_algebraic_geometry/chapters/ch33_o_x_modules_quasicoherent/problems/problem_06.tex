\begin{problem}[VI.33.P6: Finite locally free versus projective]\label{prob:vi33-06}
Let $P$ be a finitely generated $A$-module. Show that $P$ is projective if and only if $\widetilde P$ is finite locally free on $\operatorname{Spec}A$.

\begin{solution}A finitely generated projective module is locally free after localization on a finite distinguished cover. Conversely, if $\widetilde P$ is finite locally free, the corresponding localizations $P_{f_i}$ are finite free on a finite cover with $(f_1,\ldots,f_r)=A$; the standard local criterion for projectivity then gives that $P$ is finitely generated projective.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
