\begin{problem}[VI.33.P3: Closed subscheme sequence]\label{prob:vi33-03}
Let $I\subseteq A$, let $i:Z=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\hookrightarrow X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, and derive the exact sequence $0\to\widetilde I\to\mathcal O_X\to i_*\mathcal O_Z\to0$.

\begin{solution}Sheafify $0\to I\to A\to A/I\to0$. Exactness of tilde gives the result, and $\widetilde{A/I}$ is precisely $i_*\mathcal O_Z$, the quotient structure sheaf viewed on $X$.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
