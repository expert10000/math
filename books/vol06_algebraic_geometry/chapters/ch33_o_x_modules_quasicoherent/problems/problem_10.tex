\begin{problem}[VI.33.P10: Local criterion for zero quasi-coherent sheaf]\label{prob:vi33-10}
Let $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$. Prove that $\mathcal F=0$ iff $M=0$, and that it is enough to check vanishing on a distinguished cover.

\begin{solution}Global sections recover $M$, so $\mathcal F=0$ implies $M=0$, and the converse is immediate. If $D(f_i)$ cover $X$ and all $M_{f_i}=0$, then every stalk is zero because each prime lies in some $D(f_i)$; hence $\mathcal F=0$.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
