\begin{problem}[VI.33.P1: Affine reconstruction for quasi-coherent sheaves]\label{prob:vi33-01}
Let $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and let $\mathcal F$ be quasi-coherent. Prove that the canonical map $\widetilde{\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)}\to\mathcal F$ is an isomorphism.

\begin{solution}Choose an affine module model $\mathcal F\cong\widetilde M$. Then $\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)\cong M$, and the canonical map becomes $\widetilde M\to\widetilde M$. Equivalently one may check on every $D(f)$, where both sides have section module $M_f$.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
