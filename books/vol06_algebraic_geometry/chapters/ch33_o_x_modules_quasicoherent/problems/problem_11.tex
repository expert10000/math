\begin{problem}[VI.33.P11: Morphisms are module maps on affine schemes]\label{prob:vi33-11}
For $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$, prove $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_X}(\widetilde M,\widetilde N)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,N)$.

\begin{solution}This is full faithfulness of the affine equivalence. Explicitly, a sheaf morphism gives a map on global sections $M\to N$, while a module map localizes compatibly on every $D(f)$ and hence defines a sheaf morphism.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
