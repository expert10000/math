\begin{problem}[VI.33.P9: A nilpotent thickening as a coherent module]\label{prob:vi33-09}
Let $A=k[t,\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$ and $M=A/(t)$. Describe $\operatorname{Supp}(\widetilde M)$ and explain what module data remain invisible in the support.

\begin{solution}The annihilator contains $(t)$, and the support is the closed locus $V(t)$. The support records only where the stalk is nonzero; it does not record the nilpotent action of $\varepsilon$ inside the module or the thickness of the ambient scheme.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
