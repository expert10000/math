\begin{problem}[VI.33.P2: Exactness of the tilde functor]\label{prob:vi33-02}
Prove that $M\mapsto\widetilde M$ is exact.

\begin{solution}Localization is exact. Therefore a sequence of modules is exact if and only if its localization at every prime is exact. Since $(\widetilde M)_{\mathfrak p}=M_{\mathfrak p}$, the sheafified sequence is exact on every stalk.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
