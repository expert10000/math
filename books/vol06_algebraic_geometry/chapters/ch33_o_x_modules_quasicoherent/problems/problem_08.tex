\begin{problem}[VI.33.P8: Affine exactness of global sections]\label{prob:vi33-08}
Let $X$ be affine and $0\to\mathcal F'\to\mathcal F\to\mathcal F''\to0$ an exact sequence of quasi-coherent sheaves. Prove that global sections remain exact on the right.

\begin{solution}Write the three sheaves as module sheaves under the affine equivalence. The sheaf sequence corresponds to an exact module sequence. Global sections recover those modules, so surjectivity on the right follows.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
