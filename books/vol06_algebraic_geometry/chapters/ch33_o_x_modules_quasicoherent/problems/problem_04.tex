\begin{problem}[VI.33.P4: Support of a finite module]\label{prob:vi33-04}
Let $M$ be a finitely generated $A$-module. Prove $\operatorname{Supp}(\widetilde M)=V(\operatorname{Ann}M)$.

\begin{solution}
If $\operatorname{Ann}M\not\subseteq\mathfrak p$, an annihilator becomes a unit in $A_{\mathfrak p}$, so $M_{\mathfrak p}=0$. Conversely, if $M_{\mathfrak p}=0$, finite generation gives one $s\notin\mathfrak p$ killing all generators, so $s\in\operatorname{Ann}M$ and $\operatorname{Ann}M\not\subseteq\mathfrak p$.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
