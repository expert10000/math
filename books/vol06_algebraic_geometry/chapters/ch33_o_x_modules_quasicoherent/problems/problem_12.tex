\begin{problem}[VI.33.P12: Bridge to graded modules]\label{prob:vi33-12}
Let $S$ be a graded ring and $M$ a graded $S$-module. Explain which part of the affine construction must change on $\operatorname{Proj}S$.

\begin{solution}The affine recipe uses $M_f$ on $D(f)$. On $\operatorname{Proj}S$, basic opens are indexed by homogeneous $f$, and their coordinate rings are the degree-zero parts $S_{(f)}$. Correspondingly one uses the degree-zero part $M_{(f)}$ of the homogeneous localization. The present chapter supplies the sheaf-gluing framework; grading supplies the new algebraic ingredient.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
