\begin{problem}[VI.33.P5: Pullback is extension of scalars]\label{prob:vi33-05}
Let $A\to B$ and $f:\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$. Prove $f^*\widetilde M\cong\widetilde{B\otimes_A M}$.

\begin{solution}Both sides are quasi-coherent on $\operatorname{Spec}B$. Their global sections are $B\otimes_A M$, and the affine equivalence identifies the corresponding sheaves. One may also verify the formula after localizing on distinguished opens.\end{solution}
\end{problem}
