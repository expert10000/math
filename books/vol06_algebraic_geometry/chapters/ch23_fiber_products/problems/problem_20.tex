\subsection*{VI.23.P20: Boundary preview: fibers without computing them}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi23-p20}
Let $X\to S$ and $s\in S$. Explain formally why $X\times_S\Spec\kappa(s)$ is the correct object to call the fiber over $s$, without performing any detailed fiber computation.
\par\textbf{Solution.} The morphism $\Spec\kappa(s)\to S$ selects the point together with its residue field. The fiber product universally represents maps to $X$ whose composite to $S$ factors through this selected point. This identifies the correct pullback construction; VI/25 develops its geometry and affine tensor formula in detail.
\end{problem}
