\subsection*{VI.23.P18: A fiber product can be empty}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi23-p18}
Give an affine criterion for $\Spec B\times_{\Spec A}\Spec C$ to be empty.
\par\textbf{Solution.} The fiber product is $\Spec(B\otimes_AC)$, so it is empty exactly when $B\otimes_AC$ is the zero ring. Thus compatibility over the base can eliminate every prime even when both factors are nonempty.
\end{problem}
\paragraph{Provenance.} Canonical tensor-product diagnostic.
