\subsection*{VI.23.P15: Construction by affine gluing}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi23-p15}
Outline a proof that fiber products of arbitrary schemes exist using affine charts.
\par\textbf{Solution.} Cover $S$ by affine opens $U_i$, then cover the inverse images in $X$ and $Y$ by affine opens $V_{ij}$ and $W_{ik}$. Form every affine product $V_{ij}\times_{U_i}W_{ik}$ by tensor products. On overlaps, localization gives canonical identifications, and uniqueness of fiber products supplies the cocycle identities. VI/20 then glues these pieces. The local projection maps glue as well and satisfy the universal property locally, hence globally.
\end{problem}
\paragraph{Provenance.} Canonical existence theorem from the AG7 fiber-product stream plus VI/20 gluing.
