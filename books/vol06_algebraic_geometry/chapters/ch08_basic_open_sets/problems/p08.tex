\begin{problem}[Quotients commute with principal localization]
Let $I\subseteq A$, $f\in A$, and let $\bar f$ denote the image of $f$ in $A/I$.
Prove
\[
(A/I)_{\bar f}\cong A_f/IA_f.
\]
Deduce a homeomorphism
\[
D_{A/I}(\bar f)\cong V_A(I)\cap D_A(f).
\]
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Geometry often imposes equations and then restricts to an open chart.  This problem proves
that the two algebraic operations are perfectly compatible.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Compare universal properties: both sides kill $I$ and invert $f$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Construct a map $A\to(A/I)_{\bar f}$ and factor it first through $A_f$, then through the
quotient by $IA_f$.

\begin{solution}
The natural map
\[
A\longrightarrow(A/I)_{\bar f}
\]
kills $I$ and sends $f$ to a unit.  Hence it factors uniquely through $A_f$, and the
factor kills $IA_f$.  Thus there is a map
\[
A_f/IA_f\longrightarrow(A/I)_{\bar f}.
\]
Conversely, the map $A\to A_f/IA_f$ kills $I$, hence factors through $A/I$, and the image
of $\bar f$ is a unit.  Therefore it factors through $(A/I)_{\bar f}$.  The two maps are
inverse by uniqueness, proving the ring isomorphism.

Taking spectra, P5 gives
\[
\Spec((A/I)_{\bar f})\cong D_{A/I}(\bar f).
\]
The quotient correspondence identifies $\Spec(A/I)$ with $V_A(I)$, and under this
identification the condition $\bar f\notin\mathfrak q$ is exactly
$f\notin\pi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$.  Hence
\[
D_{A/I}(\bar f)\cong V_A(I)\cap D_A(f).
\]
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Closed restriction and principal-open restriction commute.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Apply the theorem to $A=k[x,y]$, $I=(xy)$, and $f=x+y$.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis preparing the quotient/open compatibility used repeatedly in later
scheme chapters.
