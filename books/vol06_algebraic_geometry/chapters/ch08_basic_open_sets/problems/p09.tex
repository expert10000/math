\begin{problem}[Functoriality of principal opens]
Let $\varphi:A\to B$ and let
\[
\varphi^\ast:\Spec B\to\Spec A,
\qquad
\mathfrak q\mapsto\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q).
\]
Prove
\[
(\varphi^\ast)^{-1}(D_A(f))=D_B(\varphi(f)).
\]
Use this to reprove continuity of $\varphi^\ast$ from the principal-open basis.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Principal opens are adapted to contravariance: their inverse images are principal again.
This makes many continuity arguments one line long.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Unpack the definition of contraction.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Check when $f$ belongs to $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$.

\begin{solution}
For $\mathfrak q\in\Spec B$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak q\in(\varphi^\ast)^{-1}(D_A(f))
&\iff \varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)\in D_A(f)\\
&\iff f\notin\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)\\
&\iff \varphi(f)\notin\mathfrak q\\
&\iff \mathfrak q\in D_B(\varphi(f)).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the preimage formula holds.  Since the $D_A(f)$ form a basis of $\Spec A$ and their
preimages are open, $\varphi^\ast$ is continuous.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The basic-open topology is exactly the topology for which ring homomorphisms induce
continuous maps by contraction.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
For $k[t]\to k[x]$, $t\mapsto x^m$, compute the inverse image of $D(t-a)$.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
