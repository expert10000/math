\begin{problem}[Quotients commute with localization at a point]
Let $I\subseteq\mathfrak p\subset A$ with $\mathfrak p$ prime.  Prove
\[
(A/I)_{\mathfrak p/I}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}
\]
and prove that the corresponding residue fields are naturally isomorphic.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Closed subschemes are imposed by quotients, while local geometry is imposed by localization; the two operations must be coordinated.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use exactness of localization and then quotient by the maximal ideals.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Track the multiplicative set $A\setminus\mathfrak p$ through the quotient.

\begin{solution}
Let $S=A\setminus\mathfrak p$.  Then
\[
(A/I)_{\mathfrak p/I}=S^{-1}(A/I)
\cong S^{-1}A/S^{-1}I
=A_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}.
\]
Its maximal ideal is $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}$, so the residue field is
\[
\frac{A_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}}
{\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}}
\cong
A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}
=\kappa_A(\mathfrak p).
\]
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Imposing equations changes the local ring but not the value field at a point already satisfying those equations.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Apply this to a plane curve at a closed point.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis problem based on the local-ring and residue-field corpus.
