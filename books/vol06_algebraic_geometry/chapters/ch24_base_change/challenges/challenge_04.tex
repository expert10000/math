\begin{problem}[Challenge: Base change and scheme-theoretic intersection]\label{chall:vi24-04}
For closed subschemes $Z_1,Z_2\hookrightarrow X$ of an affine $S$-scheme, compare the scalar extension of $Z_1\cap Z_2$ with the intersection of their scalar extensions. Prove the expected equality using ideals and tensor products.
\end{problem}
