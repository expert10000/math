\begin{problem}[Affine scalar-extension formula]\label{prob:vi24-p05}
For $A\to B$ and $A\to A'$, derive the coordinate ring of the base change of $\Spec B\to\Spec A$ along $\Spec A'\to\Spec A$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
By VI/23,
\[
\Spec B\times_{\Spec A}\Spec A'\cong\Spec(B\otimes_AA').
\]
The two projections correspond to $b\mapsto b\otimes1$ and $a'\mapsto1\otimes a'$.
\end{solution}
