\begin{problem}[Open immersions are stable]\label{prob:vi24-p07}
Prove that the base change of an open immersion is an open immersion.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The assertion is local on source and target. On affine principal opens it is the ring identity
\[
B_b\otimes_AA'\cong(B\otimes_AA')_{b\otimes1}.
\]
Hence the pullback of $D(b)$ is $D(b\otimes1)$. General open immersions are covered by such affine principal opens.
\end{solution}
