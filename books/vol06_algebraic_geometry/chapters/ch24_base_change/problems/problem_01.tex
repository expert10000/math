\begin{problem}[Legacy CA13-E6: compute $\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C$]\label{prob:vi24-p01}
Compute $\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C$ and interpret the result geometrically as a base change.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Write the second copy of $\mathbb C$ as $\mathbb R[t]/(t^2+1)$. Then
\[
\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C
\cong
\mathbb C[t]/(t^2+1)
=
\mathbb C[t]/((t-i)(t+i)).
\]
The two factors are coprime in $\mathbb C[t]$, so the Chinese remainder theorem gives
\[
\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C\cong\mathbb C\times\mathbb C.
\]
Under one convenient convention the isomorphism sends a simple tensor $z\otimes w$ to
$(zw,z\overline w)$. Hence
\[
\Spec\mathbb C\times_{\Spec\mathbb R}\Spec\mathbb C
\cong
\Spec\mathbb C\amalg\Spec\mathbb C.
\]
Thus the real point with residue field $\mathbb C$ splits into two complex points after scalar extension.
\end{solution}
