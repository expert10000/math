\begin{problem}[Base change of a closed subscheme]\label{prob:vi24-p17}
If $Z\hookrightarrow X=\Spec B$ is defined by $I\subseteq B$, describe $Z_{A'}\hookrightarrow X_{A'}$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The base-changed ideal is $I(B\otimes_AA')$, and
\[
Z_{A'}\cong\Spec\bigl((B\otimes_AA')/I(B\otimes_AA')\bigr).
\]
This is the scheme-theoretic extension of the same equations.
\end{solution}
