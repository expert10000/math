\begin{problem}[Construct the base-change square]\label{prob:vi24-p03}
Given $X\to S$ and $S'\to S$, state and prove the universal property of $X_{S'}=X\times_SS'$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
This is the fiber-product universal property from VI/23 with one factor equal to $S'$. A map $T\to X_{S'}$ is uniquely equivalent to compatible maps $T\to X$ and $T\to S'$, meaning that their composites to $S$ agree.
\end{solution}
