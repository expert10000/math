\begin{problem}[Purely inseparable creation of nilpotents]\label{prob:vi24-p16}
In characteristic $p$, let $k=\mathbb F_p(s^p)$ and $L=\mathbb F_p(s)$. Show $L\otimes_kL$ is nonreduced.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
View one copy as
\[
L\cong k[u]/(u^p-s^p).
\]
After extending scalars to $L$,
\[
L\otimes_kL\cong L[u]/(u^p-s^p)=L[u]/((u-s)^p).
\]
Thus $u-s$ is nilpotent. This demonstrates that reducedness need not survive arbitrary field extension.
\end{solution}
