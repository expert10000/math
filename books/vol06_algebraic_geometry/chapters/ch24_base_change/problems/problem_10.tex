\begin{problem}[Products commute with base change]\label{prob:vi24-p10}
Show
\[
(X\times_SY)_{S'}\cong X_{S'}\times_{S'}Y_{S'}.
\]
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Both sides represent the same data from a test scheme $T$: maps $T\to X$, $T\to Y$, and $T\to S'$ whose composites to $S$ agree. The representing objects are therefore canonically isomorphic.
\end{solution}
