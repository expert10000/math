\begin{problem}[Nilpotents under field extension]\label{prob:vi24-p15}
Show that dual numbers remain nonreduced after any field extension $k\subseteq K$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
One has
\[
(k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2))\otimes_kK\cong K[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2).
\]
The class of $\varepsilon$ is still nonzero and square-zero, so the scalar extension is nonreduced.
\end{solution}
