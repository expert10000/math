\begin{problem}[Geometric interpretation of $\mathbb C/\mathbb R$ splitting]\label{prob:vi24-p14}
Explain why P1 shows that connectedness and irreducibility can change under field extension.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
$\Spec\mathbb C$ has one point and is connected and irreducible. After base change from $\mathbb R$ to $\mathbb C$, P1 gives $\Spec(\mathbb C\times\mathbb C)$, a disjoint union of two points. Thus both connectedness and irreducibility fail after this extension.
\end{solution}
