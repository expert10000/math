\begin{problem}[Base-change functoriality]\label{prob:vi24-p19}
Show that $X\mapsto X_{S'}$ and $u\mapsto u_{S'}$ define a functor $\mathrm{Sch}/S\to\mathrm{Sch}/S'$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The identity law and composition law follow by uniqueness in the universal property of the relevant pullbacks. Thus identities map to identities and composites to composites.
\end{solution}
