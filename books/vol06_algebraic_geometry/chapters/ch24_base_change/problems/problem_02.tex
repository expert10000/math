\begin{problem}[Legacy CA13-E7: scalar extension of a polynomial quotient]\label{prob:vi24-p02}
Let $k\subseteq K$ be a field extension and $f\in k[x]$. Prove
\[
(k[x]/(f))\otimes_kK\cong K[x]/(f).
\]
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Use $k[x]\otimes_kK\cong K[x]$. Tensoring the exact presentation
\[
(f)\longrightarrow k[x]\longrightarrow k[x]/(f)\longrightarrow0
\]
with $K$ gives
\[
(f)\otimes_kK\longrightarrow K[x]\longrightarrow(k[x]/(f))\otimes_kK\longrightarrow0.
\]
The image of the first map is the ideal generated by the same polynomial $f$, now regarded in $K[x]$. Therefore
\[
(k[x]/(f))\otimes_kK\cong K[x]/(f).
\]
Geometrically this is exactly the base change of the affine hypersurface defined by $f$.
\end{solution}
