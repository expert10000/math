\begin{problem}[Base change of a graph]\label{prob:vi24-p12}
Let $u:X\to Y$ be over $S$. Show that the graph of $u_{S'}$ is the base change of the graph of $u$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The graph is the pullback of the diagonal of $Y$ by VI/23. Pullbacks compose, and the diagonal itself commutes with base change by P11. Hence the two graph constructions are canonically isomorphic.
\end{solution}
