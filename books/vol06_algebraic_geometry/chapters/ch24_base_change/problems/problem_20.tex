\begin{problem}[Fiber preview without consuming VI/25]\label{prob:vi24-p20}
Explain why $X_s=X\times_S\Spec\kappa(s)$ is a base change and state what remains for VI/25.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The point morphism $\Spec\kappa(s)\to S$ is a legitimate change of base, so its pullback is the scheme-theoretic fiber. VI/25 will compute such fibers, distinguish ordinary from geometric fibers, and analyze reducedness, components, and residue-field behavior. Those calculations are not migrated here.
\end{solution}
