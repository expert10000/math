\begin{problem}[Closed immersions are stable]\label{prob:vi24-p08}
Prove that the base change of a closed immersion is a closed immersion.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Affine-locally a closed immersion is induced by a surjection $B\twoheadrightarrow B/I$. Tensoring with $A'$ remains right exact, so
\[
B\otimes_AA'\twoheadrightarrow(B/I)\otimes_AA'
\]
is surjective. Thus the base-changed morphism is affine-locally a quotient, hence a closed immersion.
\end{solution}
