\begin{problem}[Base change of a diagonal]\label{prob:vi24-p11}
Identify the base change of $\Delta_{X/S}:X\to X\times_SX$ along $S'\to S$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Using the previous dossier,
\[
(X\times_SX)_{S'}\cong X_{S'}\times_{S'}X_{S'}.
\]
Under this identification, the base-changed map has both projections equal to $\operatorname{id}_{X_{S'}}$, so by uniqueness it is $\Delta_{X_{S'}/S'}$.
\end{solution}
