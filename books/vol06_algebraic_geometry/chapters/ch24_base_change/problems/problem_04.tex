\begin{problem}[Identity and iterated base change]\label{prob:vi24-p04}
Prove $X_S\cong X$ and $(X_{S'})_{S''}\cong X_{S''}$ for $S''\to S'\to S$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The identity statement follows because $X$ itself satisfies the pullback property for $X\to S\leftarrow S$. For iteration, both sides represent maps to $X$ and $S''$ compatible over $S$; uniqueness of fiber products gives the canonical isomorphism.
\end{solution}
