\subsection*{Challenge VI.04.C3 --- Idempotent endomorphisms of the affine line}
\label{chal:vi04-c3}
\paragraph{Challenge.}
Classify all morphisms \(e:\mathbb A^1\to\mathbb A^1\) satisfying
\[
e\circ e=e.
\]
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Guided hints.}  If \(e(t)=p(t)\) is nonconstant, compare degrees of
\(p(p(t))\) and \(p(t)\).
\paragraph{Solution.}
If \(p\) is constant, every constant map is idempotent.  Otherwise, with \(d=\deg p\ge1\),
\[
d^2=\deg(p\circ p)=\deg p=d,
\]
so \(d=1\).  Write \(p(t)=at+b\).  From \(p(p(t))=p(t)\) we get
\(a^2=a\) and \(ab+b=b\).  Since \(k\) is a field, \(a=0\) or \(1\).  The nonconstant
case has \(a=1\), forcing \(b=0\), hence \(p(t)=t\).  Therefore the idempotent
endomorphisms are the identity and all constant maps.
\paragraph{Extension.}  Interpret an idempotent morphism as a retraction onto its image.
\fi
