\subsection*{Challenge VI.04.C1 --- Endomorphisms and automorphisms of the hyperbola}
\label{chal:vi04-c1}
\paragraph{Challenge.}
Write \(H=V(xy-1)\cong\mathbb G_m\).  Classify all morphisms \(H\to H\) and all
automorphisms of \(H\).
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Guided hints.}  A homomorphism \(k[u,u^{-1}]\to k[u,u^{-1}]\) is determined by
the image of \(u\), and that image must be a unit.
\paragraph{Solution.}
Every unit is \(c u^n\), \(c\in k^\times\), \(n\in\mathbb Z\), so every endomorphism has
pullback \(u\mapsto c u^n\).  It is an automorphism exactly when \(n=\pm1\): only then can
another Laurent monomial map compose with it to give exponent \(1\).  Thus geometrically
\(u\mapsto c u\) and \(u\mapsto c u^{-1}\) are precisely the automorphisms.
\paragraph{Extension.}  Describe composition in terms of the parameters \((c,n)\).
\fi
