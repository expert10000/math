\begin{problem}[Affine fiber formula]\label{prob:vi25-p02}
    Let $A\to B$ and $\mathfrak p\in\Spec A$.  Prove
\[
(\Spec B)_{\mathfrak p}\cong\Spec(B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)).
\]
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    This is the affine fiber-product formula applied to the maps $\Spec B\to\Spec A$ and $\Spec\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to\Spec A$.  The tensor product is the pushout in rings, so its spectrum is the pullback in affine schemes.
    \end{solution}
