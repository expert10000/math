\begin{problem}[Reduction modulo $2$ creates a doubled fiber]\label{prob:vi25-p15}
    Compute the fiber at $2$ of $X=\Spec\mathbb Z[x]/(x^2-2)\to\Spec\mathbb Z$.
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    Tensoring with $\mathbb F_2$ gives
\[
\mathbb F_2[x]/(x^2),
\]
so the special fiber is a nonreduced doubled point.  The generic fiber $\mathbb Q[x]/(x^2-2)$ is a reduced degree-two point.
    \end{solution}
