\subsection*{VI.25.P3: Hyperbola projection: empty and one-point fibers}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi25-p03}
For
\[
X=\Spec k[t,u]/(tu-1)\to\Spec k[t],
\]
compute every closed fiber.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This example shows that a perfectly nonempty total scheme can have an empty special fiber and one-point fibers elsewhere.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Specialize \(t\) to \(a\in k\) and analyze \(k[u]/(au-1)\).  Remember that \(\Spec 0=\varnothing\).

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Tensor the coordinate ring with \(k[t]/(t-a)\).
\item Separate the cases \(a=0\) and \(a\ne0\).
\item For \(a\ne0\), use the relation \(u=a^{-1}\).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
For a closed \(k\)-rational point \(t=a\), the residue field is \(k\), and the fiber ring is
\[
k[t,u]/(tu-1)\otimes_{k[t]}k
\cong
k[u]/(au-1).
\]
If \(a=0\), then
\[
k[u]/(au-1)=k[u]/(1)=0,
\]
so
\[
X_0=\Spec 0=\varnothing.
\]
If \(a\ne0\), evaluation at \(u=a^{-1}\) gives
\[
k[u]/(au-1)\cong k.
\]
Hence
\[
X_a\cong\Spec k
\qquad (a\ne0).
\]
Thus the family has one reduced rational point over every nonzero parameter and no point at all over \(0\).  Algebraically, the disappearance occurs because the specialized equation becomes the impossible relation \(1=0\).
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The zero ring is the correct coordinate ring for an empty fiber.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
The example is also the principal-open morphism \(\mathbb G_m	o\mathbb A^1\) in affine coordinates.

Canonical affine fiber computation.
