\begin{problem}[Reduced versus nonreduced fibers]\label{prob:vi25-p08}
Give one family whose special fiber is nonreduced although nearby fibers are reduced.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Take $X=\Spec k[t,u]/(u^2-t)\to\Spec k[t]$ with $\operatorname{char}k\ne2$.  At $t=0$ the fiber is $k[u]/(u^2)$, nonreduced.  At a nonzero value $a$ for which $u^2-a$ is squarefree, the fiber ring is reduced.  Thus reducedness need not be constant in a family.
\end{solution}
