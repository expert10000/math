\begin{problem}[A double cover with a doubled special fiber]\label{prob:vi25-p04}
    Assume $\operatorname{char}k\ne2$ and study
$X=\Spec k[t,u]/(u^2-t^2)\to\Spec k[t]$.
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    At $t=a$ the fiber is $\Spec k[u]/((u-a)(u+a))$.  If $a\ne0$, the factors are distinct and comaximal, so the fiber is two reduced points when both are $k$-rational.  At $a=0$ the ring is $k[u]/(u^2)$, a single nonreduced doubled point.  Thus the set and the nilpotent structure both jump.
    \end{solution}
