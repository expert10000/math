\subsection*{VI.25.P4: A double cover with a doubled special fiber}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi25-p04}
Assume \(\operatorname{char}k\ne2\) and study
\[
X=\Spec k[t,u]/(u^2-t^2)\to\Spec k[t].
\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the basic model in which two reduced points collide to form one nonreduced point.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Specialize \(t=a\) and factor \(u^2-a^2=(u-a)(u+a)\).  Compare the comaximal case \(a\ne0\) with the repeated-root case \(a=0\).

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item For \(a\ne0\), \((u-a)\) and \((u+a)\) are distinct because \(2a\ne0\).
\item Use the Chinese remainder theorem for nonzero \(a\).
\item At \(a=0\), identify the nilpotent class of \(u\).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
At \(t=a\), the fiber is
\[
X_a=\Spec k[u]/(u^2-a^2)
=
\Spec k[u]/((u-a)(u+a)).
\]
If \(a\ne0\), then \(\operatorname{char}k\ne2\) implies \(a\ne-a\).  Hence the ideals \((u-a)\) and \((u+a)\) are comaximal:
\[
(u+a)-(u-a)=2a\in k^\times.
\]
By the Chinese remainder theorem,
\[
k[u]/((u-a)(u+a))
\cong
k[u]/(u-a)\times k[u]/(u+a)
\cong
k\times k.
\]
Thus \(X_a\) consists of two distinct reduced \(k\)-points.

At \(a=0\),
\[
X_0=\Spec k[u]/(u^2).
\]
Its underlying topological space has one point, but the class of \(u\) is a nonzero nilpotent.  Therefore the two reduced branches coalesce into a doubled nonreduced point in the special fiber.  The family exhibits both a jump in the point set and a jump in reducedness.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
A repeated factor in the specialized equation produces nilpotent structure in the fiber.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
This is the simplest algebraic model for ramification and collision of sections.
