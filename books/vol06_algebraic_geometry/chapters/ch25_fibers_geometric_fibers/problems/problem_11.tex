\begin{problem}[A real conic after geometric extension]\label{prob:vi25-p11}
    Study $X=\Spec\mathbb R[x,y]/(x^2+y^2)$ before and after scalar extension to $\mathbb C$.
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    Over $\mathbb C$,
\[
x^2+y^2=(x+iy)(x-iy),
\]
so the complexification is the union of two lines.  The example shows that factorization and component structure can be hidden over the residue field and exposed by a geometric fiber.
    \end{solution}
