\begin{problem}[The fibers of $\Spec\mathbb Q\to\Spec\mathbb Z$]\label{prob:vi25-p07}
Compute the fiber over $(0)$ and over every $(p)$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The generic fiber is $\Spec(\mathbb Q\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q)\cong\Spec\mathbb Q$.  For $(p)$, since $p$ is invertible in $\mathbb Q$, $\mathbb Q\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb F_p\cong0$, so the fiber is empty.
\end{solution}
