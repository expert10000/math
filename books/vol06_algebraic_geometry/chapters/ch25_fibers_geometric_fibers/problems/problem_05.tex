\begin{problem}[Generic and special fibers of the affine line over $\mathbb Z$]\label{prob:vi25-p05}
Compute the generic fiber and every closed fiber of $\Spec\mathbb Z[x]\to\Spec\mathbb Z$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
At $(0)$ the residue field is $\mathbb Q$, giving $\Spec\mathbb Q[x]$.  At $(p)$ the residue field is $\mathbb F_p$, giving $\Spec\mathbb F_p[x]$.
\end{solution}
