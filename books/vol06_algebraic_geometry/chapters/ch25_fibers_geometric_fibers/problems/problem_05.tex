\subsection*{VI.25.P5: Generic and special fibers of the affine line over \(\mathbb Z\)}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi25-p05}
Compute the generic fiber and every closed fiber of
\[
\Spec\mathbb Z[x]\to\Spec\mathbb Z.
\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This calculation is the prototype for viewing one arithmetic scheme simultaneously in characteristic zero and in every prime characteristic.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the residue fields \(\kappa((0))=\mathbb Q\) and \(\kappa((p))=\mathbb F_p\).

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Tensor \(\mathbb Z[x]\) with \(\mathbb Q\) for the generic fiber.
\item Tensor with \(\mathbb F_p\) for the closed fiber at \((p)\).
\item Interpret both rings as affine lines over their new ground fields.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The generic point of \(\Spec\mathbb Z\) is \((0)\), with residue field \(\mathbb Q\).  Hence
\[
X_\eta
=
\Spec\bigl(\mathbb Z[x]\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q\bigr)
\cong
\Spec\mathbb Q[x]
=
\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb Q}.
\]
For a closed point \((p)\), the residue field is \(\mathbb F_p\).  Reduction of coefficients gives
\[
X_p
=
\Spec\bigl(\mathbb Z[x]\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb F_p\bigr)
\cong
\Spec\mathbb F_p[x]
=
\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb F_p}.
\]
Thus the single scheme \(\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb Z}\) contains a characteristic-zero generic affine line and, for every prime \(p\), an affine line in characteristic \(p\).  The equations do not change; only the residue field of the base point does.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The same integral model produces fibers in characteristic \(0\) and in every prime characteristic.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
The same method applies to any finitely presented \(\mathbb Z\)-algebra by reducing its defining equations modulo \(p\).
