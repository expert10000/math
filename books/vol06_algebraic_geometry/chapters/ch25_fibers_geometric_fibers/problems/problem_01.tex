\begin{problem}[Legacy DG4-P12: intersecting fibers degenerating to parallel lines]\label{prob:vi25-p01}
    Let $k$ be a field and let $\pi:\mathbb A^3_k\to\mathbb A^1_k$ be projection to $z$.  Preserve the legacy construction by taking
\[
X=V\bigl(y(y-zx-1)\bigr)\subset\mathbb A^3_k.
\]
Show that for $c\ne0$ the fiber $X_c$ is the union of two intersecting lines, while $X_0$ is the union of two parallel lines.
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    The fiber at $c$ is obtained by setting $z=c$:
\[
X_c=\Spec k[x,y]/\bigl(y(y-cx-1)\bigr).
\]
It is the union of the lines $L_1:y=0$ and $L_2:y=cx+1$.  If $c\ne0$, solving both equations gives
\[
x=-c^{-1},\qquad y=0,
\]
so the lines meet in exactly one affine point.  At $c=0$, the equations become $y=0$ and $y=1$, two distinct parallel lines.  The scheme-theoretic computation therefore preserves the exact legacy phenomenon and shows that the incidence pattern changes even though every fiber is a reduced union of two affine lines.
    \end{solution}
