\begin{problem}[Gaussian integers: ramified, split, inert]\label{prob:vi25-p06}
Analyze the fibers of $\Spec\mathbb Z[T]/(T^2+1)\to\Spec\mathbb Z$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The $p$-fiber is $\Spec\mathbb F_p[T]/(T^2+1)$.  For $p=2$, $T^2+1=(T+1)^2$, so it is nonreduced.  For $p\equiv1\pmod4$, the polynomial splits into two distinct linear factors, hence two reduced points.  For $p\equiv3\pmod4$, it is irreducible and the fiber is $\Spec\mathbb F_{p^2}$.
\end{solution}
