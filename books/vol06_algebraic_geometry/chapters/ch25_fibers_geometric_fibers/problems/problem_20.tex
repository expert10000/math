\begin{problem}[Ordinary fiber, geometric fiber, and set-theoretic inverse image]\label{prob:vi25-p20}
Explain the three levels of information carried by $f^{-1}(s)$, $X_s$, and $X_{\bar s}$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The set-theoretic inverse image records only points mapping to $s$.  The scheme $X_s$ equips that set with the residue-field-specialized structure sheaf, detecting nilpotents and residue fields.  The geometric fiber $X_{\bar s}$ further extends $\kappa(s)$ to an algebraically closed field, exposing splitting and inseparable phenomena.  Each level strictly refines the preceding viewpoint.
\end{solution}
