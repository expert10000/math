\begin{problem}[Factorization controls a finite arithmetic fiber]\label{prob:vi25-p19}
Let $F\in\mathbb Z[T]$ be monic. Explain how the factorization of $\overline F$ in $\mathbb F_p[T]$ controls the $p$-fiber of $\Spec\mathbb Z[T]/(F)$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The fiber is
\[
\Spec\bigl(\mathbb F_p[T]/(\overline F)\bigr).
\]
Distinct irreducible factors give distinct components, with residue fields determined by their degrees. Repeated factors produce nilpotents. Thus factorization records both splitting and ramification.
\end{solution}
