\subsection*{VI.25.P19: Factorization controls a finite arithmetic fiber}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi25-p19}
Let \(F\in\mathbb Z[T]\) be monic. Explain how the factorization of \(\overline F\) in \(\mathbb F_p[T]\) controls the \(p\)-fiber of
\[
\Spec\mathbb Z[T]/(F).
\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This dossier turns elementary polynomial factorization into a precise scheme-theoretic classifier of arithmetic fibers.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Factor \(\overline F=\prod_i f_i^{e_i}\) into distinct monic irreducibles and apply the Chinese remainder theorem.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The \(p\)-fiber ring is \(\mathbb F_p[T]/(\overline F)\).
\item Distinct irreducible factors give distinct open-and-closed primary pieces.
\item The exponent \(e_i\) measures nilpotent thickening; \(\deg f_i\) measures residue degree.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The \(p\)-fiber is
\[
X_p
=
\Spec\bigl(\mathbb F_p[T]/(\overline F)\bigr).
\]
Factor
\[
\overline F=\prod_{i=1}^r f_i^{e_i},
\]
where the \(f_i\) are distinct monic irreducible polynomials.  The ideals \((f_i^{e_i})\) are pairwise comaximal, so the Chinese remainder theorem gives
\[
\mathbb F_p[T]/(\overline F)
\cong
\prod_{i=1}^r
\mathbb F_p[T]/(f_i^{e_i}).
\]
Therefore the fiber has \(r\) connected components, one for each distinct irreducible factor.  The unique closed point of the \(i\)-th factor has residue field
\[
\mathbb F_p[T]/(f_i)\cong\mathbb F_{p^{\deg f_i}},
\]
so \(\deg f_i\) is its residue degree.

If every \(e_i=1\), then \(\overline F\) is squarefree and the fiber is reduced.  If some \(e_i>1\), the class of \(f_i\) is nilpotent in the corresponding factor, so the fiber is nonreduced.  Thus the factorization pattern records splitting through the number and degrees of irreducible factors and ramification through repeated exponents.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The scheme fiber remembers both the reduced factorization and the multiplicities of its factors.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
The Gaussian-integer example P6 is the degree-two special case of this general classifier.
