\subsection*{VI.25.P17: Purely inseparable geometric nonreducedness}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi25-p17}
In characteristic \(p\), let
\[
k=\mathbb F_p(a^p),\qquad L=k(a).
\]
Analyze
\[
\Spec L\times_{\Spec k}\Spec L.
\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the complementary phenomenon to separable splitting: instead of several reduced points, inseparability produces one point with nilpotent thickness.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Present one copy of \(L\) as \(k[T]/(T^p-a^p)\), then extend scalars to the other copy of \(L\).

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Compute \(L\otimes_kL\) using the polynomial presentation.
\item In characteristic \(p\), use \(T^p-a^p=(T-a)^p\).
\item Describe the nilradical and the unique prime ideal.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The extension \(L/k\) is purely inseparable of degree \(p\), and
\[
L\cong k[T]/(T^p-a^p).
\]
Tensoring this presentation with \(L\) over \(k\) gives
\[
L\otimes_kL
\cong
L[T]/(T^p-a^p).
\]
In characteristic \(p\),
\[
T^p-a^p=(T-a)^p.
\]
Set \(\varepsilon=T-a\).  Then
\[
L\otimes_kL\cong L[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^p).
\]
This ring has one prime ideal, \((\varepsilon)\), and residue field \(L\).  Its nilradical is also \((\varepsilon)\), with
\[
\varepsilon^p=0,\qquad \varepsilon^{p-1}\ne0.
\]
Thus the fiber product has one underlying point but a nonreduced infinitesimal thickening of length \(p\).  Both input schemes are spectra of fields and hence reduced; the nilpotents are created by the repeated \(p\)th-power factor produced by purely inseparable base change.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Separability controls whether scalar extension splits cleanly or develops repeated infinitesimal structure.
