\begin{problem}[Purely inseparable geometric nonreducedness]\label{prob:vi25-p17}
    In characteristic $p$, let $k=\mathbb F_p(a^p)$ and $L=k(a)$. Analyze $\Spec L\times_{\Spec k}\Spec L$.
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    Write $L\cong k[T]/(T^p-a^p)$.  After tensoring with $L$, the polynomial becomes
\[
T^p-a^p=(T-a)^p.
\]
Thus
\[
L\otimes_kL\cong L[T]/((T-a)^p),
\]
which has the nonzero nilpotent class of $T-a$.  A field point can therefore acquire nilpotent structure after an inseparable scalar extension.
    \end{solution}
