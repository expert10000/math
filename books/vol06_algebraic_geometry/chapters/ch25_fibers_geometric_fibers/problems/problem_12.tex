\subsection*{VI.25.P12: Fibers after base change}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi25-p12}
Let \(S'\to S\), let \(s'\mapsto s\), and set \(X'=X\times_SS'\). Prove
\[
X'_{s'}\cong X_s\times_{\kappa(s)}\kappa(s').
\]
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This comparison formula is the formal bridge from arbitrary base change in VI/24 to fiber geometry in VI/25.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Expand both sides as iterated fiber products and use the induced residue-field homomorphism \(\kappa(s)\to\kappa(s')\).

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Start with \((X\times_SS')\times_{S'}\Spec\kappa(s')\).
\item Cancel the intermediate \(S'\) by associativity.
\item Factor \(\Spec\kappa(s')\to S\) through \(\Spec\kappa(s)\).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
By definition,
\[
X'_{s'}
=
(X\times_SS')\times_{S'}\Spec\kappa(s').
\]
Associativity of fiber products gives a canonical isomorphism
\[
(X\times_SS')\times_{S'}\Spec\kappa(s')
\cong
X\times_S\Spec\kappa(s').
\]
Because \(s'\) maps to \(s\), the local homomorphism on stalks induces a field homomorphism
\[
\kappa(s)\longrightarrow\kappa(s').
\]
Therefore the map \(\Spec\kappa(s')\to S\) factors through \(\Spec\kappa(s)\to S\).  Reassociating,
\[
X\times_S\Spec\kappa(s')
\cong
\bigl(X\times_S\Spec\kappa(s)\bigr)
\times_{\Spec\kappa(s)}
\Spec\kappa(s').
\]
The first factor is \(X_s\), hence
\[
\boxed{
X'_{s'}
\cong
X_s\times_{\Spec\kappa(s)}\Spec\kappa(s')
}.
\]
So a fiber in the base-changed family is obtained from the old fiber by extension of its residue field.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Base change does not create a new fiber formula; it extends the coefficient field of the corresponding old fiber.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Iterating this identity yields the tower comparison developed in Challenge 4.
