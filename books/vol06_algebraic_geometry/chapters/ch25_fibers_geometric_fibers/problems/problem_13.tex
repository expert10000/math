\subsection*{VI.25.P13: Fiber of a closed immersion}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi25-p13}
Let
\[
i:\Spec(A/I)\hookrightarrow\Spec A.
\]
Compute the fiber at \(\mathfrak p\in\Spec A\).
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This calculation shows scheme-theoretically that a closed subscheme has a point over exactly the primes in its support.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Compute \((A/I)\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)\) and distinguish whether \(I\subseteq\mathfrak p\).

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Use \((A/I)\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)\cong\kappa(\mathfrak p)/I\kappa(\mathfrak p)\).
\item If \(I\subseteq\mathfrak p\), the image of \(I\) is zero.
\item If \(I\nsubseteq\mathfrak p\), one element of \(I\) becomes invertible.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The fiber ring is
\[
(A/I)\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)
\cong
\kappa(\mathfrak p)/I\kappa(\mathfrak p).
\]
If \(I\subseteq\mathfrak p\), every element of \(I\) maps to zero in the residue field, so
\[
I\kappa(\mathfrak p)=0
\]
and the fiber is
\[
\Spec\kappa(\mathfrak p).
\]
If \(I\nsubseteq\mathfrak p\), choose \(a\in I\setminus\mathfrak p\).  Its image in \(\kappa(\mathfrak p)\) is nonzero, hence invertible.  Therefore
\[
1\in I\kappa(\mathfrak p),
\]
so the quotient ring is zero and the fiber is empty.  Consequently
\[
i^{-1}(\mathfrak p)\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
I\subseteq\mathfrak p
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathfrak p\in V(I).
\]
When nonempty, the fiber consists of exactly one point with unchanged residue field.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The fiber criterion recovers the support \(V(I)\) from the closed immersion.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
The result is the pointwise form of stability of closed immersions under base change.
