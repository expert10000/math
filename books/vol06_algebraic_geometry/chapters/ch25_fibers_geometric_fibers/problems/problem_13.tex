\begin{problem}[Fiber of a closed immersion]\label{prob:vi25-p13}
    Let $i:\Spec(A/I)\hookrightarrow\Spec A$. Compute the fiber at $\mathfrak p\in\Spec A$.
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    The fiber ring is
\[
(A/I)\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)\cong\kappa(\mathfrak p)/I\kappa(\mathfrak p).
\]
If $I\subseteq\mathfrak p$, the image of $I$ is zero and the fiber is $\Spec\kappa(\mathfrak p)$.  Otherwise some element of $I$ becomes a nonzero, hence invertible, element of the field, so the quotient is zero and the fiber is empty.
    \end{solution}
