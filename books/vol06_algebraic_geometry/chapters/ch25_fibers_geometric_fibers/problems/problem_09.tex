\begin{problem}[Ordinary irreducibility versus geometric irreducibility]\label{prob:vi25-p09}
    View $X=\Spec\mathbb C$ as an $\mathbb R$-scheme. Compare the ordinary fiber with its base change to $\mathbb C$.
    \end{problem}
    \begin{solution}
    Over $\mathbb R$, $X$ is one point and hence irreducible.  After extending the ground field,
\[
\mathbb C\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C\cong\mathbb C\times\mathbb C,
\]
so $X_{\mathbb C}$ is two points and is reducible.  This is the basic reason geometric fibers are needed.
    \end{solution}
