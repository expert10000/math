\begin{problem}[Fiber of an open immersion]\label{prob:vi25-p14}
For $D(f)\hookrightarrow\Spec A$, compute the fiber over $\mathfrak p$.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The fiber ring is $A_f\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)$.  If $f\notin\mathfrak p$, its image is already nonzero and invertible in $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$, so the fiber is $\Spec\kappa(\mathfrak p)$.  If $f\in\mathfrak p$, localization forces zero to be invertible and the tensor product is the zero ring, so the fiber is empty.
\end{solution}
