\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.30.C1: Classify open-dimension equality]\label{chall:vi30-01}
Investigate hypotheses on a Noetherian scheme $X$ ensuring $\dim U=\dim X$ for every nonempty open $U$. Compare irreducibility, equidimensionality, Jacobson hypotheses, and finite type over a field. Produce examples showing that each naive weakening can fail.
\end{problem}
