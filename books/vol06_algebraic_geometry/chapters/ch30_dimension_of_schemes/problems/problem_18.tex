\begin{problem}[VI.30.P18: A finite union of closed subsets]\label{prob:vi30-18}
Suppose $X=Y_1\cup\cdots\cup Y_r$ with each $Y_i$ closed. Prove $\dim X=\max_i\dim Y_i$ if the $Y_i$ contain all irreducible components of $X$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical component argument.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Every irreducible closed subset of $X$, and hence the maximal member of any irreducible chain, lies in one of the $Y_i$ containing its irreducible component. Thus every chain is bounded by some $\dim Y_i$, while the reverse inequality is immediate from $Y_i\subseteq X$.
\end{solution}
