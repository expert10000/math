\begin{problem}[VI.30.P8: Reduction invariance]\label{prob:vi30-08}
Prove $\dim X_{\mathrm{red}}=\dim X$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical consequence of VI/10 and VI/29.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Reduction does not change the underlying topological space. Since global dimension is defined solely from chains of irreducible closed subsets of that space, the dimensions are equal.
\end{solution}
