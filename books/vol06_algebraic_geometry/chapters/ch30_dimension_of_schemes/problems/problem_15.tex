\begin{problem}[VI.30.P15: Arbitrary disjoint unions]\label{prob:vi30-15}
Prove \(\dim(\coprod_iX_i)=\sup_i\dim X_i\).

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical non-Noetherian extension.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Each component \(X_i\) is open and closed in
\[
X=\coprod_iX_i.
\]
An irreducible subset \(Z\subseteq X\) cannot meet two distinct components: otherwise the intersections with those
open-and-closed components would separate \(Z\) into two nonempty proper closed subsets. Thus every irreducible
closed subset lies in one \(X_i\), and an entire nested chain lies in the component containing its largest member.

Therefore every chain length in \(X\) is bounded by some \(\dim X_i\), so
\[
\dim X\le\sup_i\dim X_i.
\]
Conversely every chain inside an \(X_i\) is a chain in \(X\). Hence
\[
\boxed{
\dim\left(\coprod_iX_i\right)=\sup_i\dim X_i.
}
\]
\end{solution}
\fi
