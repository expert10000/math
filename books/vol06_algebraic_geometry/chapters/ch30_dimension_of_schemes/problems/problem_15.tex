\begin{problem}[VI.30.P15: Arbitrary disjoint unions]\label{prob:vi30-15}
Prove $\dim(\coprod_iX_i)=\sup_i\dim X_i$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical non-Noetherian extension.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Each $X_i$ is open and closed. Any irreducible subset meeting two distinct components would be the union of two proper closed subsets in its subspace topology, impossible. Thus every irreducible-closed chain lies in one component, and the supremum of all chain lengths is the supremum of component dimensions.
\end{solution}
