% VI30-DETAILED-PROBLEM-20
\begin{problem}[VI.30.P20: Hypothesis audit]\label{prob:vi30-20}
For each statement below, decide whether it holds for every scheme: (i) \(\dim U\leq\dim X\) for open \(U\); (ii) \(\dim U=\dim X\) for every nonempty open \(U\); (iii) \(\dim X=\dim X_{\mathrm{red}}\); (iv) \(\dim X=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)\).

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical synthesis dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
For (i), yes. Taking closures of a strict irreducible-closed chain in \(U\) gives a strict chain in \(X\), so
\[
\dim U\le\dim X
\]
for every open subset of every scheme.

For (ii), no. The AG8-P3d example
\[
X=\Spec k\sqcup\mathbb A^1_k,\qquad U=\Spec k
\]
has \(\dim U=0<1=\dim X\). Even integral Noetherian schemes can fail: for
\[
X=\Spec k[x,y]_{(x)},
\]
the dense open \(D(x)\) has dimension \(0\) while \(X\) has dimension \(1\). Equality is guaranteed in the
finite-type integral setting over a field.

For (iii), yes. Reduction is a homeomorphism on underlying spaces, so it preserves all irreducible-closed chains:
\[
\dim X_{\mathrm{red}}=\dim X.
\]

For (iv), no as a statement about arbitrary schemes. A general scheme may be reducible and possess no single
function field \(K(X)\). The formula
\[
\boxed{\dim X=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)}
\]
is the theorem for a variety, i.e. an integral finite-type \(k\)-scheme.

Thus (i) and (iii) are unconditional, while (ii) and (iv) require substantial hypotheses.
\end{solution}
\fi
