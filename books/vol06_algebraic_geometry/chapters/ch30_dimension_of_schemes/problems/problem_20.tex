\begin{problem}[VI.30.P20: Hypothesis audit]\label{prob:vi30-20}
For each statement below, decide whether it holds for every scheme: (i) $\dim U\leq\dim X$ for open $U$; (ii) $\dim U=\dim X$ for every nonempty open $U$; (iii) $\dim X=\dim X_{\mathrm{red}}$; (iv) $\dim X=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical synthesis dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
(i) Yes. (ii) No; AG8-P3d gives a counterexample, and even an integral Noetherian example exists. (iii) Yes, because reduction preserves the topological space. (iv) It is a finite-type integral statement over a field, not a formula for arbitrary schemes; many schemes have no single function field at all.
\end{solution}
