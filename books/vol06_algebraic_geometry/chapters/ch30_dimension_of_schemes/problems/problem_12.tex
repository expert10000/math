\begin{problem}[VI.30.P12: Dimension and transcendence degree]\label{prob:vi30-12}
Let \(X\) be a variety over \(k\). Prove \(\dim X=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)\) without assigning a second legacy destination to VI/27's function-field problem.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dimension-side completion using VI/27 data.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Put
\[
d=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X).
\]
Choose a nonempty affine open
\[
V=\Spec A\subseteq X.
\]
Because \(X\) is integral and finite type, \(A\) is a finitely generated \(k\)-domain, and VI/27 identifies
\[
\Frac(A)=K(X).
\]
The affine finite-type dimension theorem therefore gives
\[
\dim V=\dim A=\operatorname{trdeg}_k\Frac(A)=d.
\]
Since \(V\subseteq X\),
\[
\dim X\ge d.
\]

For the reverse inequality, take any chain of irreducible closed subsets
\[
Z_0\subsetneq Z_1\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq Z_n
\]
in \(X\). Choose a point \(z\in Z_0\) and an affine neighborhood
\[
W=\Spec B
\]
of \(z\). Each intersection \(Z_i\cap W\) is nonempty, irreducible, and closed in \(W\). The inclusions remain
strict. Indeed, if \(Z_i\cap W=Z_{i+1}\cap W\), then the proper closed subset \(Z_i\subsetneq Z_{i+1}\) would
contain the nonempty open subset \(Z_{i+1}\cap W\) of the irreducible space \(Z_{i+1}\), impossible.

Thus
\[
n\le\dim W.
\]
The affine open \(W\) is again a finitely generated \(k\)-domain with fraction field \(K(X)\), so
\[
\dim W=d.
\]
Hence every chain in \(X\) has length at most \(d\), giving \(\dim X\le d\). Combining inequalities,
\[
\boxed{\dim X=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)}.
\]

The function field itself remains owned by VI/27; VI/30 proves only the dimension theorem built from that data.
\end{solution}
\fi
