\begin{problem}[VI.30.P11: An integral Noetherian counterexample]\label{prob:vi30-11}
Let $A=k[x,y]_{(x)}$. Show $\dim\Spec A=1$ but $\dim D(x)=0$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical warning: finite type matters.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Because every element outside $(x)$ is inverted, $A\cong k(y)[x]_{(x)}$, a one-dimensional local PID-like domain with primes $(0)$ and $(x)$. The open $D(x)$ removes $(x)$ and leaves only $(0)$, so its dimension is $0$.
\end{solution}
