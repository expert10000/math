\begin{problem}[VI.30.P2: AG8 Problem 3(d): failure for a general scheme]\label{prob:vi30-02}
Set $X=\Spec k\sqcup\mathbb A^1_k$ and $U=\Spec k\subseteq X$. Show that $U$ is nonempty open but $\dim U\neq\dim X$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Legacy: AG8-P3d, the part-(d) counterexample split from AG8 Problem 3. Parts (a) and (c) remain reserved for VI/32 and VI/31.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Both components are open and closed. Hence $U$ is nonempty open. We have $\dim U=0$ and $\dim\mathbb A^1_k=1$, so the disjoint-union formula gives $\dim X=1$. Therefore $\dim U=0<1=\dim X$.
\end{solution}
