\begin{problem}[VI.30.P2: AG8 Problem 3(d): failure for a general scheme]\label{prob:vi30-02}
Set \(X=\Spec k\sqcup\mathbb A^1_k\) and \(U=\Spec k\subseteq X\). Show that \(U\) is nonempty open but \(\dim U\neq\dim X\).

\textbf{Provenance.} Legacy: AG8-P3d, the part-(d) counterexample split from AG8 Problem 3. Parts (a) and (c) remain reserved for VI/32 and VI/31.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
In a disjoint union each component is both open and closed. Hence
\[
U=\Spec k
\]
is a nonempty open subset of
\[
X=\Spec k\sqcup\mathbb A^1_k.
\]
Its dimension is
\[
\dim U=0.
\]
For a finite disjoint union,
\[
\dim X
=
\max\{\dim\Spec k,\dim\mathbb A^1_k\}
=
\max\{0,1\}
=
1.
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\dim U=0<1=\dim X}.
\]
The example shows that the general inequality \(\dim U\le\dim X\) cannot be strengthened to equality for arbitrary
schemes.
\end{solution}
\fi
