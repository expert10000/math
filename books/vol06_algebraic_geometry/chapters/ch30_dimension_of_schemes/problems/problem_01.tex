\begin{problem}[VI.30.P1: AG8 Problem 2: nonempty opens of a variety]\label{prob:vi30-01}
Let $X$ be a variety over $k$ and let $\varnothing\neq U\subseteq X$ be open. Prove that $\dim U=\dim X$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Legacy: AG8-P2a (global/open-dimension portion of Problem 2). The function-field setup is already canonical in VI/27; local dimension and codimension remain for VI/32 and VI/31.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Choose a nonempty affine open $V=\Spec A\subseteq U$. Since $X$ is integral of finite type, $A$ is a finitely generated $k$-domain and $\Frac(A)=K(X)$. Thus $\dim V=\operatorname{trdeg}_kK(X)=\dim X$. Since $V\subseteq U\subseteq X$, the general inequalities give $\dim X=\dim V\leq\dim U\leq\dim X$.
\end{solution}
