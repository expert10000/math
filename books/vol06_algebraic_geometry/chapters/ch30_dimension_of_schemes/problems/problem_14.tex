\begin{problem}[VI.30.P14: Generically finite morphisms]\label{prob:vi30-14}
Under the hypotheses of Problem 13, assume $K(X)$ is algebraic over $K(Y)$. Prove $\dim X=\dim Y$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical function-field application.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Algebraic field extensions do not change transcendence degree over $k$. Therefore the two function fields have equal transcendence degree, hence the varieties have equal dimension.
\end{solution}
