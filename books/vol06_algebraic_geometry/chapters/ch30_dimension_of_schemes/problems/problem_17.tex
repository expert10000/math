\begin{problem}[VI.30.P17: Finite morphisms]\label{prob:vi30-17}
Let $f:X\to Y$ be finite and surjective between affine schemes. Explain why $\dim X=\dim Y$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical bridge to finite morphisms.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Write $Y=\Spec A$ and $X=\Spec B$. Finiteness makes $B$ integral over the image of $A$; surjectivity rules out losing the relevant spectrum. The integral-extension dimension theorem gives equality of prime-chain dimensions.
\end{solution}
