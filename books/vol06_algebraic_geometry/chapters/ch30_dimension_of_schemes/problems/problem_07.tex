\begin{problem}[VI.30.P7: Noetherian irreducible components]\label{prob:vi30-07}
Let $X$ be Noetherian with irreducible components $X_1,\ldots,X_r$. Prove $\dim X=\max_i\dim X_i$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical Noetherian component theorem.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Every irreducible closed subset of $X$ is contained in some irreducible component. Along an ascending chain of irreducible closed subsets, the largest member lies in one $X_i$, so the entire chain lies there. Conversely each component contributes its chains to $X$.
\end{solution}
