\begin{problem}[VI.30.P7: Noetherian irreducible components]\label{prob:vi30-07}
Let \(X\) be Noetherian with irreducible components \(X_1,\ldots,X_r\). Prove \(\dim X=\max_i\dim X_i\).

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical Noetherian component theorem.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
A Noetherian space has finitely many irreducible components. Take a chain
\[
Z_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq Z_n
\]
of irreducible closed subsets of \(X\). The largest member \(Z_n\) is irreducible, so it lies in some irreducible
component \(X_i\). Then every \(Z_j\subseteq Z_n\) also lies in \(X_i\). Hence
\[
n\le\dim X_i\le\max_i\dim X_i.
\]
Taking the supremum over chains gives
\[
\dim X\le\max_i\dim X_i.
\]
The reverse inequality is immediate because each \(X_i\) is a closed subspace of \(X\). Therefore
\[
\boxed{\dim X=\max_i\dim X_i}.
\]
\end{solution}
\fi
