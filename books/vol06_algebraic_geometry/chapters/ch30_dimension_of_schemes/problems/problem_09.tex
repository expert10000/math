\begin{problem}[VI.30.P9: Normalization invariance]\label{prob:vi30-09}
Let $X$ be an integral scheme and $X^\nu\to X$ its normalization. Under the standard affine normalization construction, explain why dimension is unchanged.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical bridge from VI/28--VI/29.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
On $U=\Spec A$, normalization is $\Spec\overline A$ with $A\subseteq\overline A$ integral. VI/29 gives $\dim\overline A=\dim A$. These affine equalities control the dimensions of the corresponding components, so normalization preserves dimension.
\end{solution}
