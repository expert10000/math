\begin{problem}[VI.30.P3: Affine compatibility]\label{prob:vi30-03}
Prove directly that the global topological dimension of $\Spec A$ equals the Krull dimension of $A$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/30 development from the VI/29 affine chain dictionary.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Irreducible closed subsets of $\Spec A$ are exactly $V(\mathfrak p)$ for primes $\mathfrak p$, and inclusion reverses: $\mathfrak p\subsetneq\mathfrak q$ iff $V(\mathfrak q)\subsetneq V(\mathfrak p)$. Reversing chains preserves length, so the two suprema agree.
\end{solution}
