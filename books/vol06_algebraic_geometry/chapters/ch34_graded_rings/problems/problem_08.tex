\begin{problem}[VI.34.P8: Veronese quadratic cone]\label{prob:vi34-08}
Prove \(k[x,y]^{(2)}\cong k[u,v,w]/(uw-v^2)\) and interpret the affine spectrum.
\begin{solution}
Map \(u,v,w\) to \(x^2,xy,y^2\). These generate the Veronese ring and satisfy the single relation \(uw=v^2\). The spectrum is the quadratic cone in affine three-space.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
