\begin{problem}[VI.34.P3: Homogeneous primality]\label{prob:vi34-03}
Let \(\mathfrak p\) be homogeneous. Prove that it is prime if and only if it passes the prime test on homogeneous elements.
\begin{solution}
Reduce to \(S/\mathfrak p\). If nonzero \(f,g\) had zero product, their least-degree nonzero components would have nonzero product and would be the least-degree component of \(fg\), contradiction.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
