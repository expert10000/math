\begin{problem}[VI.34.P11: Degree-zero scaling invariance]\label{prob:vi34-11}
Let \(F/G\) be a homogeneous rational expression. Prove it is invariant under scaling exactly when numerator and denominator have equal degree.
\begin{solution}
If degrees are \(a,b\), scaling multiplies the ratio by \(\lambda^{a-b}\). This is invariant for all nonzero \(\lambda\) exactly when \(a=b\), modulo trivial exceptional field phenomena.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
