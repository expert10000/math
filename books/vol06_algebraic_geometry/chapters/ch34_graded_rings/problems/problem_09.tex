\begin{problem}[VI.34.P9: Weighted localization]\label{prob:vi34-09}
Let \(S=k[x,y]\) with \(\deg x=2,\deg y=3\). Describe \(S_{(x)}\) and \(S_{(y)}\).
\begin{solution}
A degree-zero monomial fraction over a power of \(x\) has even exponent of \(y\), so \(S_{(x)}=k[y^2/x^3]\). Similarly \(S_{(y)}=k[x^3/y^2]\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
