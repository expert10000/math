\begin{problem}[VI.34.P2: Homogeneous radicals]\label{prob:vi34-02}
Prove that \(\sqrt I\) is homogeneous whenever \(I\) is homogeneous.
\begin{solution}
Pass to \(S/I\). It suffices to show the nilradical of a graded ring is homogeneous. Compare an extreme nonzero homogeneous component in a sufficiently large power, then subtract and induct on the number of components.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
