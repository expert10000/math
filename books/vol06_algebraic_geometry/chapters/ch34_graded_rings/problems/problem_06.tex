\begin{problem}[VI.34.P6: Graded hypersurface sequence]\label{prob:vi34-06}
Let \(f\in S_d\) be homogeneous and a non-zero-divisor. Prove \(0\to S(-d)\xrightarrow{\cdot f}S\to S/(f)\to0\) is exact in graded modules.
\begin{solution}
The underlying sequence is exact. Since \(S(-d)_n=S_{n-d}\), multiplication by \(f\) carries degree \(n\) in the shifted module to \(S_n\), so all maps have degree zero.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
