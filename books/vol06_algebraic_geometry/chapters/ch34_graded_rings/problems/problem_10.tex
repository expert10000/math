\begin{problem}[VI.34.P10: Cone vertex and irrelevant ideal]\label{prob:vi34-10}
For standard graded \(S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]/I\) over a field with \(I\) homogeneous and proper, prove \(S_+\) cuts out the cone vertex.
\begin{solution}
The quotient \(S/S_+\) is \(k\), so \(S_+\) is maximal. It is the image of \((x_0,\ldots,x_n)\), hence corresponds to the origin of the cone.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
