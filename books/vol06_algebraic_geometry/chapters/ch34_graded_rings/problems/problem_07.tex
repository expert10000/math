\begin{problem}[VI.34.P7: Exact degree-zero localization]\label{prob:vi34-07}
Prove \(0\to M'_{(f)}\to M_{(f)}\to M''_{(f)}\to0\) for a short exact sequence of graded modules.
\begin{solution}
Localize first; ordinary localization is exact. The localized maps are degree zero, and exactness of graded modules can be read one degree at a time.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
