\begin{problem}[VI.34.P1: Homogeneous ideals from components]\label{prob:vi34-01}
Let \(S=\bigoplus S_n\) and \(I\subseteq S\). Prove the equivalence of the three conditions in the component criterion.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The three conditions are:
\[
\text{(i) }I\text{ is generated by homogeneous elements},
\]
\[
\text{(ii) every homogeneous component of every }f\in I\text{ lies in }I,
\]
and
\[
\text{(iii) }I=\bigoplus_n(I\cap S_n).
\]

Assume (i). Write \(f=\sum_\alpha a_\alpha h_\alpha\) with \(h_\alpha\) homogeneous. Decompose every
\(a_\alpha\) into homogeneous pieces. Then every product of a homogeneous component of \(a_\alpha\) with
\(h_\alpha\) is homogeneous and still belongs to \(I\). Grouping by total degree yields exactly the homogeneous
components of \(f\), proving (ii).

Assume (ii). Every \(f\in I\) decomposes as \(\sum f_n\) with \(f_n\in I\cap S_n\), so
\[
I\subseteq\sum_n(I\cap S_n).
\]
The reverse inclusion is obvious, and directness comes from the direct grading of \(S\). Hence (iii).

Assume (iii). Every element of \(I\) is a finite sum of elements from the sets \(I\cap S_n\), all of which are
homogeneous. Thus the homogeneous elements of \(I\) generate \(I\), proving (i).
\end{solution}
\fi
