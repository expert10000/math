\begin{problem}[Challenge 4: Projective graphs]\label{chal:vi38-04}
Let \(f:X\to\mathbb P^n_Y\) be a \(Y\)-morphism.  Prove that its graph \(X\to X\times_Y\mathbb P^n_Y\) is a closed immersion because \(\mathbb P^n_Y\to Y\) is separated, and investigate how graph embeddings can be combined with projective embeddings of \(X\to Y\).
\end{problem}
