\begin{problem}[Challenge 1: Universal base-locus criterion]\label{chal:vi38-01}
Let \(S\) be a finitely generated standard graded ring and \(F_0,\ldots,F_m\) homogeneous of one degree.  Formulate and prove a criterion for the associated map to \(\mathbb P^m\) to be everywhere defined using radicals of homogeneous ideals rather than pointwise coordinates.
\end{problem}
