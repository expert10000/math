\begin{problem}[VI.38.P8: Proj of a Segre product]\label{prob:vi38-08}
Prove \(\Proj(A\#B)\cong\Proj A\times\Proj B\) for polynomial rings \(A=k[x_0,\ldots,x_m]\), \(B=k[y_0,\ldots,y_n]\).
\begin{solution}
The standard opens \(D_+(x_i\otimes y_j)\) cover \(\Proj(A\#B)\).  Their coordinate rings are generated by the ratios \((x_a\otimes y_j)/(x_i\otimes y_j)=x_a/x_i\) and \((x_i\otimes y_b)/(x_i\otimes y_j)=y_b/y_j\), hence equal \(A_{(x_i)}\otimes B_{(y_j)}\).  These are exactly the coordinate rings of \(U_i\times V_j\); localization gives compatible overlap maps, so the charts glue to an isomorphism.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
