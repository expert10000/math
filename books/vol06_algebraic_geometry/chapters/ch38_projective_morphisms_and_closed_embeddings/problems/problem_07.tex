\begin{problem}[VI.38.P7: General Veronese embedding]\label{prob:vi38-07}
Give the complete ring-theoretic proof that the \(d\)-uple map \(\mathbb P^n\to\mathbb P^N\) is a closed immersion.
\begin{solution}
Let \(R\) have one degree-one variable for each degree-\(d\) monomial of \(S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]\).  Mapping those variables to the monomials gives a surjection \(R\twoheadrightarrow S^{(d)}\).  Therefore \(\Proj S^{(d)}\hookrightarrow\Proj R\) is a closed immersion.  The canonical Veronese isomorphism \(\Proj S\cong\Proj S^{(d)}\) identifies the composite with the \(d\)-uple coordinate formula.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
