\begin{problem}[VI.38.P1: Domain theorem for Proj]\label{prob:vi38-01}
Let \(\varphi:S\to T\) be degree preserving.  Prove carefully that the standard-open maps \(\Spec T_{(\varphi(f))}\to\Spec S_{(f)}\) glue on \(U_\varphi\).
\begin{solution}
For homogeneous \(f,g\in S_+\), the overlap is \(D_+(\varphi(fg))\).  Both chart maps restrict to the map on degree-zero localizations induced by \(S_{fg}\to T_{\varphi(fg)}\).  Localization in stages identifies these restrictions, so the affine morphisms glue.  Their point map is contraction, and every point of \(U_\varphi\) lies in at least one such chart.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
