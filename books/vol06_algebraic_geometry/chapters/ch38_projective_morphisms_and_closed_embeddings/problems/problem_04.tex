\begin{problem}[VI.38.P4: Morphism from same-degree forms]\label{prob:vi38-04}
Let \(F_0,\ldots,F_m\in k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]_d\).  Prove that if their projective common zero locus is empty, they define a morphism \(\mathbb P^n\to\mathbb P^m\).
\begin{solution}
Use the graded map \(k[y_0,\ldots,y_m]\to k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]^{(d)}\), \(y_i\mapsto F_i\).  The empty common zero locus says its Proj domain is all of \(\Proj k[x]^{(d)}\).  Veronese invariance identifies that source with \(\mathbb P^n\), and the point formula follows from contraction on standard opens.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
