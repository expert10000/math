\begin{problem}[VI.38.P9: Segre ideal]\label{prob:vi38-09}
Prove that the kernel of \(k[z_{ij}]\to A\#B\), \(z_{ij}\mapsto x_i\otimes y_j\), is generated by the \(2\times2\) minors.
\begin{solution}
The minors vanish.  Modulo them one may interchange the column indices attached to any two row indices.  Hence monomials with the same row-index multiset and column-index multiset are congruent.  The image of a monomial records exactly those two multisets.  Group any kernel polynomial by image monomial; coefficients in each group sum to zero, so each group is a combination of pairwise monomial differences, all generated by repeated swaps.  Thus the kernel is the minor ideal.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
