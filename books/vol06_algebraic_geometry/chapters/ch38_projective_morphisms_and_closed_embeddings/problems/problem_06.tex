\begin{problem}[VI.38.P6: Quadratic Veronese embedding]\label{prob:vi38-06}
Prove from graded rings that \(\nu_2:\mathbb P^1\to\mathbb P^2\), \([s:t]\mapsto[s^2:st:t^2]\), is a closed immersion and compute its ideal.
\begin{solution}
The map \(k[Y_0,Y_1,Y_2]\to k[s,t]^{(2)}\) is surjective.  Its kernel contains \(Y_0Y_2-Y_1^2\).  Every monomial in the image is represented uniquely modulo this relation by a monomial not containing both \(Y_0\) and \(Y_2\), which proves the kernel is principal.  Thus the image is the conic \(V_+(Y_0Y_2-Y_1^2)\), and the quotient map gives a closed immersion.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
