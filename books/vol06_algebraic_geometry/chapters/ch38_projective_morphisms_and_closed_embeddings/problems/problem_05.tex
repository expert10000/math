\begin{problem}[VI.38.P5: Relative projectivity survives base change]\label{prob:vi38-05}
Let \(X\to Y\) be projective and \(Y'\to Y\) arbitrary.  Prove directly from a chosen projective embedding that \(X_{Y'}\to Y'\) is projective.
\begin{solution}
Choose \(X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_Y\).  Base change gives a closed immersion \(X_{Y'}\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_Y\times_Y Y'\).  Relative projective space commutes with base change, so the target is \(\mathbb P^n_{Y'}\).  This is the required factorization.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
