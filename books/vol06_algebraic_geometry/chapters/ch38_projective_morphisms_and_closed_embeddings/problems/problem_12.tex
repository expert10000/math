\begin{problem}[VI.38.P12: Very ample twists on projective space]\label{prob:vi38-12}
Show that \(\OO_{\mathbb P^n}(d)\) is very ample for every \(d\ge1\).
\begin{solution}
The \(d\)-uple Veronese embedding \(\nu_d:\mathbb P^n\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^N\) is closed.  On standard overlaps, a degree-\(d\) monomial coordinate transforms by the \(d\)-th power of the standard \(\OO(1)\) transition factor.  Hence \(\nu_d^*\OO_{\mathbb P^N}(1)\cong\OO_{\mathbb P^n}(d)\), which is exactly very ampleness.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
