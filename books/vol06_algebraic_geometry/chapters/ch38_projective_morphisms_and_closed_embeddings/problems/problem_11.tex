\begin{problem}[VI.38.P11: Composition of projective morphisms]\label{prob:vi38-11}
Prove projective morphisms are closed under composition, explicitly identifying the relative projective ambient dimension.
\begin{solution}
If \(X\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^m_Y\) and \(Y\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^n_Z\), then \(\mathbb P^m_Y\) is closed in \(\mathbb P^m_Z\times_Z\mathbb P^n_Z\).  Relative Segre embeds this product into \(\mathbb P^N_Z\) with \(N=(m+1)(n+1)-1\).  The composite closed immersion of \(X\) into that space proves \(X\to Z\) projective.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
