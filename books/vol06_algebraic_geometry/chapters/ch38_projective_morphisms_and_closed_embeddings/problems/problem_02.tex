\begin{problem}[VI.38.P2: A map with a base point]\label{prob:vi38-02}
Analyze the map of graded rings \(k[a,b]\to k[x,y]^{(2)}\) given by \(a\mapsto x^2\), \(b\mapsto xy\).  Determine the induced projective domain and the pointwise formula.
\begin{solution}
The two forms vanish simultaneously at \([0:1]\), so the domain is \(\mathbb P^1\setminus\{[0:1]\}=D_+(x)\).  There the formula is \([x:y]\mapsto[x^2:xy]=[x:y]\).  At \([0:1]\) both coordinates vanish, reflecting contraction to the irrelevant ideal of \(k[a,b]\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
