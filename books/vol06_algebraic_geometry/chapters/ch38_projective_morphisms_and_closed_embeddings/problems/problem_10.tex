\begin{problem}[VI.38.P10: Segre quadric]\label{prob:vi38-10}
Identify \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) with a closed quadric in \(\mathbb P^3\) and compute its four standard product charts.
\begin{solution}
The coordinate matrix is \(\begin{pmatrix}Z_{00}&Z_{01}\\Z_{10}&Z_{11}\end{pmatrix}\), so the ideal is \((Z_{00}Z_{11}-Z_{01}Z_{10})\).  On \(D_+(Z_{ij})\), the ratios in the same column and row give one affine coordinate from each \(\mathbb P^1\), so every chart is \(\mathbb A^1\times\mathbb A^1\cong\mathbb A^2\).  These four charts cover the quadric.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
