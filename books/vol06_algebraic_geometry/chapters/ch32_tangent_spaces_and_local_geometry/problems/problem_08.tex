\begin{problem}[VI.32.P8: Node singularity]\label{prob:vi32-08}
Assume $\operatorname{char}k\ne2$. Prove the origin of $y^2=x^2(x+1)$ is singular and identify the two tangent-cone directions.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
The local dimension is $1$. Both first derivatives vanish at the origin, so the Zariski tangent space has dimension $2$. The degree-two initial form is $y^2-x^2=(y-x)(y+x)$, giving the two tangent lines $y=x$ and $y=-x$.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
