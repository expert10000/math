\begin{problem}[VI.32.P15: Local dimension and codimension bridge]\label{prob:vi32-15}
Let $Y\subseteq X$ be irreducible closed with generic point $\eta_Y$. Reconcile VI/31 with the present definition by proving $\operatorname{codim}(Y,X)=\dim_{\eta_Y}^{\mathrm{loc}}X$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
This is exactly VI/31: choose an affine chart at $\eta_Y$, corresponding to a prime $\mathfrak p$. Codimension is $\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)$, while the local-ring dimension is $\dim A_{\mathfrak p}=\operatorname{ht}(\mathfrak p)$.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
