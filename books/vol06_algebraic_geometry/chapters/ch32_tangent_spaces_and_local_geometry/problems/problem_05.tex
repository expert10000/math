\begin{problem}[VI.32.P5: Nakayama and embedding dimension]\label{prob:vi32-05}
For a Noetherian local ring, prove that $\dim_\kappa\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$ is the minimal number of generators of $\mathfrak m$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
If elements generate $\mathfrak m$, their classes generate the quotient. Conversely, if their classes span $\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2$, then $\mathfrak m=(a_1,\ldots,a_r)+\mathfrak m^2$. Nakayama applied to the finitely generated module $\mathfrak m$ gives $\mathfrak m=(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$. Minimality corresponds to linear independence.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
