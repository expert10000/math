\begin{problem}[VI.32.P19: Tangent dimensions in a product]\label{prob:vi32-19}
Compute the tangent space at the origin of $\mathbb A^m_k\times\mathbb A^n_k$ and relate its dimension to the factors.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
The product is $\mathbb A^{m+n}_k$. Its cotangent space has basis the $m+n$ coordinate classes and splits into the direct sum of the two factor cotangent spaces. Dualizing gives $T_{(0,0)}\cong k^m\oplus k^n$.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
