\begin{problem}[VI.32.P17: Regularity is intrinsic]\label{prob:vi32-17}
Explain why regularity does not depend on a chosen affine embedding of $X$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
Regularity is defined from the isomorphism class of the local ring $\OO_{X,x}$ and its maximal ideal. Different affine embeddings may give different presentations and Jacobian matrices, but they yield isomorphic local rings and hence the same Krull and embedding dimensions.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
