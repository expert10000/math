\begin{problem}[VI.32.P16: Cusp multiple lenses]\label{prob:vi32-16}
For $A=k[t^2,t^3]$, compute the local dimension and embedding dimension at $(t^2,t^3)$ and explain the normalization comparison.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
The ring is a one-dimensional domain, so the local dimension is $1$. The classes of $t^2$ and $t^3$ are independent modulo the square of the maximal ideal, so embedding dimension is $2$. The normalization $k[t]_{(t)}$ has dimension and embedding dimension both $1$, so normalization repairs the singular local ring.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
