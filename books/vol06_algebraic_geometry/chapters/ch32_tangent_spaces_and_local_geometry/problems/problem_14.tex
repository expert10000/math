\begin{problem}[VI.32.P14: Non-rational point over R]\label{prob:vi32-14}
For $x=(t^2+1)\in\Spec\mathbb R[t]$, compute $\kappa(x)$, local dimension, and embedding dimension.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
The residue field is $\mathbb R[t]/(t^2+1)\cong\mathbb C$. The maximal ideal has height $1$, so the local ring has dimension $1$. Since a one-dimensional regular local ring obtained by localizing the PID $\mathbb R[t]$ has principal maximal ideal, the cotangent space is one-dimensional over $\mathbb C$.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
