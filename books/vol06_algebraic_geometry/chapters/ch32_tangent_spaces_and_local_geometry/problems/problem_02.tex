\begin{problem}[VI.32.P2: AG8 Problem 3(a): failure for a general scheme]\label{prob:vi32-02}
Let
\[
X=\Spec k\sqcup\mathbb A^1_k
\]
and let \(x\) be the point of the first component. Show that \(x\) is closed but
\[
\dim\mathcal O_{X,x}\ne\dim X.
\]

\textbf{Provenance.} Legacy AG8 Problem 3(a) counterexample requirement; canonical destination VI/32.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Each component of a disjoint union is open and closed. Hence the unique point
\[
x\in\Spec k
\]
is closed in the first component and therefore closed in all of \(X\).

Because the component \(\Spec k\) is an open neighborhood of \(x\), the local ring is exactly
\[
\mathcal O_{X,x}\cong k.
\]
Thus
\[
\dim\mathcal O_{X,x}=0.
\]

On the other hand, dimension of a disjoint union is the supremum of component dimensions:
\[
\dim X
=
\max\{\dim\Spec k,\dim\mathbb A^1_k\}
=
\max\{0,1\}
=
1.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\dim\mathcal O_{X,x}=0\ne1=\dim X.
}
\]

This counterexample completes the AG8 Problem 3 routing: the open-subset failure was assigned to VI/30, the
dimension--codimension failure to VI/31, and this closed-point local-dimension failure belongs here in VI/32.  The
example also isolates the mechanism: global dimension is controlled by the largest-dimensional component, whereas a
local ring sees only the component(s) passing through the chosen point.
\end{solution}
\fi
