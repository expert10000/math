\begin{problem}[VI.32.P11: Nilpotent thickened line]\label{prob:vi32-11}
At the origin of $\Spec k[x,\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$, compute local and embedding dimensions.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
The nilpotent does not change prime chains, so local dimension is the same as for $k[x]$ at $(x)$, namely $1$. The maximal ideal is generated by $x,\varepsilon$, and neither class vanishes modulo its square, giving embedding dimension $2$. Thus the point is singular.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
