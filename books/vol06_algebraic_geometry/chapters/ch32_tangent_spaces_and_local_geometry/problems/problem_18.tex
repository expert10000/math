\begin{problem}[VI.32.P18: Regularity and reduction]\label{prob:vi32-18}
Give an example showing that reduction can change regularity even though it does not change Krull dimension.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\begin{solution}
Take the dual numbers. Its reduction is $\Spec k$, regular of local and embedding dimension $0$. The original scheme has the same local Krull dimension $0$ but embedding dimension $1$, so it is singular.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
