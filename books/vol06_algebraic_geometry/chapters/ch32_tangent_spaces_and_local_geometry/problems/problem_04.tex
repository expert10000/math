\begin{problem}[VI.32.P4: Cotangent space of affine space]\label{prob:vi32-04}
Compute \(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2\) at the origin of \(\mathbb A^n_k\).

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical VI/32 synthesis.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
At the origin,
\[
R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb A^n,0}
=
k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]_{(x_1,\ldots,x_n)}
\]
and
\[
\mathfrak m=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)R.
\]
Every element of \(\mathfrak m\) has, modulo \(\mathfrak m^2\), a unique linear part
\[
a_1x_1+\cdots+a_nx_n.
\]
Indeed terms of total degree at least \(2\) lie in \(\mathfrak m^2\), and denominators that are units at the origin
reduce to nonzero constants modulo \(\mathfrak m\). Thus the classes of
\[
x_1,\ldots,x_n
\]
span \(\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2\).

They are linearly independent: if
\[
a_1x_1+\cdots+a_nx_n\in\mathfrak m^2,
\]
then the left side has zero linear term only when all \(a_i=0\). Hence
\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak m/\mathfrak m^2
\cong
k^n,
}
\]
with basis \(\bar x_1,\ldots,\bar x_n\). Consequently the tangent space is its dual and also has dimension \(n\).
\end{solution}
\fi
