\begin{problem}[Legacy AG3 Problem 14: Gluing schemes]\label{prob:vi20-p01}
Let $\{X_i\}_{i\in I}$ be schemes.  For each $i\ne j$, let $U_{ij}\subseteq X_i$ be open and let
$\varphi_{ij}:U_{ij}\xrightarrow{\sim}U_{ji}$ be an isomorphism.  Assume
$\varphi_{ji}=\varphi_{ij}^{-1}$ and the full triple-overlap compatibility/cocycle conditions.
Construct a scheme $X$ with open embeddings $\psi_i:X_i\to X$ whose images cover $X$, satisfy
$\psi_i(X_i)\cap\psi_j(X_j)=\psi_i(U_{ij})$, and obey $\psi_i=\psi_j\circ\varphi_{ij}$ on $U_{ij}$.
Prove the glued scheme is unique up to unique isomorphism compatible with the charts.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the foundational local-to-global construction for schemes and the unique AG3 gluing problem assigned to VI/20.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Build the quotient set first, give it the final topology for the chart maps, then define the structure sheaf as compatible families of local sections.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Adjoin $U_{ii}=X_i$ and identity transitions.
\item Use inverse and cocycle laws to prove the relation is an equivalence relation.
\item Compute inverse images of chart images to prove they are open.
\item Define $\OO_X(V)$ as compatible families $(s_i)$.
\item Identify stalks with chart stalks and prove uniqueness by gluing chart identities.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Put $U_{ii}=X_i$ and $\varphi_{ii}=\operatorname{id}$.  On $Y=\coprod_iX_i$, declare $x\in X_i$ equivalent to $\varphi_{ij}(x)\in X_j$ whenever $x\in U_{ij}$.  Identity gives reflexivity, $\varphi_{ji}=\varphi_{ij}^{-1}$ gives symmetry, and the cocycle gives transitivity.  Let $X=Y/{\sim}$ and $\psi_i:X_i\to X$ be the quotient maps.

Declare $V\subseteq X$ open iff $\psi_i^{-1}(V)$ is open for all $i$.  Then
\[
\psi_j^{-1}(\psi_i(X_i))=U_{ji},
\]
so each chart image is open.  The cocycle prevents distinct points inside one $X_i$ from being identified, hence $\psi_i$ is injective; the final topology makes it a homeomorphism onto its image.  A class lies in both chart images exactly when it comes from $U_{ij}$, giving
\[
\psi_i(X_i)\cap\psi_j(X_j)=\psi_i(U_{ij})=\psi_j(U_{ji}).
\]

For open $V\subseteq X$, write $V_i=\psi_i^{-1}(V)$ and define $\OO_X(V)$ to be the ring of families $(s_i)$ with $s_i\in\OO_{X_i}(V_i)$ satisfying
\[
s_i|_{V_i\cap U_{ij}}=\varphi_{ij}^{\sharp}(s_j|_{V_j\cap U_{ji}})
\]
for all $i,j$.  Restrictions are componentwise.  The sheaf axiom holds componentwise because each $\OO_{X_i}$ is a sheaf; the cocycle guarantees the overlap compatibilities are coherent.  Restricting to $\psi_i(X_i)$ recovers $\OO_{X_i}$, so $\psi_i$ is an isomorphism of ringed spaces onto an open subspace.

At $x=\psi_i(x_i)$ we have $\OO_{X,x}\cong\OO_{X_i,x_i}$, hence the glued stalk is local.  Therefore $(X,\OO_X)$ is locally ringed.  Since the open chart images cover $X$ and each is a scheme, $X$ is a scheme.

If $X'$ is another gluing, the chart identifications $\psi_i(X_i)\to\psi_i'(X_i)$ agree on overlaps by the prescribed transition maps.  They glue to a unique isomorphism $X\to X'$, whose inverse is obtained similarly.  Thus the gluing is unique up to unique chart-compatible isomorphism.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The topology and the functions are glued in parallel; local rings then come for free from the original charts.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Derive the mapping property for compatible families $X_i\to Y$ and reconstruct an arbitrary scheme from an affine open cover.
