\begin{problem}[Reconstruct a scheme from an affine cover]\label{prob:vi20-p05}
Prove every scheme is canonically recovered by gluing the members of any affine open cover along their intersections.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This makes the slogan 'schemes are glued affine schemes' precise.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use identity transitions on literal intersections.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Use identity transitions on literal intersections.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Take $U_{ij}=U_i\cap U_j$ and identity transition maps.  The original inclusions $U_i\hookrightarrow X$ form a compatible family.  By P1 and its uniqueness/mapping property they induce a map from the glued scheme to $X$, which is an isomorphism on the open cover, hence an isomorphism globally.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
An affine atlas is complete reconstruction data.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Replace the affine cover by any open cover.
