\begin{problem}[Associativity of finite gluing]\label{prob:vi20-p10}
Show that gluing three charts in two stages gives a scheme uniquely isomorphic to the one-step gluing, provided the original cocycle data are respected.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Staged constructions should not depend on arbitrary parenthesization.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the mapping property/uniqueness of P1.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Use the mapping property/uniqueness of P1.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Both staged and one-step constructions contain the same three charts as open subschemes with the same prescribed overlap identifications.  By uniqueness in P1 there is a unique isomorphism compatible with every chart.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The gluing theorem is canonical enough to ignore construction order.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Extend to finite families.
