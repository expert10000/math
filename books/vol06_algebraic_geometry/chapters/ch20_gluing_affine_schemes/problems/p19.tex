\begin{problem}[Gluing is local on the source]\label{prob:vi20-p19}
Prove a morphism from a glued scheme is uniquely determined by its restrictions to any open cover refining the original charts.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is a flexible local-to-global principle for later chapters.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Refine the chart cover and use sheaf uniqueness.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Refine the chart cover and use sheaf uniqueness.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
If two morphisms agree on the refined cover, they agree on every original chart because those are covered by refinement members.  Hence they agree globally by the gluing mapping property.  Compatible morphisms on the refinement first glue on each original chart and then globally.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Morphisms can be constructed and compared on arbitrarily fine affine neighborhoods.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use P2 to work only with common distinguished opens.
