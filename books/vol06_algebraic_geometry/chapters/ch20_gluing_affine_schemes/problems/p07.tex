\begin{problem}[Doubled-origin line]\label{prob:vi20-p07}
Construct the doubled-origin line by gluing two copies of $\A^1_k$ along $D(t)$ by the identity; compute its global sections and prove it is not affine.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the standard minimal example showing that affine charts can glue to a non-affine scheme.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the equalizer $k[t]\times k[t]\rightrightarrows k[t,t^{-1}]$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Use the equalizer $k[t]\times k[t]\rightrightarrows k[t,t^{-1}]$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The quotient identifies all nonzero points but not the two origins.  Global compatibility forces two polynomials to agree in the localization, hence already in $k[t]$, so $\Gamma(X,\OO_X)\cong k[t]$.  If $X$ were affine, affine reconstruction would give $X\cong\Spec k[t]$, contradicting the two distinct origins.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Global sections do not reconstruct non-affine schemes.
