\begin{problem}[Doubled-origin line]\label{prob:vi20-p07}
Construct the doubled-origin line by gluing two copies of $\A^1_k$ along $D(t)$ by the identity; compute its global sections and prove it is not affine.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the standard minimal example showing that affine charts can glue to a non-affine scheme.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the equalizer $k[t]\times k[t]\rightrightarrows k[t,t^{-1}]$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Use the equalizer $k[t]\times k[t]\rightrightarrows k[t,t^{-1}]$.
\end{enumerate}

% VI20_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINEMENT P07
\begin{solution}
Let $X_1=X_2=\Spec k[t]$ and glue $D(t)\subseteq X_1$ to $D(t)\subseteq X_2$ by the identity.  Every point belonging to $D(t)$ is identified across the two charts.  The two primes $(t)$, however, lie outside the overlap and remain distinct; call their images $0_1$ and $0_2$.  Hence the quotient is the affine line with two origins.

For global sections the sheaf equalizer gives
\[
\Gamma(X,\OO_X)
=\{(p,q)\in k[t]\times k[t]:p=q\text{ in }k[t,t^{-1}]\}.
\]
Since $k[t]\hookrightarrow k[t,t^{-1}]$ is injective, compatibility forces $p=q$.  Therefore
\[
\Gamma(X,\OO_X)\cong k[t].
\]
If $X$ were affine, affine reconstruction would give
\[
X\cong\Spec\Gamma(X,\OO_X)\cong\Spec k[t].
\]
But $X$ has the two distinct points $0_1$ and $0_2$ mapping to the single origin of $\Spec k[t]$.  Thus no such isomorphism can exist, and the doubled-origin line is not affine.
\end{solution}


\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Global sections do not reconstruct non-affine schemes.
