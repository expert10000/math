\begin{problem}[Chart-independent local rings]\label{prob:vi20-p13}
If a glued point is represented by $x_i\in X_i$ and $x_j\in X_j$, prove the transition map identifies the two chart local rings.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
A glued point must have intrinsic local algebra independent of its representative.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Localize the sheaf isomorphism induced by $\varphi_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Localize the sheaf isomorphism induced by $\varphi_{ij}$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Because $x_i\in U_{ij}$ and $x_j=\varphi_{ij}(x_i)$, the scheme isomorphism $\varphi_{ij}$ induces a local-ring isomorphism $\OO_{X_j,x_j}\to\OO_{X_i,x_i}$.  Both identify with the glued stalk $\OO_{X,x}$.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Cocycle compatibility applies to local rings as well as points.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Check the induced residue-field isomorphisms.
