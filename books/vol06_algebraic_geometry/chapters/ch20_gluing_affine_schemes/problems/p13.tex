\begin{problem}[Chart-independent local rings]\label{prob:vi20-p13}
If a glued point is represented by $x_i\in X_i$ and $x_j\in X_j$, prove the transition map identifies the two chart local rings.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
A glued point must have intrinsic local algebra independent of its representative.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Localize the sheaf isomorphism induced by $\varphi_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Localize the sheaf isomorphism induced by $\varphi_{ij}$.
\end{enumerate}

% VI20_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINEMENT P13
\begin{solution}
Let a point $x\in X$ be represented by $x_i\in X_i$ and $x_j\in X_j$.  Equality of their quotient classes means $x_i\in U_{ij}$ and
\[
x_j=\varphi_{ij}(x_i).
\]
Because $\varphi_{ij}:U_{ij}\xrightarrow{\sim}U_{ji}$ is an isomorphism of schemes, it induces an isomorphism on stalks
\[
\varphi_{ij,x_i}^{\sharp}:\OO_{X_j,x_j}\xrightarrow{\sim}\OO_{X_i,x_i}.
\]
This is an isomorphism of local rings: it carries the maximal ideal of one stalk onto the maximal ideal of the other.

On the other hand, the chart embeddings identify the glued sheaf locally with each chart sheaf, giving canonical isomorphisms
\[
\OO_{X,x}\cong\OO_{X_i,x_i},\qquad
\OO_{X,x}\cong\OO_{X_j,x_j}.
\]
The compatibility condition used to define the glued sheaf makes the composite of these two identifications exactly the stalk map induced by $\varphi_{ij}$.  Therefore the local ring of the glued point is independent of the chart representative, and the transition map supplies the canonical comparison.
\end{solution}


\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Cocycle compatibility applies to local rings as well as points.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Check the induced residue-field isomorphisms.
