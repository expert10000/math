\begin{problem}[Gluing along the whole chart]\label{prob:vi20-p17}
If $\varphi:X_1\to X_2$ is an isomorphism, glue along $U_{12}=X_1$, $U_{21}=X_2$ and identify the result.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This checks the opposite extreme boundary case.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Every point and every local function is identified by $\varphi$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every point and every local function is identified by $\varphi$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
The quotient map from either chart is bijective onto all of $X$ and is an isomorphism of ringed spaces by construction.  Thus the gluing is canonically isomorphic to $X_1$ and $X_2$.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Full-overlap gluing forgets the duplicate chart.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use this to justify redundant-chart invariance.
