\begin{problem}[Affine atlas as localization data]\label{prob:vi20-p20}
Show an affine atlas may be refined so every transition is described locally by isomorphisms between localizations $A_f\cong B_g$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This packages P2, VI/18, and VI/19 into a computational atlas formalism.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Refine every pairwise overlap by P2.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Refine every pairwise overlap by P2.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
For chart rings $A_i,A_j$, P2 covers $U_i\cap U_j$ by $W=D(f)\subseteq U_i=D(g)\subseteq U_j$.  VI/18 identifies $W$ with both $\Spec(A_i)_f$ and $\Spec(A_j)_g$; VI/19 translates the transition isomorphism into an isomorphism of those localized rings.  These local isomorphisms satisfy induced cocycle relations on further refinements.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
General scheme atlases can be made algebraically explicit chart by chart.
