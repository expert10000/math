\begin{problem}[Gluing compatible morphisms to an affine target]\label{prob:vi20-p14}
Let $X$ be glued from $X_i$ and let $Y=\Spec A$. Show compatible maps $X_i\to Y$ correspond to ring maps $A\to\Gamma(X_i,\OO)$ compatible under overlap restriction.
\end{problem}

\begin{solution}
Let $Y=\Spec A$.  By the universal property of an affine target, a morphism
\[
f_i:X_i\longrightarrow Y
\]
is equivalent to a ring homomorphism
\[
\alpha_i:A\longrightarrow\Gamma(X_i,\OO_{X_i}).
\]
On an overlap $U_{ij}$, the two chart morphisms agree exactly when their pullbacks of every $a\in A$ agree after restriction, with the transition identification inserted.  Algebraically this says the two composites
\[
A\xrightarrow{\alpha_i}\Gamma(X_i,\OO)\longrightarrow\Gamma(U_{ij},\OO)
\]
and
\[
A\xrightarrow{\alpha_j}\Gamma(X_j,\OO)\longrightarrow\Gamma(U_{ji},\OO)
\xrightarrow{\varphi_{ij}^{\sharp}}\Gamma(U_{ij},\OO)
\]
are equal.

Thus compatible families of chart morphisms are exactly compatible families of ring maps on global chart sections.  By the gluing mapping property, every such compatible family of morphisms $f_i$ glues to a unique morphism $f:X\to Y$, and restriction recovers the original $f_i$.  For an affine target, therefore, morphism gluing is controlled entirely by compatibility of pullbacks of global affine coordinates.
\end{solution}
