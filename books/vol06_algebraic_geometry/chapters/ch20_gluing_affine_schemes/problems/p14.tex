\begin{problem}[Gluing compatible morphisms to an affine target]\label{prob:vi20-p14}
Let $X$ be glued from $X_i$ and let $Y=\Spec A$. Show compatible maps $X_i\to Y$ correspond to ring maps $A\to\Gamma(X_i,\OO)$ compatible under overlap restriction.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This combines VI/19's affine universal property with VI/20 gluing.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Translate each chart map by VI.19.P1, then impose agreement on overlaps.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Translate each chart map by VI.19.P1, then impose agreement on overlaps.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
A chart map corresponds to $A\to\Gamma(X_i,\OO)$.  Two chart maps agree on an overlap iff the two induced maps to its section ring agree.  Hence compatible ring maps give compatible chart morphisms, which glue uniquely to $X\to Y$ by the mapping property.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Affine targets turn geometric gluing into equalizer algebra.
