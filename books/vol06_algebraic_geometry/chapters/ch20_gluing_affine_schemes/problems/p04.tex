\begin{problem}[Universal mapping property of a gluing]\label{prob:vi20-p04}
Prove $\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y)$ is the set of compatible families $f_i:X_i\to Y$ for a glued scheme $X$.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the most efficient uniqueness and morphism-gluing principle.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Glue point maps and sheaf maps on the open chart cover.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Glue point maps and sheaf maps on the open chart cover.
\end{enumerate}

% VI20_FULL_SOLUTION_REFINEMENT P04
\begin{solution}
Let $X$ be the gluing of the $X_i$.  Restriction of any morphism $f:X\to Y$ to the open chart images gives morphisms $f_i:X_i\to Y$.  On $U_{ij}$ the two chart inclusions represent the same open subset of $X$, so the restrictions satisfy
\[
f_i|_{U_{ij}}=f_j\circ\varphi_{ij}.
\]
Thus restriction gives a map from $\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y)$ to compatible families.

Conversely, suppose morphisms $f_i:X_i\to Y$ satisfy these overlap identities.  Define the point map by
\[
f(\psi_i(x))=f_i(x).
\]
If $\psi_i(x)=\psi_j(y)$, then $y=\varphi_{ij}(x)$, and compatibility gives $f_j(y)=f_i(x)$, so the point map is well defined.  It is continuous because its restriction to every open chart image is the continuous map $f_i$, and those chart images cover $X$.

The pullback maps $f_i^\sharp$ on structure sheaves agree on the overlaps, again by compatibility.  Sheaf morphisms therefore glue uniquely to a morphism
\[
f^\sharp:\OO_Y\longrightarrow f_*\OO_X.
\]
At a point of $X$, choose a chart containing it.  The induced stalk map is the stalk map of the corresponding $f_i$, hence is local.  Thus $f$ is a scheme morphism.  Uniqueness follows because a morphism is determined by its continuous map and sheaf morphism on an open cover.  These two constructions are inverse, proving the stated mapping property.
\end{solution}


\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Maps out of a glued object are determined locally.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use it to prove associativity of finite gluing.
