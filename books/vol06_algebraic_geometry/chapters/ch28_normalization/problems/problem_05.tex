\begin{problem}[VI.28.P5: Normality iff normalization is trivial]\label{prob:vi28-05}
Show that an integral scheme \(X\) is normal if and only if its normalization morphism \(X^\nu\to X\) is an isomorphism.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Assume \(X\) is normal. On every affine open \(U=\Spec A\), the domain \(A\) is integrally closed in its fraction field, so
\[
\overline A=A.
\]
Hence the normalization restricts to the identity on every affine chart. These local identities glue, so \(\nu:X^\nu\to X\) is an isomorphism.

Conversely, suppose \(\nu\) is an isomorphism. The scheme \(X^\nu\) is normal by construction. Normality is invariant under isomorphism, so \(X\) is normal. Equivalently, on each affine chart the isomorphism identifies \(A\) with its integral closure \(\overline A\), proving \(A\) integrally closed.
\end{solution}
\fi
