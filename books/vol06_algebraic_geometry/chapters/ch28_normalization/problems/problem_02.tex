\begin{problem}[VI.28.P2: AG7 T05 reconstruction: localization, gluing, and global normalization]\label{prob:vi28-02}
Let \(X\) be integral. Normalize every affine chart \(U_i=\Spec A_i\). Using compatibility of integral closure with localization and common distinguished refinements of overlaps, prove that the normalized affine charts glue to a normal integral scheme \(X^\nu\) with a morphism \(\nu:X^\nu\to X\) independent of the chosen cover.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical reconstruction of AG7 T05 global normalization theory; no numbered legacy Problem 8 block is present in the audited source snapshot.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
For each affine chart \(U_i=\Spec A_i\), let
\[
U_i^\nu=\Spec\overline{A_i},
\]
where the closure is taken in the common field \(K(X)\).

Consider an overlap \(U_i\cap U_j\). By the common distinguished-refinement theorem, every point of the overlap has a neighborhood that is simultaneously
\[
D(f)\subseteq U_i,\qquad D(g)\subseteq U_j.
\]
The two coordinate rings are canonically isomorphic:
\[
(A_i)_f\cong(A_j)_g.
\]
Integral closure commutes with localization, so
\[
(\overline{A_i})_f
=
\overline{(A_i)_f}
\cong
\overline{(A_j)_g}
=
(\overline{A_j})_g.
\]
Thus the normalized charts have canonical isomorphisms on a basis of the overlaps. On triple overlaps these maps agree because all are induced inside the same function field and commute with further localization. Hence the cocycle condition holds, and VI/20 gluing produces \(X^\nu\).

The affine inclusions \(A_i\hookrightarrow\overline{A_i}\) are compatible on overlaps and glue to \(\nu:X^\nu\to X\). Normality and integrality are affine-local, so \(X^\nu\) is normal integral.

If a second affine cover is chosen, refine both covers by common affine intersections. The normalized restrictions coincide by the same localization identity; therefore the two glued normalizations are canonically isomorphic over \(X\).
\end{solution}
