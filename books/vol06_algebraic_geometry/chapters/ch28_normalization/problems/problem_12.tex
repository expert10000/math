\begin{problem}[VI.28.P12: UFDs give normal schemes]\label{prob:vi28-12}
Show that if $A$ is a UFD then $\Spec A$ is normal.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical bridge to standard algebra examples.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
A UFD is integrally closed in its fraction field: if a reduced fraction $a/b$ is integral, a monic relation implies $b$ divides a suitable power of $a$, and unique factorization forces $b$ to be a unit.  Therefore $A$ is normal and so is its spectrum.
\end{solution}
