\begin{problem}[VI.28.P6: Normalization is idempotent]\label{prob:vi28-06}
Prove $(X^\nu)^\nu\cong X^\nu$ canonically.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The scheme $X^\nu$ is normal.  By the previous criterion its own normalization morphism is an isomorphism.  The isomorphism is canonical because both constructions satisfy the same universal property.
\end{solution}
