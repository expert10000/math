\begin{problem}[VI.28.P6: Normalization is idempotent]\label{prob:vi28-06}
Prove \((X^\nu)^\nu\cong X^\nu\) canonically.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The first normalization \(X^\nu\) is a normal integral scheme. By Problem~5, the normalization morphism of a normal integral scheme is an isomorphism. Hence
\[
(X^\nu)^\nu\cong X^\nu.
\]
The isomorphism is canonical: on an affine normalized chart
\[
\Spec\overline A,
\]
the second normalization uses the integral closure of \(\overline A\) in
\(\Frac(\overline A)=K(X)\), which is \(\overline A\) itself. Thus the affine isomorphisms are literally induced by identity ring maps and glue without choices.
\end{solution}
\fi
