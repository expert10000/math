\begin{problem}[VI.28.P4: Normalize the cusp]\label{prob:vi28-04}
Compute the normalization of $A=k[x,y]/(y^2-x^3)$ by identifying it with $k[t^2,t^3]\subset k[t]$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical cusp computation derived from the AG7 normalization theory.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Send $x\mapsto t^2$ and $y\mapsto t^3$.  The image is $k[t^2,t^3]$ and its fraction field is $k(t)$.  The element $t=y/x$ in the fraction field satisfies $T^2-x=0$, so it is integral over $A$ but is not in $A$.  Adjoining $t$ gives $k[t]$, which is a PID and therefore integrally closed.  Hence $k[t]$ is the integral closure and $\mathbb A^1_k\to V(y^2-x^3)$ is the normalization.
\end{solution}
