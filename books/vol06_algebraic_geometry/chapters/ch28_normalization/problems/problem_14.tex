\begin{problem}[VI.28.P14: Finite normalization over a field]\label{prob:vi28-14}
Let $X$ be integral and finite type over a field $k$.  Explain why $X^\nu\to X$ is finite.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical finite-type consequence.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
On an affine chart $U=\Spec A$, the ring $A$ is a finitely generated $k$-domain.  The finiteness theorem for integral closure says its integral closure $\overline A$ in $\Frac(A)$ is a finite $A$-module.  Therefore $\Spec\overline A\to\Spec A$ is finite.  Finiteness is affine-local on the target, so the global normalization morphism is finite.
\end{solution}
