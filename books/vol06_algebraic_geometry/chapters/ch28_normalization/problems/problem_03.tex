\begin{problem}[VI.28.P3: Universal property of normalization]\label{prob:vi28-03}
Prove that a dominant morphism $f:Z\to X$ from a normal integral scheme factors uniquely through $X^\nu$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical consequence of the AG7 normalization stream.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
On affine $U=\Spec A\subseteq X$ and affine $W=\Spec B\subseteq f^{-1}(U)$, dominance embeds $K(X)$ into $K(Z)$.  If $z\in\overline A$, its image is integral over the image of $A$, hence integral over $B$ because the same monic equation has coefficients in $B$.  Since $B$ is integrally closed in $K(Z)$, that image lies in $B$.  Thus $A\to B$ extends to $\overline A\to B$.  These maps agree on overlaps and glue.  Uniqueness follows because two factorizations agree at the generic point and hence on affine coordinate rings inside the common function field.
\end{solution}
