\begin{problem}[VI.28.P19: Normalization and arbitrary base change]\label{prob:vi28-19}
Why can one not assert $(X^\nu)_{S'}\cong (X_{S'})^\nu$ for arbitrary base change without hypotheses?

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical warning dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
If base change destroys normality, then $(X^\nu)_{S'}$ need not be normal, whereas $(X_{S'})^\nu$ is normal by construction when normalization is defined on the relevant integral component.  Hence the two operations cannot agree in general.  Additional hypotheses are needed for compatibility.
\end{solution}
