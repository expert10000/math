\begin{problem}[VI.28.P7: Restriction to a nonempty open]\label{prob:vi28-07}
For nonempty open $U\subseteq X$, prove that $\nu^{-1}(U)\to U$ is the normalization of $U$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
It is enough to work on distinguished affine opens.  If $X=\Spec A$ and $U=D(f)$, then $\nu^{-1}(U)=\Spec(\overline A)_f$, while the normalization of $U=\Spec A_f$ is $\Spec\overline{A_f}$.  Localization compatibility identifies the two.  General opens follow by gluing.
\end{solution}
