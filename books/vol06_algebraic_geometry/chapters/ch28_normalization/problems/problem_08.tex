\begin{problem}[VI.28.P8: Local rings upstairs]\label{prob:vi28-08}
Let $y\in X^\nu$ lie over $x\in X$.  Explain how $\OO_{X^\nu,y}$ sits inside $K(X)$ and why it is integral over the image of $\OO_{X,x}$.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Choose an affine chart $X=\Spec A$ near $x$, with $X^\nu=\Spec\overline A$ above it.  If $y$ corresponds to $\mathfrak q\subset\overline A$ over $\mathfrak p\subset A$, then $\OO_{X^\nu,y}=(\overline A)_{\mathfrak q}\subset K(X)$.  Localization preserves integrality, so this local ring is integral over $A_{\mathfrak p}=\OO_{X,x}$.
\end{solution}
