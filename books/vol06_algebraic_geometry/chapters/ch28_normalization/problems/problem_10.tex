\begin{problem}[VI.28.P10: Birationality of normalization]\label{prob:vi28-10}
Assume $X$ is an integral finite-type scheme over a field.  Using the definition that two integral schemes are birational when they contain isomorphic nonempty dense opens, show that the normalization $X^\nu\to X$ is birational.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier; the later birational-geometry arc develops this language further.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The integral closure is finite over each affine coordinate ring $A$.  Choose finitely many $A$-module generators $b_i\in\overline A\subset\Frac(A)$ and a common nonzero denominator $f\in A$ with $fb_i\in A$ for all $i$.  Then $(\overline A)_f=A_f$, so the normalization is an isomorphism over the nonempty dense open $D(f)$.  These affine dense opens give the required birationality; equivalently, the function fields are canonically equal.
\end{solution}
