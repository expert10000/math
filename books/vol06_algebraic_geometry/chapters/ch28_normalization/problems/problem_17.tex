\begin{problem}[VI.28.P17: Normalization versus reduction]\label{prob:vi28-17}
Compare reduction and normalization and give an example showing they are different operations.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical boundary dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
Reduction replaces $A$ by $A/\sqrt{0}$ and keeps the same underlying topological space.  Normalization of an integral domain replaces $A$ by its integral closure in its fraction field and may change fibers over singular points.  For the cusp, $A=k[t^2,t^3]$ is already reduced, so reduction does nothing, while normalization changes it to $k[t]$.
\end{solution}
