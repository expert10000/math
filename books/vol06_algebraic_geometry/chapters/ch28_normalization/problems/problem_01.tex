\begin{problem}[VI.28.P1: AG7 T05 reconstruction: affine normalization]\label{prob:vi28-01}
Let $A$ be a domain, $K=\Frac(A)$, and $\overline A$ the integral closure of $A$ in $K$.  Prove that $\Spec\overline A\to\Spec A$ is integral, dominant, surjective, normal on the source, and induces an isomorphism of function fields.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical reconstruction of the affine normalization construction in AG7 T05; no numbered legacy Problem 8 block is claimed.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
The extension $A\subseteq\overline A$ is integral by definition of integral closure, so the induced morphism is integral.  The inclusion has zero kernel, hence the morphism is dominant.  Lying over for integral extensions makes it surjective.  By construction $\overline A$ is integrally closed in $K$, so the source is normal.  Finally $A\subseteq\overline A\subseteq K$ and every element of $\overline A$ already lies in $K$, while $A$ supplies the same nonzero denominators; hence $\Frac(\overline A)=K=\Frac(A)$.
\end{solution}
