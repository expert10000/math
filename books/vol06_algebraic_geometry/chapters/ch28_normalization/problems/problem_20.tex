\begin{problem}[VI.28.P20: Boundary to dimension and divisors]\label{prob:vi28-20}
State precisely what VI/28 establishes that later dimension and divisor chapters may use.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical boundary dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
For an integral scheme, VI/28 supplies a canonical normal integral model $X^\nu$ with the same function field, an integral surjective birational normalization morphism, compatibility with restriction to opens, and finiteness in the finite-type-over-a-field case.  VI/28 does not yet prove height-one valuation criteria, codimension formulas, or divisor-class statements.
\end{solution}
