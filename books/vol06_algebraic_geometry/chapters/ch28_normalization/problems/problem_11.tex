\begin{problem}[VI.28.P11: Normality is affine-local]\label{prob:vi28-11}
Assume an integral scheme admits an affine open cover by spectra of normal domains. Prove every local ring is an integrally closed domain.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Let \(x\in X\). Choose a normal affine chart \(U=\Spec A\) containing \(x\), with
\(x\leftrightarrow\mathfrak p\). Then
\[
\mathcal O_{X,x}=A_{\mathfrak p}.
\]
Since \(A\) is a normal domain, it is integrally closed in \(\Frac(A)\). Localization preserves integral closedness, so
\[
A_{\mathfrak p}
\]
is integrally closed in its own fraction field, which is the same \(\Frac(A)\). Therefore every local ring of \(X\) is an integrally closed domain, and \(X\) is normal.

The converse affine-local statement is immediate from the definition: if all local rings are integrally closed domains, then every affine coordinate ring is normal by the standard affine criterion.
\end{solution}
\fi
