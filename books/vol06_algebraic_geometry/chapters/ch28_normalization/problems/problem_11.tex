\begin{problem}[VI.28.P11: Normality is affine-local]\label{prob:vi28-11}
Assume an integral scheme admits an affine open cover by spectra of normal domains.  Prove every local ring is an integrally closed domain.

\textbf{Provenance.} Canonical dossier.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
For $x\in X$, choose a normal affine chart $U=\Spec A$ containing $x$, corresponding to $\mathfrak p\in\Spec A$.  Then $\OO_{X,x}=A_{\mathfrak p}$.  Localization of an integrally closed domain is integrally closed, so every local ring is normal.
\end{solution}
