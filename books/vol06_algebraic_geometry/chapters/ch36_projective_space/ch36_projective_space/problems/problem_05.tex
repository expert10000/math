\begin{problem}[VI.36.P5: A closed point over a nonclosed field]\label{prob:vi36-05}
For an irreducible polynomial \(f(t)\in k[t]\) of degree \(d>1\), construct the corresponding closed point of \(\mathbb P^1_k\) in the chart \(U_0\) and compute its residue field.
\begin{solution}
The maximal ideal \((f)\subset k[t]\) defines a closed point of \(U_0\cong\mathbb A^1_k\), hence of \(\mathbb P^1_k\).  Its residue field is \(k[t]/(f)\), a degree-\(d\) field extension of \(k\).  Homogenizing \(f\) yields a homogeneous polynomial defining the same projective point on that chart.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
