\begin{problem}[VI.36.P12: Why projective space is not affine]\label{prob:vi36-12}
For \(n>0\), use global sections and dimension to prove that \(\mathbb P^n_k\) is not affine.
\begin{solution}
If \(\mathbb P^n_k\) were affine, the affine recovery theorem would give \(\mathbb P^n_k\cong\Spec\Gamma(\mathbb P^n,\mathcal O)=\Spec k\).  But \(\dim\mathbb P^n=n>0\) while \(\dim\Spec k=0\), a contradiction.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
