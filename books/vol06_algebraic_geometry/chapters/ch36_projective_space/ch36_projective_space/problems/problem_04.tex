\begin{problem}[VI.36.P4: Dimension and codimension of linear spaces]\label{prob:vi36-04}
Let \(L\subset\mathbb P^n\) be the projective linear locus cut out by \(r\) independent linear forms.  Compute its dimension and codimension.
\begin{solution}
After a linear coordinate change, \(L\) is given by \(x_0=\cdots=x_{r-1}=0\), hence \(L\cong\mathbb P^{n-r}\).  Therefore \(\dim L=n-r\) and \(\operatorname{codim}(L,\mathbb P^n)=r\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
