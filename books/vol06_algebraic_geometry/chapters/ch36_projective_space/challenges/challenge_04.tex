\begin{problem}[Challenge 4: Segre rank-one locus]\label{chal:vi36-04}
For general \(m,n\), prove set-theoretically over a field that every nonzero rank-one matrix \((z_{ij})\) is represented by Segre coordinates \(z_{ij}=x_i y_j\), unique up to reciprocal rescaling of the two vectors.
\end{problem}
