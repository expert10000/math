\begin{problem}[VI.36.P1: Reconstruct \(\mathbb P^1\) by gluing]\label{prob:vi36-01}
Starting from two copies \(X_0=\Spec k[t]\) and \(X_1=\Spec k[u]\), glue \(D(t)\) to \(D(u)\) by \(u=t^{-1}\).  Prove that the resulting scheme is isomorphic to \(\mathbb P^1_k\).
\begin{solution}
Identify \(X_0\) with \(D_+(x_0)\) by \(t=x_1/x_0\) and \(X_1\) with \(D_+(x_1)\) by \(u=x_0/x_1\).  VI/35 identifies their intersection with \(D_+(x_0x_1)\), and homogeneous localization gives \(u=t^{-1}\).  The gluing data therefore agree with the standard open cover of \(\Proj k[x_0,x_1]\), so uniqueness in the gluing theorem gives the isomorphism.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
