\begin{problem}[VI.36.P8: Quadratic Veronese chart computation]\label{prob:vi36-08}
Compute the quadratic Veronese map \(\nu_2:\mathbb P^1\to\mathbb P^2\) on the affine chart \(s\ne0\), and show that its image there is the parabola \(z=x^2\).
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Write
\[
[s:t]=[1:u]
\]
on the source chart \(s\neq0\). Then
\[
\nu_2([1:u])
=
[1:u:u^2].
\]
In the target chart \(X\neq0\), use affine coordinates
\[
y=Y/X,
\qquad
z=Z/X.
\]
The image is
\[
(y,z)=(u,u^2),
\]
so it satisfies
\[
\boxed{z=y^2}.
\]
Conversely every point \((y,z)\) with \(z=y^2\) is obtained from \(u=y\). Thus on these affine charts the
Veronese coordinate map is exactly the familiar parametrization of the parabola.
\end{solution}
\fi
