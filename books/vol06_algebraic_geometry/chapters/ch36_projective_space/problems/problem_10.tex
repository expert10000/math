\begin{problem}[VI.36.P10: Projective completion of a parabola]\label{prob:vi36-10}
Homogenize \(y-x^2=0\) and determine the additional point at infinity of its projective closure set-theoretically.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Use
\[
x=x_1/x_0,
\qquad
y=x_2/x_0.
\]
The affine equation has degree \(2\). Multiplying by \(x_0^2\) gives the homogeneous equation
\[
\boxed{x_0x_2-x_1^2=0}.
\]
On \(x_0\neq0\), dehomogenization recovers \(y-x^2=0\).

At infinity set \(x_0=0\). Then
\[
-x_1^2=0,
\]
so \(x_1=0\). Since homogeneous coordinates cannot all vanish, \(x_2\neq0\), giving the unique point
\[
\boxed{[0:0:1]}.
\]
Thus the projective completion adds one point to the affine parabola, set-theoretically.
\end{solution}
\fi
