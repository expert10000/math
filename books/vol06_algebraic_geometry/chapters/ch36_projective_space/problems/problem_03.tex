\begin{problem}[VI.36.P3: Affine chart and hyperplane at infinity]\label{prob:vi36-03}
Show that choosing any nonzero linear form \(L\) on \(k^{n+1}\) produces an affine open \(D_+(L)\cong\mathbb A^n_k\) whose complement is a projective hyperplane.
\begin{solution}
An invertible linear change of variables sends \(L\) to \(x_0\).  It induces a graded automorphism of the polynomial ring and hence an automorphism of projective space.  Under this automorphism \(D_+(L)\) becomes \(D_+(x_0)\cong\mathbb A^n\), while the complement becomes \(V_+(x_0)\cong\mathbb P^{n-1}\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
