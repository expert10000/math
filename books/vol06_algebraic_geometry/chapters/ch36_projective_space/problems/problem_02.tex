\begin{problem}[VI.36.P2: Three-chart atlas for \(\mathbb P^2\)]\label{prob:vi36-02}
Write all three transition maps among the standard affine charts of \(\mathbb P^2\) and verify the cocycle identity on the triple overlap.
\begin{solution}
From \(U_0\) coordinates \((u,v)=(x_1/x_0,x_2/x_0)\), the maps are \(U_0\to U_1:(u,v)\mapsto(1/u,v/u)\) and \(U_0\to U_2:(u,v)\mapsto(1/v,u/v)\).  Similar formulas follow cyclically.  Composing \(0\to1\to2\) gives \((u,v)\mapsto(u/v,1/v)\), exactly the direct \(0\to2\) change after ordering coordinates appropriately.  Hence the cocycle condition holds.
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
