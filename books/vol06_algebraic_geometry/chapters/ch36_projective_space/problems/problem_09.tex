\begin{problem}[VI.36.P9: Segre chart computation]\label{prob:vi36-09}
Compute the Segre map \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\to\mathbb P^3\) on the chart \(s\ne0,u\ne0\) and identify its affine image equation.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Write the source point as
\[
([1:a],[1:b]).
\]
The Segre map sends it to
\[
[1:b:a:ab].
\]
On the target chart \(z_{00}\neq0\), write affine coordinates
\[
y=z_{01}/z_{00},
\qquad
x=z_{10}/z_{00},
\qquad
w=z_{11}/z_{00}.
\]
Then the image is
\[
(x,y,w)=(a,b,ab),
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{w=xy}.
\]
Conversely every point of this affine graph is obtained from \((a,b)=(x,y)\). Thus the Segre map is chartwise the
polynomial graph map
\[
\mathbb A^1\times\mathbb A^1\to\mathbb A^3,
\qquad
(a,b)\mapsto(a,b,ab).
\]
\end{solution}
\fi
