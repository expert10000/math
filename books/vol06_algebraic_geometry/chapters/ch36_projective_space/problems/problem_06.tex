\begin{problem}[VI.36.P6: Base change of a non-rational point]\label{prob:vi36-06}
Let \(x\) be the closed point of \(\mathbb P^1_k\) associated with a separable irreducible polynomial \(f\) of degree \(d\).  Describe what happens after base change to a splitting field \(K\).
\begin{solution}
On the affine chart the fiber is \(\Spec K[t]/(f)\).  Since \(f\) is separable and splits as \(\prod_{i=1}^d(t-a_i)\), the Chinese remainder theorem gives \(K[t]/(f)\cong\prod_iK\).  Thus the geometric fiber is a disjoint union of \(d\) \(K\)-rational points \([1:a_i]\).
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
