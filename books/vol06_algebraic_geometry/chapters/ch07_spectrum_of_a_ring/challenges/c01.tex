\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.07 - Generic points from irreducible closed sets]
Let $Z\subseteq\Spec A$ be nonempty, closed, and irreducible.  Prove there exists a unique
prime $\mathfrak p$ such that
\[
Z=\overline{\{\mathfrak p\}}.
\]
\end{problem}
\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Write $Z=V(I)$ and use irreducibility to show $\sqrt I$ is prime.
\begin{solution}
Since $Z$ is irreducible, $\mathfrak p:=\sqrt I$ is prime.  Then
\[
Z=V(I)=V(\mathfrak p)=\overline{\{\mathfrak p\}}.
\]
If also $Z=V(\mathfrak q)$ for a prime $\mathfrak q$, equality of closed sets gives
$\mathfrak q=\sqrt{\mathfrak q}=\sqrt I=\mathfrak p$.
\end{solution}
\paragraph{Extension.}
VI/09 develops generic points systematically and distinguishes them from closed points.
\paragraph{Provenance.}
New forward-looking challenge built from the legacy prime/irreducible dictionary.
