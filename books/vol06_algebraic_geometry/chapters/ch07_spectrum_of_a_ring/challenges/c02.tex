\begin{problem}[Challenge VI.07 - Reduction does not change the topological spectrum]
Let
\[
A_{\mathrm{red}}=A/\sqrt{(0)}.
\]
Prove the quotient map induces a homeomorphism
\[
\Spec(A_{\mathrm{red}})\cong\Spec A.
\]
\end{problem}
\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Every prime ideal contains the nilradical.
\begin{solution}
Prime correspondence for the quotient identifies $\Spec(A_{\mathrm{red}})$ with
\[
V_A(\sqrt{(0)}).
\]
But every prime contains $\sqrt{(0)}$, so this closed set is all of $\Spec A$.  The quotient
homeomorphism theorem therefore gives the result.
\end{solution}
