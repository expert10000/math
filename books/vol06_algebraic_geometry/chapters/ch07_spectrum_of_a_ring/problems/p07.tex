\begin{problem}[Functoriality of $\Spec$]
Let
\[
A\xrightarrow{\varphi}B\xrightarrow{\psi}C
\]
be ring homomorphisms.  Prove that contraction defines continuous maps on spectra and that
\[
(\psi\circ\varphi)^\ast=\varphi^\ast\circ\psi^\ast.
\]
Also prove $(\operatorname{id}_A)^\ast=\operatorname{id}_{\Spec A}$.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the first appearance of $\Spec$ as a contravariant functor, the organizing principle
behind affine schemes.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Contraction of a prime is prime.  Compute inverse images of closed sets $V(I)$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Show
\[
(\varphi^\ast)^{-1}(V_A(I))=V_B(IB).
\]

\begin{solution}
For $\mathfrak q\in\Spec B$, the contraction $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$ is prime.
Furthermore
\[
\mathfrak q\in(\varphi^\ast)^{-1}(V_A(I))
\iff
I\subseteq\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)
\iff
IB\subseteq\mathfrak q,
\]
so
\[
(\varphi^\ast)^{-1}(V_A(I))=V_B(IB).
\]
Thus $\varphi^\ast$ is continuous.

For $\mathfrak r\in\Spec C$,
\[
(\psi\circ\varphi)^{-1}(\mathfrak r)
=
\varphi^{-1}(\psi^{-1}(\mathfrak r)),
\]
hence the composition identity.  The identity statement is immediate.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Ring arrows reverse because points are prime ideals and points move by inverse image.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Translate the result into a commutative diagram and preview affine-scheme morphisms in VI/19.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis from legacy contraction and spectrum-map material.
