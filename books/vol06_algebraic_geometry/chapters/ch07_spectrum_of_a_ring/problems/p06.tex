\begin{problem}[Quotients are closed subspaces]
For an ideal $I\subseteq A$, prove that prime correspondence upgrades to a homeomorphism
\[
\Spec(A/I)\cong V_A(I).
\]
Then track an arbitrary closed subset of $\Spec(A/I)$ under this homeomorphism.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the topological prototype of a closed immersion of affine schemes.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Every ideal of $A/I$ is $J/I$ for an ideal $J\supseteq I$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Identify
\[
V_{A/I}(J/I)
\]
with a closed subset of $V_A(I)$.

\begin{solution}
Prime correspondence gives a bijection
\[
\mathfrak q=\mathfrak p/I
\longleftrightarrow
\mathfrak p\in V_A(I).
\]
For $J\supseteq I$,
\[
\mathfrak q\in V_{A/I}(J/I)
\iff
J/I\subseteq\mathfrak p/I
\iff
J\subseteq\mathfrak p.
\]
Hence this closed set maps to
\[
V_A(J)\subseteq V_A(I).
\]
Every closed subset of the subspace $V_A(I)$ is of this form after replacing an ambient ideal
by its sum with $I$.  Thus the bijection is a homeomorphism.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Imposing equations algebraically by quotienting is exactly the same topologically as passing
to the corresponding closed locus.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Apply the theorem to a parabola, a doubled point, and $\ZZ/n\ZZ$.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical problem consolidating legacy quotient-spectrum examples from CA13.
