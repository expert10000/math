\begin{problem}[Sheafification commutes with restriction to an open subset]
Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be open and $\mathcal F$ a presheaf on $X$. Prove a natural isomorphism
\[
(\mathcal F|_U)^\#\cong(\mathcal F^\#)|_U.
\]
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Sheafification is itself local; passing to a smaller open region should not change the repair.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the universal property, stalk preservation, and the fact that a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism when it is so on every stalk.  Keep the distinction between a presheaf statement on sections and a local statement on germs explicit.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Compare stalks at $x\in U$, or verify the universal property on $U$.

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
For $x\in U$, neighborhoods of $x$ contained in $U$ are cofinal among all neighborhoods used to compute the restricted stalk, so
\[
(\mathcal F|_U)_x\cong\mathcal F_x.
\]
Sheafification preserves stalks, hence both $(\mathcal F|_U)^\#$ and $(\mathcal F^\#)|_U$ have stalk canonically $\mathcal F_x$ at every $x\in U$. The unit $\mathcal F|_U\to(\mathcal F^\#)|_U$ induces these isomorphisms and factors uniquely through $(\mathcal F|_U)^\#$. The resulting map between sheaves is an isomorphism on every stalk, hence an isomorphism. Naturality follows from uniqueness.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The construction can be checked and performed open-by-open.

\paragraph{Alternative approaches.}
Whenever possible one may replace the espace-etale model by the two-plus construction, or conversely replace a cover/refinement argument by the universal property.  The two approaches agree because both construct the same associated sheaf.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Replace an arbitrary open basis by principal opens in an affine spectrum.
