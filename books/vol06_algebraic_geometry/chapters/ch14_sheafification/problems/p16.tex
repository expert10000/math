\begin{problem}[Stalkwise equivalence of presheaves becomes an isomorphism]
Let $f:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G$ be a presheaf morphism such that $f_x:\mathcal F_x\to\mathcal G_x$ is an isomorphism for every $x$. Prove $f^\#:\mathcal F^\#\to\mathcal G^\#$ is an isomorphism.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Sheafification identifies presheaves that carry the same local germ information.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the universal property, stalk preservation, and the fact that a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism when it is so on every stalk.  Keep the distinction between a presheaf statement on sections and a local statement on germs explicit.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Use P8 and the stalkwise isomorphism criterion for sheaves.

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Under the canonical stalk identifications, P8 gives $(f^\#)_x=f_x$. By hypothesis this is an isomorphism for every $x$. Since $f^\#$ is a morphism of sheaves and a sheaf morphism is an isomorphism iff it is an isomorphism on all stalks, $f^\#$ is an isomorphism.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
This is the local-equivalence principle behind viewing sheafification as a localization process.

\paragraph{Alternative approaches.}
Whenever possible one may replace the espace-etale model by the two-plus construction, or conversely replace a cover/refinement argument by the universal property.  The two approaches agree because both construct the same associated sheaf.
