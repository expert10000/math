\begin{problem}[Sheafifying the constant presheaf]
For an abelian group $A$, let $A_{\mathrm{pre}}$ be the naive constant presheaf on $X$. Prove that its sheafification is the sheaf $\underline A$ of locally constant $A$-valued functions. Describe the unit explicitly.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the standard first nontrivial computation of an associated sheaf and appears repeatedly in the legacy sheaf notes.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the universal property, stalk preservation, and the fact that a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism when it is so on every stalk.  Keep the distinction between a presheaf statement on sections and a local statement on germs explicit.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Both have stalk $A$. Map a constant section to the corresponding constant function and use local constancy for local surjectivity.

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
Define $u:A_{\mathrm{pre}}(U)\to\underline A(U)$ by sending $a$ to the constant function $x\mapsto a$. This is compatible with restrictions. Every locally constant function is locally constant in the literal sense: each point has an open neighborhood on which the function equals one value $a\in A$, so $u$ is locally surjective. Its stalk map is the identity $A\to A$ at every point. Therefore the induced map
\[
A_{\mathrm{pre}}^\#\longrightarrow\underline A
\]
is an isomorphism on all stalks and hence an isomorphism of sheaves. The unit is exactly the inclusion of globally constant sections into locally constant sections.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Sheafification allows the chosen constant to vary from one locally disconnected piece to another.

\paragraph{Alternative approaches.}
Whenever possible one may replace the espace-etale model by the two-plus construction, or conversely replace a cover/refinement argument by the universal property.  The two approaches agree because both construct the same associated sheaf.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Compute global sections in terms of connected components when the space is locally connected.
