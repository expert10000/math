\begin{problem}[Functoriality is forced by universality]
Given presheaf morphisms $\mathcal F\xrightarrow f\mathcal G\xrightarrow g\mathcal H$, construct $f^\#$ and prove
\[
(\id_{\mathcal F})^\#=\id_{\mathcal F^\#},\qquad (g\circ f)^\#=g^\#\circ f^\#.
\]
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
The associated-sheaf construction must be a functor, not a collection of unrelated choices.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the universal property, stalk preservation, and the fact that a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism when it is so on every stalk.  Keep the distinction between a presheaf statement on sections and a local statement on germs explicit.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Factor $\eta_{\mathcal G}f$ through $\eta_{\mathcal F}$ and use uniqueness twice.

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\begin{solution}
The composite $\eta_{\mathcal G}f:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G^\#$ has sheaf target, so there is a unique $f^\#: \mathcal F^\#\to\mathcal G^\#$ with
\[
f^\#\eta_{\mathcal F}=\eta_{\mathcal G}f.
\]
For $f=\id$, both $(\id)^\#$ and $\id_{\mathcal F^\#}$ satisfy the same equation with the unit, hence are equal. For a composite,
\[
(g^\#f^\#)\eta_{\mathcal F}=g^\#\eta_{\mathcal G}f=\eta_{\mathcal H}gf.
\]
By uniqueness this equals $(gf)^\#$.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Functoriality is a theorem of the universal property; it need not be separately imposed on the explicit germ construction.

\paragraph{Alternative approaches.}
Whenever possible one may replace the espace-etale model by the two-plus construction, or conversely replace a cover/refinement argument by the universal property.  The two approaches agree because both construct the same associated sheaf.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use the same method to prove naturality of the unit.
