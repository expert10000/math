\begin{problem}[Challenge: Finite products and sheafification]
For presheaves of abelian groups $\mathcal F$ and $\mathcal G$, prove that the canonical map
\[
(\mathcal F\times\mathcal G)^\#\longrightarrow\mathcal F^\#\times\mathcal G^\#
\]
is an isomorphism. Explain why the proof is different in spirit from preservation of coproducts by a left adjoint.
\end{problem}
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Solution sketch.}
At each point, filtered colimits of abelian groups commute with finite products, so $(\mathcal F\times\mathcal G)_x\cong\mathcal F_x\times\mathcal G_x$. Sheafification preserves these stalks, while the product sheaf has stalk the finite product of stalks. The canonical map is therefore a stalkwise isomorphism and hence an isomorphism. Unlike coproduct preservation, this is not a formal consequence of left-adjointness; it uses the local/stalk description and finite-limit behavior of filtered colimits in abelian groups.
\paragraph{Extension.}
Identify exactly which part of the argument is formal from the universal property and which part uses the concrete category of sets, groups, rings, or modules.
