\begin{problem}[Challenge: A non-locally-connected constant-sheaf calculation]
Let $X=\{0\}\cup\{1/n:n\ge1\}\subset\mathbb R$ with the subspace topology and let $A$ be a nonzero abelian group. Describe the sheafification of the naive constant presheaf on $X$ and analyze its global sections. In particular, determine which $A$-valued functions on $X$ are locally constant.
\end{problem}
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Solution sketch.}
Every isolated point $1/n$ may carry an arbitrary value locally. At $0$, however, local constancy requires that all sufficiently large $n$ have the same value as $0$. Hence global sections of the associated sheaf are sequences $(a_0,a_1,a_2,\ldots)$ that are eventually equal to $a_0$. This illustrates that ``locally constant'' is subtler than ``constant on connected components'' when components are not open.
\paragraph{Extension.}
Identify exactly which part of the argument is formal from the universal property and which part uses the concrete category of sets, groups, rings, or modules.
