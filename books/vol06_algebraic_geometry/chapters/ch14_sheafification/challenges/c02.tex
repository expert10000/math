\begin{problem}[Challenge: Sheafification as localization at local equivalences]
Call a presheaf morphism a \emph{local equivalence} if it induces an isomorphism on every stalk. Prove that the unit $\eta_{\mathcal F}:\mathcal F\to\mathcal F^\#$ is a local equivalence and that every map from $\mathcal F$ to a sheaf factors uniquely through this local equivalence. Show also that sheafification sends every local equivalence to an isomorphism.
\end{problem}
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Solution sketch.}
Stalk preservation proves the first assertion. The second is the sheafification universal property. For the final assertion, if $f$ is a local equivalence, then under the unit identifications $(f^\#)_x=f_x$ is an isomorphism for all $x$; the stalkwise criterion for sheaves makes $f^\#$ an isomorphism. Thus sheafification inverts exactly the class of maps relevant to local germ data.
\paragraph{Extension.}
Identify exactly which part of the argument is formal from the universal property and which part uses the concrete category of sets, groups, rings, or modules.
