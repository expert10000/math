\begin{problem}[Challenge: Sheaves of sets and etale spaces]
Work with sheaves of sets on a topological space $X$. Starting from a sheaf $\mathcal F$, form $E=\coprod_x\mathcal F_x$ with the germ topology and prove that $\pi:E\to X$ is a local homeomorphism. Conversely, for a local homeomorphism $\pi:E\to X$, prove that $U\mapsto\Gamma(U,E)$ is a sheaf of sets. Construct the natural equivalence between these two procedures.
\end{problem}
\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
\paragraph{Solution sketch.}
The key point is that a germ $s_x$ has a basic neighborhood $\widetilde s(U)$ on which $\pi$ is a homeomorphism. Conversely, sections of a local homeomorphism glue as ordinary maps because agreement is pointwise and continuity is local. For the two composites, evaluate a sheaf section by its germs, and evaluate a point $e\in E_x$ by the germ of the unique local section through $e$. These maps are inverse and natural.
\paragraph{Extension.}
Identify exactly which part of the argument is formal from the universal property and which part uses the concrete category of sets, groups, rings, or modules.
