\begin{problem}[Open-set Yoneda lemma and representables]
Prove the Yoneda bijection $\operatorname{Nat}(h_U,\mathcal F)\cong\mathcal F(U)$ and then determine all morphisms $h_U\to h_V$.  Interpret the answer entirely in terms of inclusions of open sets.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this challenge matters.}
It pushes the presheaf formalism beyond routine restriction calculations and exposes the
categorical or affine structure that later sheaf theory will exploit.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Translate topology into a poset of opens and use functoriality or the universal property
suggested in the statement.

\begin{solution}
Applying the Yoneda bijection with $\mathcal F=h_V$ gives
\[
\operatorname{Nat}(h_U,h_V)\cong h_V(U)=\Hom_{\operatorname{Open}(X)}(U,V).
\]
Because the open-set category is a poset, this is a singleton exactly when $U\subseteq V$ and is empty otherwise.  Thus representable presheaves recover the open-set category inside the presheaf category.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{Extension.}
Formulate the same construction in an arbitrary category rather than only
$\operatorname{Open}(X)$.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical challenge built around the legacy presheaf foundations.
