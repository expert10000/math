\begin{problem}[Presheaves on finite Alexandrov spaces]
Let $X$ be a finite $T_0$ Alexandrov space.  Show that a presheaf on $X$ can be encoded by a contravariant diagram on the poset of minimal open neighborhoods.  Work out the correspondence explicitly for a three-point chain and a three-point ``V'' poset.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this challenge matters.}
It pushes the presheaf formalism beyond routine restriction calculations and exposes the
categorical or affine structure that later sheaf theory will exploit.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Translate topology into a poset of opens and use functoriality or the universal property
suggested in the statement.

\begin{solution}
Each point $x$ has a smallest open neighborhood $U_x$.  In an Alexandrov space, inclusions among these opens encode the specialization order.  Restrict a presheaf to the $U_x$ and record the maps induced by inclusions.  In the explicit three-point examples, arbitrary opens are unions of these minimal neighborhoods, so the required diagram can be written out directly.  The result makes the topological incidence relations visible as algebraic arrows.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{Extension.}
Formulate the same construction in an arbitrary category rather than only
$\operatorname{Open}(X)$.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical challenge built around the legacy presheaf foundations.
