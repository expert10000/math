\begin{problem}[Direct image of a presheaf]
Let $f:X\to Y$ be continuous.  Construct $f_*$ on presheaves and on morphisms.  Prove $(g\circ f)_*=g_*f_*$ for a second continuous map $g:Y\to Z$.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Direct image is the first geometric functor on presheaves and persists for sheaves.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use inverse images of opens and $(g\circ f)^{-1}=f^{-1}\circ g^{-1}$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Use inverse images of opens and $(g\circ f)^{-1}=f^{-1}\circ g^{-1}$.

\begin{solution}
Define $(f_*\mathcal F)(V)=\mathcal F(f^{-1}V)$.  For $W\subseteq V$ use the restriction for $f^{-1}W\subseteq f^{-1}V$.  A morphism $\varphi$ is sent to $\varphi_{f^{-1}V}$.  For $g$, evaluation on an open $T\subseteq Z$ gives $\mathcal F(f^{-1}(g^{-1}T))$ on both sides with the same restrictions.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Pushforward of presheaf data is reindexing by inverse image of opens.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Later prove that direct image of a sheaf is a sheaf.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis problem.
