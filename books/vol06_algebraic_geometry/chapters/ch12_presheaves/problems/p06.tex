\begin{problem}[Naturality of a presheaf morphism]
Let $\varphi:\mathcal F\to\mathcal G$ be a family of homomorphisms $\varphi_U$ on all opens.  Prove that it is a morphism of presheaves exactly when it carries every restricted section to the restriction of its image.  Show that naturality for adjacent inclusions suffices in a finite-chain topology.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Morphisms in the presheaf category are natural transformations, not independent maps.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Write the naturality square and chase one section around it.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Write the naturality square and chase one section around it.

\begin{solution}
The required condition is $\varphi_V(s|_V)=\varphi_U(s)|_V$ for all $V\subseteq U$.  This is precisely commutativity of the naturality square.  In a finite chain, every inclusion factors into adjacent inclusions; if the squares commute for adjacent inclusions, functoriality and composition show they commute for every composite inclusion.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Compatibility with restriction is exactly naturality.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Formulate the same statement for a general poset category.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis problem.
