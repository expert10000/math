\begin{problem}[Presheaf of differential-equation solutions]
Let $L$ be a local linear differential operator on an open subset $X\subseteq\RR^n$.  Prove
\[
\mathcal S(U)=\{u\in C^\infty(U):Lu=0\}
\]
forms a presheaf.  Work out $L=d/dx-1$ on disconnected opens of $\RR$.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Solution spaces of local equations are a major source of presheaves and sheaves.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Locality means $(Lu)|_V=L(u|_V)$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Locality means $(Lu)|_V=L(u|_V)$.

\begin{solution}
If $Lu=0$ on $U$ and $V\subseteq U$, then $L(u|_V)=(Lu)|_V=0$, so restriction lands in $\mathcal S(V)$.  Identity and composition are ordinary restrictions.  For $u'=u$ on $U=\coprod U_i$, each connected component has solutions $c_i e^x$, so a section is a tuple of constants indexed by components.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Local equations naturally organize their solutions by open set.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Replace the ODE by harmonic or holomorphic functions.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis problem.
