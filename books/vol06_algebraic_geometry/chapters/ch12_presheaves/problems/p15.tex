\begin{problem}[A three-stage finite presheaf]
Let the open sets form a chain $\varnothing\subset U_2\subset U_1\subset X$.  Given groups $A_0,A_1,A_2$ and homomorphisms $r_1:A_0\to A_1$, $r_2:A_1\to A_2$, construct the presheaf.  Classify its endomorphisms.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Finite spaces turn natural transformations into explicit commutative diagrams.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
An endomorphism is one map on each $A_i$ commuting with $r_1,r_2$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
An endomorphism is one map on each $A_i$ commuting with $r_1,r_2$.

\begin{solution}
Define $\mathcal F(X)=A_0$, $\mathcal F(U_1)=A_1$, $\mathcal F(U_2)=A_2$, and $\mathcal F(\varnothing)=0$.  Set the long restriction $A_0\to A_2$ equal to $r_2r_1$.  An endomorphism is a triple $(\alpha_0,\alpha_1,\alpha_2)$ satisfying $\alpha_1r_1=r_1\alpha_0$ and $\alpha_2r_2=r_2\alpha_1$.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Presheaf categories on finite spaces are diagram categories.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Replace the chain by a branching finite poset.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New canonical synthesis problem.
