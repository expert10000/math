\begin{problem}[Affine localization on a basis]
Let $A$ be a ring.  On principal opens of $\Spec A$, assign $D(f)\mapsto A_f$.  Define restrictions for inclusions generated by $D(fg)\subseteq D(f)$ and prove identity and composition.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the algebraic prototype of the structure sheaf.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Inside $A_{fg}$, the element $f$ is invertible.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Inside $A_{fg}$, the element $f$ is invertible.

\begin{solution}
The canonical map $A\to A_{fg}$ sends $f$ to a unit, so by the universal property it factors uniquely through $A_f$, giving $A_f\to A_{fg}$.  Passing further to $D(fgh)$ and using transitivity of localization shows the composite agrees with $A_f\to A_{fgh}$.  Taking $g=1$ gives the identity.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Restriction of algebraic data is implemented by further localization.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Use the radical criterion to treat an arbitrary inclusion $D(g)\subseteq D(f)$.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
Legacy-compatible affine preview; the full structure sheaf is reserved for VI/17.
