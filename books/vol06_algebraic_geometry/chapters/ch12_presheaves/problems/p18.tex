\begin{problem}[Yoneda on the open-set category]
Fix an open $U\subseteq X$ and let
\[
h_U(V)=\Hom_{\operatorname{Open}(X)}(V,U).
\]
For any set-valued presheaf $\mathcal F$, prove a natural bijection
\[
\operatorname{Nat}(h_U,\mathcal F)\cong\mathcal F(U).
\]
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the Yoneda lemma in the concrete poset category of open sets.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Evaluate a natural transformation at the identity morphism $U\to U$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Evaluate a natural transformation at the identity morphism $U\to U$.

\begin{solution}
Given $\eta:h_U\to\mathcal F$, map it to $\eta_U(\id_U)\in\mathcal F(U)$.  Conversely, given $s\in\mathcal F(U)$, define $\eta^s_V$ on the unique element of $h_U(V)$, when $V\subseteq U$, by $s|_V$; when $V\not\subseteq U$ the source is empty.  Naturality is exactly transitivity of restrictions.  The two constructions are inverse.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
A section over $U$ is equivalent to a natural transformation from the representable presheaf $h_U$.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Generalize to an arbitrary small category.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
New advanced canonical problem.
