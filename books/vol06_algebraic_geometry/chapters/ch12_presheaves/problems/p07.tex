\begin{problem}[Objectwise kernels, images, and quotients]
For presheaves of abelian groups prove that kernels, objectwise images, and quotients by subpresheaves are presheaves.  Explain why this statement alone does not imply that the corresponding objects are sheaves.
\end{problem}

\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This prepares VI/15 while keeping the distinction between presheaf and sheaf constructions explicit.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use naturality to prove stability under restrictions.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
Use naturality to prove stability under restrictions.

\begin{solution}
For kernels and images, naturality sends kernel elements to kernel elements and image elements to image elements.  For a quotient $\mathcal F/\mathcal H$, define $[s]|_V=[s|_V]$; this is well defined because $\mathcal H$ is restriction-stable.  The presheaf axioms descend from $\mathcal F$.  Nothing in these constructions supplies locality or gluing, so the resulting presheaves need not be sheaves.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Presheaf categories permit sectionwise algebra, while some sheaf constructions later require local correction.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Preview why the image sheaf can contain sections only locally in the objectwise image.
