\subsection*{VI.22.P1: The canonical open subscheme}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p01}
Let $U\subseteq X$ be open.  Prove that $(U,\OO_X|_U)$ is a scheme and that $U\hookrightarrow X$ is an open immersion.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
% VI22-FULL-P01
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the intrinsic construction of open subschemes.  It shows that no new equations or quotient sheaf are needed: openness is implemented by restricting the existing scheme structure.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use an affine neighbourhood of a point of $U$, then refine its intersection with $U$ by distinguished opens.  For the immersion statement, keep track of the topology and the restricted sheaf separately.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Fix $u\in U$ and choose an affine open neighbourhood $V=\Spec A$ of $u$ in $X$.
\item Because $U\cap V$ is open in $\Spec A$, choose $f\in A$ with $u\in D(f)\subseteq U\cap V$.
\item Use $D(f)\cong\Spec A_f$ to obtain an affine neighbourhood of $u$ inside $U$.
\item Check that the inclusion has the required homeomorphism and sheaf-restriction properties.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Let $u\in U$.  Choose an affine open neighbourhood $V=\Spec A$ of $u$ in $X$.
The set $U\cap V$ is an open neighbourhood of $u$ in $\Spec A$.  Distinguished opens form a
basis, so there is $f\in A$ such that
\[
 u\in D(f)\subseteq U\cap V.
\]
By the principal-open theorem from VI/18,
\[
 (D(f),\OO_X|_{D(f)})\cong\Spec A_f.
\]
Thus every point of $U$ has an affine neighbourhood for the restricted sheaf
$\OO_X|_U$, so $(U,\OO_X|_U)$ is a scheme.

Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be the inclusion.  Its underlying continuous map is the identity
from $U$ onto the open subset $U\subseteq X$, hence is a homeomorphism onto an open subset.
For every open $W\subseteq X$,
\[
 \OO_U(W\cap U)=(\OO_X|_U)(W\cap U),
\]
and the sheaf morphism $j^\sharp:\OO_X\to j_*\OO_U$ is precisely restriction.  Therefore
$j$ identifies $\OO_U$ with $\OO_X|_U$, which is exactly the definition of an open immersion.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
The scheme condition is local, and an open subset inherits affine charts simply by shrinking the affine charts of the ambient scheme.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Apply the proof to principal opens and compare the resulting inclusion with the morphism induced by $A\to A_f$.
