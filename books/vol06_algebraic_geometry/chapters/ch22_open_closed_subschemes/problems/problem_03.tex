\subsection*{VI.22.P3: Affine closed subschemes from ideals}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p03}
Let $I\subseteq A$.  Prove that $\Spec(A/I)\to\Spec A$ is a homeomorphism onto $V(I)$ and compute the stalks.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
% VI22-FULL-P03
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
This is the fundamental affine model of a closed immersion.  It upgrades the prime-correspondence theorem from topology to local scheme structure.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Use the prime correspondence for quotients, compare closed sets, then localize the quotient map at a prime containing $I$.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Write a prime of $A/I$ as $\mathfrak p/I$.
\item Check how $V_{A/I}(J/I)$ maps into $\Spec A$.
\item Use $(A/I)_{\mathfrak p/I}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Let $q:A\twoheadrightarrow A/I$ be the quotient map.  A prime ideal of
$A/I$ is uniquely of the form $\mathfrak p/I$, where $\mathfrak p\in\Spec A$ contains $I$.
The map on spectra induced by $q$ is contraction, so
\[
 \mathfrak p/I\longmapsto \mathfrak p.
\]
This is therefore a bijection from $\Spec(A/I)$ onto $V(I)$.

To see that it is a homeomorphism onto its image, it is enough to compare closed sets.  If
$J\supseteq I$, then
\[
 V_{A/I}(J/I)
\]
maps to $V_A(J)$, viewed as a closed subset of $V_A(I)$.  Conversely every closed subset of
$V(I)$ is of this form.  Hence the bijection is a homeomorphism.

Now fix $\mathfrak p\supseteq I$.  The local ring at the corresponding point of the source is
\[
 \OO_{\Spec(A/I),\mathfrak p/I}=(A/I)_{\mathfrak p/I}.
\]
Localization commutes with quotient, giving a canonical isomorphism
\[
 (A/I)_{\mathfrak p/I}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}.
\]
Thus the closed subscheme remembers not only the support $V(I)$ but the quotient local rings
along that support.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
A closed subscheme is a closed set together with quotient local rings, and the quotient ideal survives after localization.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Compare the stalks for $I=(x)$ and $I=(x^2)$ in $k[x]$.
