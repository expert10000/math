\subsection*{VI.22.P7: The affine-local criterion for closed immersions}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p07}
Prove that a morphism that is induced by surjective ring maps on an affine cover of the target is a closed immersion.
\par\textbf{Solution.} On each affine target chart $U_\alpha=\Spec A_\alpha$, surjectivity gives
\[
i^{-1}(U_\alpha)=\Spec(A_\alpha/I_\alpha)
\]
as a closed subscheme.  On overlaps these identifications are restrictions of the same morphism, hence agree.  They glue to identify the source with a closed subscheme of the target.
\end{problem}
