\subsection*{VI.22.P15: Open immersions compose}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p15}
Prove the composite of two open immersions is an open immersion.
\end{problem}

\ifdefined\FullProblemDossiers
% VI22-FULL-P15
\paragraph{Why this problem matters.}
Stability under composition is necessary for open subschemes to behave as a genuine geometric class of morphisms.

\paragraph{Useful ideas before starting.}
Compose the underlying open embeddings and use transitivity of sheaf restriction.

\paragraph{Guided hints.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $V\hookrightarrow U$ and $U\hookrightarrow X$ are open immersions, identify their images as open subsets.
\item Use $(\OO_X|_U)|_V\cong\OO_X|_V$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{solution}
Let
\[
 V\xrightarrow{j}U\xrightarrow{i}X
\]
be open immersions.  Identify $U$ with an open subset of $X$ and $V$ with an open subset of
$U$.  An open subset of an open subset is open in the ambient space, so the composite
$i\circ j$ is a homeomorphism from $V$ onto an open subset of $X$.

For the sheaves,
\[
 \OO_U\cong\OO_X|_U
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \OO_V\cong\OO_U|_V.
\]
Therefore
\[
 \OO_V
 \cong(\OO_X|_U)|_V
 \cong\OO_X|_V.
\]
Thus the composite has exactly the sheaf-identification required of an open immersion.
\end{solution}

\paragraph{What happened mathematically.}
Topological openness and restriction of sheaves are both transitive operations.

\paragraph{Extensions.}
Show that an isomorphism is an open immersion and use composition to transport open subschemes across isomorphisms.
