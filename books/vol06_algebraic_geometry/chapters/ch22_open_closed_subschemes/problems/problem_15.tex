\subsection*{VI.22.P15: Open immersions compose}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p15}
Prove the composite of two open immersions is an open immersion.
\par\textbf{Solution.} The composite identifies the source with an open subset of an open subset, hence with an open subset of the target.  The structure sheaf is obtained by iterated restriction, canonically equal to direct restriction.
\end{problem}
