\subsection*{VI.22.P9: The reduced subscheme of an affine scheme}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p09}
For $X=\Spec A$, show $X_{\rm red}=\Spec(A/\sqrt{(0)})$ has the same underlying space as $X$ and is reduced.
\par\textbf{Solution.} Every prime contains the nilradical, so primes of $A$ and $A/\sqrt{(0)}$ correspond bijectively, with the same Zariski topology.  The quotient has zero nilradical and is therefore reduced.
\end{problem}
