\subsection*{VI.22.P17: A locally closed immersion}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p17}
Let $U\subseteq X$ be open and $Z\hookrightarrow U$ closed.  Show $Z\to X$ is an immersion and its image is locally closed.
\par\textbf{Solution.} By construction the map factors as a closed immersion $Z\hookrightarrow U$ followed by the open immersion $U\hookrightarrow X$.  Its image is closed in the open subset $U$, which is the definition of a locally closed subset.
\end{problem}
