\subsection*{VI.22.P6: Local gluing of closed subschemes}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p06}
Let $X=U\cup V$ with $U,V$ affine.  Suppose closed subschemes $Z_U\hookrightarrow U$ and $Z_V\hookrightarrow V$ agree on $U\cap V$.  Show they glue uniquely to a closed subscheme of $X$.
\par\textbf{Solution.} Their underlying closed subsets and quotient sheaves agree on the overlap.  Apply the gluing theorem of VI/20 to the two schemes $Z_U,Z_V$ along their identified overlap.  The resulting map to $X$ is locally a closed immersion on the cover $U,V$, hence is a closed immersion by the affine-local criterion.
\end{problem}
