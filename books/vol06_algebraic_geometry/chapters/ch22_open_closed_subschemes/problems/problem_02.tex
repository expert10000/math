\subsection*{VI.22.P2: Uniqueness of the open subscheme structure}
\begin{problem}\label{prob:vi22-p02}
Suppose a scheme structure on the open set $U\subseteq X$ makes the inclusion an open immersion.  Prove it is uniquely isomorphic to $(U,\OO_X|_U)$ over $X$.
\par\textbf{Solution.} By definition of open immersion, the source structure sheaf is identified with the restriction of $\OO_X$ to the image.  Both candidate structures are therefore canonically identified with $\OO_X|_U$, and compatibility with the inclusion forces uniqueness.
\end{problem}
