\subsection*{Challenge 1: Classify closed subschemes of a one-point affine scheme}
\begin{problem}\label{chal:vi22-01}
Let $A$ be local Artinian with one prime ideal.  Describe closed subschemes of $\Spec A$ in terms of ideals of $A$, and explain why they can all have the same underlying point.
\par\textbf{Solution sketch.} Every ideal $I$ gives $\Spec(A/I)\hookrightarrow\Spec A$.  If $A/I\ne0$ remains local Artinian, its spectrum is still one point.  Thus the topology can remain constant while nilpotent structure changes.
\end{problem}
