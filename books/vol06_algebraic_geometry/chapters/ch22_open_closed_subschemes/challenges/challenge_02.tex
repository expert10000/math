\subsection*{Challenge 2: Reduction is global}
\begin{problem}\label{chal:vi22-02}
Prove that the affine reductions on an arbitrary affine cover of $X$ glue independently of the cover.
\par\textbf{Solution sketch.} On a principal overlap, localization commutes with quotient by the nilradical: the nilradical of $A_f$ is $\sqrt{(0)}A_f$.  Hence the affine reductions agree on a common principal refinement.  VI/20 gluing gives the global object, and uniqueness makes it cover-independent.
\end{problem}
