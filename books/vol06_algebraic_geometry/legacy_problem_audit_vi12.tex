\section*{Audit rule}

retained exactly once in the canonical series.  The presheaf chapter does not absorb later
sheaf problems merely because their statements contain the word ``presheaf'' or ``sheaf.''

\section*{Corpus result}

The migration atlas names three VI/12 source families: the presheaf selector in the
preferred reorganized sheaf notes, the presheaf/restriction selector in AG1, and the broad
presheaf/sheaf selector in AG2.  Direct inspection shows that the preferred sheaf notes
contain no numbered Problem block in their presheaf section.  The numbered AG1 and AG2
problems nearest this material are mathematically centered on sheafification, image/quotient
sheaves, stalkwise exactness, or global sections, and are therefore routed to VI/14, VI/15,
VI/16, and VI/48 as listed above.

Consequently VI/12 does not invent false legacy provenance.  Its 18 Problem Dossiers are new
canonical synthesis problems, several explicitly derived from legacy examples.  Every
numbered legacy sheaf-family problem remains assigned to one unique canonical destination,
and the large AG1 associated-sheaf Exercise 4 is reserved intact for VI/14.
