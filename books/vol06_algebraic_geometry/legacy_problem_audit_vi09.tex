\section*{VI/09 canonical problem inventory}

VI/09 contains fifteen Problem Dossiers.  The legacy corpus contributes the complete AG8
Problem 1 and the generic-point half of AG6 Problem 7 through a documented merge.  The
remaining dossiers are new synthesis problems supporting specialization, closed points,
minimal primes, arithmetic spectra, field-sensitive closed points, nonreduced generic
points, quotient spectra, and functoriality.

No substantive legacy problem was deleted merely to fit a numerical chapter quota.
The function-field portion of AG6 Problem 7 is deliberately not solved here because VI/27
is its unique canonical destination.
