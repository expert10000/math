\section*{AG3 Problem 11 recovery checklist}
The canonical dossier VI.18.P1 preserves every mathematical layer of the source:
\begin{enumerate}
\item prime ideals of $A_f$ correspond to primes of $A$ avoiding $f$;
\item $D(g/1)$ corresponds to $D(g)\cap D(f)=D(fg)$, proving the homeomorphism;
\item the restricted structure sheaf has section ring $A_{fg}$ on $D(fg)$;
\item the affine structure sheaf has section ring $(A_f)_{g/1}$ on $D(g/1)$;
\item associativity of localization gives
$(A_f)_{g/1}\cong A_{fg}$ with the explicit fraction map;
\item these maps are checked to commute with restriction maps;
\item corresponding stalks satisfy $(A_f)_{\mfp A_f}\cong A_{\mfp}$;
\item maximal ideals are identified, so the stalk isomorphisms are local.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the canonical recovery is an isomorphism of \emph{locally ringed spaces}, not merely
a point bijection or homeomorphism.
