\section*{Audit principle}
Every substantive legacy numbered Problem must have exactly one canonical destination.  For
VI/11, the migration atlas identifies two primary source files:
\emph{theory-of-commutative-algebra-12.tex} and the local-ring/stalk selector inside
\emph{theory-of-commutative-algebra-13.tex}.  A direct inspection of those primary selectors
finds \textbf{no numbered legacy Problem environments} in the VI/11 material.  Accordingly,
VI/11 does not invent a false legacy Problem provenance.  Instead, the extensive legacy
examples and practice exercises are preserved as worked examples/exercises, while the full
Problem Dossiers in the chapter are new synthesis problems built around that corpus.
