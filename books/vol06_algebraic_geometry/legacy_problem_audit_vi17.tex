\section*{Audit principle}
Every substantive legacy problem in Volume VI must have exactly one canonical destination.
The file \texttt{theory-of-algebraic-geometry-3.tex} has a low-precedence fallback to VI/17,
but that fallback is only a safety net.  Numbered Problems whose mathematical centers lie in
later scheme chapters are moved and reserved rather than absorbed here.

\section*{Conclusion}
No numbered corpus Problem has the structure sheaf alone as its unique mathematical center.
VI/17 therefore introduces no false legacy provenance.  Its canonical Problems are new
synthesis problems or legacy-derived theory problems.  The four numbered AG3 Problems are
now explicitly reserved for later destinations, so the file-level VI/17 fallback cannot
silently consume them.  This preserves the invariant
\[
\boxed{\text{every substantive legacy problem}\longrightarrow\text{exactly one canonical destination}.}
\]
