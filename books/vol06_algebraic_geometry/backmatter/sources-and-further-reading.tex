\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and Further Reading}

This guide records primary or authoritative sources relevant to \emph{Volume VI: Algebraic Geometry and Sheaf Theory}.
It is a reading and attribution guide; the volume's own hypotheses and conventions remain authoritative.

\section*{Chapter guide}
\begin{description}
\item[VI/01--VI/11.] Affine algebraic sets/schemes, spectra, morphisms, and field-dependent point questions.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Hilbert; Hartshorne; Stacks Project.

\item[VI/12--VI/20.] Sheaves, locally ringed spaces, scheme gluing, and quasi-coherent structures.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Serre FAC; Grothendieck--Dieudonné EGA I; Hartshorne.

\item[VI/21--VI/38.] Fiber products, base change, dimension, divisors, and scheme-theoretic constructions.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Grothendieck--Dieudonné EGA; Hartshorne; Stacks Project.

\item[VI/39--VI/49.] Cohomological and advanced geometric structures; verify exact hypotheses against authoritative modern formulations.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Serre FAC; EGA; Hartshorne; Stacks Project.

\end{description}

\section*{Selected bibliographic notes}
\begin{description}
\item[Serre, FAC (1955).] Coherent algebraic sheaves; DOI \texttt{10.2307/1969915}.

\item[Grothendieck--Dieudonné, EGA.] Foundational scheme language and constructions.

\item[Hartshorne; Stacks Project.] Modern verification of exact theorem statements and hypotheses.

\end{description}

\section*{Reference policy}
Historical attribution and modern theorem verification serve different roles.
Where precise hypotheses matter, use an authoritative modern source in addition to historical attribution.
The maintained series bibliography is stored at \texttt{references/series\_primary\_sources.bib}.
