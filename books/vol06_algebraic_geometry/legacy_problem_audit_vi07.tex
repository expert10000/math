across VI/07--VI/11 and later chapters, so the audit is performed at problem/subproblem level.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Canonical additions.}
VI/07.P2--P12 are new synthesis problems built around the recovered spectrum material:
closed-set calculus, specialization, arithmetic spectra, crossing components, quotient
homeomorphisms, contravariance, image closure, product spectra, concrete contraction maps,
irreducibility, and the radical-ideal lattice.  They supplement rather than replace the
legacy corpus.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Boundary rule.}
VI/07 constructs and studies the Zariski topology on $\Spec A$.  VI/08 owns the distinguished
basis and localization, VI/09 owns systematic generic/closed-point theory, VI/10 owns
nilpotent scheme structure, and VI/11 owns local rings and residue fields.
