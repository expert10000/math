\paragraph{Audit rule.}
Every substantive numbered legacy problem in the sheaf-family corpus must have exactly one
canonical destination.  The Problem count in VI/13 is therefore not a quota.  A numbered
legacy item is included here only when its mathematical center is \emph{Sheaves and Stalks};
otherwise it is routed to the later chapter that owns the main theorem.

\paragraph{Primary source selectors.}
The migration atlas assigns the following material to VI/13:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{sheaves\_notes\_reorganized.tex}: the preferred coherent section on sheaves,
      stalks, and germs;
\item \texttt{theory-of-algebraic-geometry-1.tex}: the sheaf/stalk/germ selector;
\item \texttt{theory-of-algebraic-geometry-2.tex}: the stalk/germ selector.
\end{itemize}
All theorem-like descendants and problem/exercise descendants of those selectors were
checked against neighboring VI/12--VI/16 assignments.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
No numbered legacy Problem has its unique mathematical center in VI/13.  Consequently the
19 VI/13 Problem Dossiers are detailed canonical synthesis problems, several directly
expanding legacy theorem/example material, but none falsely claims a numbered legacy
provenance.  All nearby numbered legacy sheaf problems remain explicitly reserved for
VI/14, VI/15, VI/16, or VI/48.  Thus the global invariant
\[
\boxed{\text{each substantive legacy problem}\longrightarrow\text{exactly one canonical destination}}
\]
is maintained.
