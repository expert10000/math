\section*{Theory-stream recovery}
VI/22 reconstructs the mathematical center required by T02 and the fallback audit:
\begin{itemize}
\item canonical open subschemes $(U,\OO_X|_U)$ and open immersions;
\item affine closed subschemes $\Spec(A/I)\hookrightarrow\Spec A$ and their support $V(I)$;
\item the distinction between an ideal and its radical, including nilpotent thickenings;
\item restriction of closed subschemes to open charts and compatibility with localization;
\item gluing of compatible local affine quotients into a global closed subscheme;
\item the affine-local surjectivity criterion for closed immersions;
\item open, closed, and locally closed immersions;
\item the canonical reduction $X_{\mathrm{red}}\hookrightarrow X$ and its reduced-source mapping property.
\end{itemize}
The 20 VI/22 problem dossiers are canonical developments of this theory stream.  None is labeled
as a numbered legacy migration.

\section*{Non-duplication and boundaries}
\begin{itemize}
\item AG5-P2 stays uniquely consumed by VI/20; VI/22 uses affine overlap refinement only as prior infrastructure.
\item AG5-P3 remains reserved for VI/27; VI/22 uses reducedness only to define the reduction and does not migrate the integral-scheme theorem.
\item AG5-P4 remains reserved for VI/26; Noetherianity is not developed here.

\item Full quasi-coherent sheaf theory remains reserved for VI/38; ideal sheaves are used only as compact notation for compatible affine ideals.
\item Projective closed embeddings remain reserved for VI/37.
\end{itemize}
