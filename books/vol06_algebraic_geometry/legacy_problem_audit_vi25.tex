\section*{Direct legacy problem: DG4-P12}
The source asks for a variety $X\subset\mathbb A^3$ whose projection to the $z$-line has two
intersecting-line fibers for $z\ne0$ but two parallel lines at $z=0$.  The source solution is
\[
X=V\bigl(y(y-zx-1)\bigr).
\]
VI.25.P1 preserves this exact equation and verifies the fibers scheme-theoretically:
\[
X_c=\Spec k[x,y]/\bigl(y(y-cx-1)\bigr).
\]
For $c\ne0$ the components meet at $(-1/c,0)$; for $c=0$ they are $y=0$ and $y=1$.
