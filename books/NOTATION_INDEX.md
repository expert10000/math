## Editorial conventions

- `X, Y, Z`: spaces, schemes, or varieties
- `A, B, R, S`: rings
- `M, N`: modules
- `\mathfrak p, \mathfrak q`: prime ideals
- `\mathfrak m`: maximal ideals
- `\mathcal F, \mathcal G`: sheaves
