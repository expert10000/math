\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and Further Reading}

This guide records primary or authoritative sources relevant to \emph{Volume IV: Complex Analysis and Riemann Surfaces}.
It is a reading and attribution guide; the volume's own hypotheses and conventions remain authoritative.

\section*{Chapter guide}
\begin{description}
\item[IV/01--IV/14.] Holomorphicity, Cauchy theory, residues, argument principle, and branches.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Cauchy; Riemann; Ahlfors.

\item[IV/15--IV/23.] Analytic continuation, Möbius/conformal geometry, and passage to Riemann surfaces.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Weierstrass; Riemann; Forster.

\item[IV/24--IV/31.] Branched coverings, compact surfaces, lattices, elliptic functions, and elliptic curves as Riemann surfaces.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Riemann; Weierstrass; Forster; Farkas--Kra.

\end{description}

\section*{Selected bibliographic notes}
\begin{description}
\item[Cauchy and Riemann.] Historical foundations of complex function theory.

\item[Ahlfors; Forster.] Modern complex analysis and Riemann-surface references.

\end{description}

\section*{Reference policy}
Historical attribution and modern theorem verification serve different roles.
Where precise hypotheses matter, use an authoritative modern source in addition to historical attribution.
The maintained series bibliography is stored at \texttt{references/series\_primary\_sources.bib}.
