\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and Further Reading}

This guide records primary or authoritative sources relevant to \emph{Volume VII: Differential, Riemannian and Hyperbolic Geometry}.
It is a reading and attribution guide; the volume's own hypotheses and conventions remain authoritative.

\section*{Chapter guide}
\begin{description}
\item[VII/01--VII/19.] Smooth manifolds, bundles/forms, curves, surfaces, and classical surface geometry.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Gauss; do Carmo; Lee.

\item[VII/20--VII/30.] Riemannian/Lorentzian metrics, connections, geodesics, curvature, completeness, and global metric geometry.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Riemann; Levi-Civita; Hopf--Rinow; do Carmo; Lee.

\item[VII/31--VII/37.] Hyperbolic models, Möbius actions, Fuchsian/Kleinian groups, and boundary geometry.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Poincaré; Beardon; Ratcliffe.

\item[VII/38--VII/39.] Exact and approximate geodesics on polyhedral and triangle meshes.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Mitchell--Mount--Papadimitriou 1987; Surazhsky et al. 2005.

\item[VII/40.] The heat method for geodesic distance; DOI 10.1145/2516971.2516977.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Crane--Weischedel--Wardetzky 2013.

\item[VII/41.] Discrete differential operators and Laplace--Beltrami discretization.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Meyer et al. 2003; discrete differential-operator literature.

\item[VII/42.] Discrete curvature structures, feature lines, ridges, valleys, and numerical interpretation.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Pinkall--Polthier 1993; differential-geometry references.

\end{description}

\section*{Reference policy}
Historical attribution and modern theorem verification serve different roles.
Where precise hypotheses matter, use an authoritative modern source in addition to historical attribution.
The maintained series bibliography is stored at \texttt{references/series\_primary\_sources.bib}.
