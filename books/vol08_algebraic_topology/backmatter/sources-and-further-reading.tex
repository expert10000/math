\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Sources and Further Reading}

This guide records primary or authoritative sources relevant to \emph{Volume VIII: Algebraic Topology}.
It is a reading and attribution guide; the volume's own hypotheses and conventions remain authoritative.

\section*{Chapter guide}
\begin{description}
\item[VIII/01--VIII/14.] Fundamental groups, coverings, group actions, and classical homotopy foundations.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Poincaré; Hurewicz; Hatcher.

\item[VIII/15--VIII/27.] Simplicial/singular/cellular homology, exact sequences, coefficients, and Künneth/UCT structures.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Eilenberg--Steenrod; Hatcher; Spanier.

\item[VIII/28--VIII/30.] Cohomology, cup products, and vector bundles.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Eilenberg--Steenrod; Hatcher; Milnor--Stasheff.

\item[VIII/31--VIII/35.] Thom classes, Euler classes, Poincaré duality, intersection forms, and Lefschetz theory.
\textbf{Suggested sources:} Thom 1954; Milnor--Stasheff; Lefschetz.

\end{description}

\section*{Selected bibliographic notes}
\begin{description}
\item[Eilenberg--Steenrod; Hatcher.] Axiomatic and modern algebraic topology.

\item[Thom (1954).] Thom-space/class foundations; DOI \texttt{10.1007/BF02566923}.

\item[Milnor--Stasheff; Lefschetz.] Characteristic classes, duality, and Lefschetz viewpoints.

\end{description}

\section*{Reference policy}
Historical attribution and modern theorem verification serve different roles.
Where precise hypotheses matter, use an authoritative modern source in addition to historical attribution.
The maintained series bibliography is stored at \texttt{references/series\_primary\_sources.bib}.
