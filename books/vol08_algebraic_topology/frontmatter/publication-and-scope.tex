\section*{Audience and prerequisites}
\textbf{Audience.} Readers developing algebraic invariants of topological spaces and bundles.

\textbf{Prerequisites.} Point-set topology, linear algebra, and comfort with groups and quotient constructions; Volume VII gives useful geometric motivation.

\section*{Scope}
Fundamental groups, coverings, homology, exact sequences, cohomology, products, bundles, Thom classes, duality, intersection theory, and Lefschetz theory.
