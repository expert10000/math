\chapter*{Integration Notes}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Integration Notes}

After the Companion corpus is complete and frozen, each Companion problem will be
compared with the corresponding main-volume material. Later integration may classify a
problem as an exact existing match, a near-duplicate to merge, an additional solved
problem, a worked example, an exercise, or a Companion-only item.

No such final placement decision is implied by inclusion in this development scaffold.
