\chapter*{Publication and Scope}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Publication and Scope}

This Companion is a problem-centered volume for the \emph{Theory of Mathematics} series.
Its purpose is to gather the reconciled imported problem corpus into a coherent,
reader-facing book before decisions are made about later integration into Volumes I--VIII.

The organization is intentionally looser than the main series. Each Part corresponds to
one main Volume, but the internal sections are thematic rather than copies of the main
book's chapter structure. Main-text chapter references are therefore descriptive links,
not permanent migration decisions.

Editorial provenance, source hashes, duplicate-collapse records, and source identifiers
remain in machine-readable metadata rather than in the mathematical prose.
