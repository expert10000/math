\chapter*{How to Use This Companion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{How to Use This Companion}

Problems are intended to be readable independently, while short ``Related material''
notes point to the chapters of the main series that develop the necessary theory.
A problem may reference one chapter, a chapter range, or several separated chapters.

The Companion is also a staging volume. A problem appearing here may later become a
worked example, exercise, solved problem, merged problem, or Companion-only item in the
main series. Its stable Companion identifier and provenance record are retained through
that later decision.
