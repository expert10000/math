\subsection*{CP-VI-0105: Blowing up away from the center}

Let $\pi:\operatorname{Bl}_Z X\to X$ be the blow-up of a scheme along a closed subscheme $Z$. Show that $\pi$ is an isomorphism over $X\setminus Z$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The blow-up is $\operatorname{Proj}_X\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\mathcal I^n$, where $\mathcal I$ is the ideal sheaf of $Z$. On $U=X\setminus Z$, the restriction $\mathcal I|_U=\mathcal O_U$. Hence the Rees algebra restricts to $\mathcal O_U[t]$, whose relative Proj is canonically $U$. Therefore the blow-up morphism restricts to an isomorphism $\pi^{-1}(U)\cong U$.
