\begin{exercise}
	Show that
	\[
	\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}
	\]
	is disconnected.
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
By the Chinese remainder theorem,
\[
\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\cong \mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z.
\]
Therefore its spectrum is the disjoint union of the spectra of the two fields. It consists of two clopen points, corresponding to $(2)$ and $(3)$ modulo $6$, and is disconnected.
