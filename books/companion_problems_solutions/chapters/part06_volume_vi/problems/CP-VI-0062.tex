\subsection*{CP-VI-0062: Localizing at a prime}

Let $A$ be a ring and $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}A$. Prove that $A_{\mathfrak p}$ is a local ring with unique maximal ideal $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ and residue field $\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak p)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

An element $a/s\in A_{\mathfrak p}$ is a unit exactly when $a\notin\mathfrak p$: if $a\notin\mathfrak p$, then $a$ belongs to the multiplicative set and $s/a$ is an inverse; if $a\in\mathfrak p$, its image lies in the proper ideal $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$ and cannot be a unit. Thus the nonunits form exactly $\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$, making it the unique maximal ideal. The residue field is
\[
A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}\cong (A/\mathfrak p)_{(0)}=\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak p),
\]
since $A/\mathfrak p$ is a domain.
