\subsection*{CP-VI-0053: Continuity of polynomial maps}

Let $F=(f_1,\dots,f_m):\mathbb A_k^n\to\mathbb A_k^m$ be a polynomial map. Show directly from the definition of the Zariski topology that $F$ is continuous.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

For a closed set $V(S)\subseteq\mathbb A_k^m$, a point $a$ lies in $F^{-1}(V(S))$ iff $g(F(a))=0$ for all $g\in S$. Therefore
\[
F^{-1}(V(S))=V\bigl(\{g(f_1,\dots,f_m):g\in S\}\bigr),
\]
which is algebraic and hence closed. Thus inverse images of closed sets are closed.
