\subsection*{Problem 1 Exactness of Global Sections on an Affine Scheme}

Let \(X=\operatorname{Spec}A\) be affine, and let
\[
0\longrightarrow \mathcal F'
\longrightarrow \mathcal F
\longrightarrow \mathcal F''
\longrightarrow 0
\]
be an exact sequence of quasi-coherent \(\mathcal O_X\)-modules. We want to show that
\[
0\longrightarrow \Gamma(X,\mathcal F')
\longrightarrow \Gamma(X,\mathcal F)
\longrightarrow \Gamma(X,\mathcal F'')
\longrightarrow 0
\]
is exact.

\textbf{Solution.}

Since \(X=\operatorname{Spec}A\) is affine and the sheaves are quasi-coherent, there exist \(A\)-modules
\[
M',\qquad M,\qquad M''
\]
such that
\[
\mathcal F' \cong \widetilde{M'},
\qquad
\mathcal F \cong \widetilde M,
\qquad
\mathcal F'' \cong \widetilde{M''}.
\]

For an affine scheme, the functor
\[
M\longmapsto \widetilde M
\]
gives an equivalence between \(A\)-modules and quasi-coherent sheaves on \(\operatorname{Spec}A\). Under this equivalence, exact sequences of quasi-coherent sheaves correspond exactly to exact sequences of \(A\)-modules.

Therefore the given exact sequence of sheaves corresponds to an exact sequence of \(A\)-modules
\[
0\longrightarrow M'
\longrightarrow M
\longrightarrow M''
\longrightarrow 0.
\]

Now use the fundamental property of quasi-coherent sheaves on affine schemes:
\[
\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}A,\widetilde M)\cong M.
\]
Thus
\[
\Gamma(X,\mathcal F')\cong M',
\qquad
\Gamma(X,\mathcal F)\cong M,
\qquad
\Gamma(X,\mathcal F'')\cong M''.
\]

Taking global sections gives exactly the module sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow M'
\longrightarrow M
\longrightarrow M''
\longrightarrow 0.
\]

Since this sequence is exact, the sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow \Gamma(X,\mathcal F')
\longrightarrow \Gamma(X,\mathcal F)
\longrightarrow \Gamma(X,\mathcal F'')
\longrightarrow 0
\]
is exact.

Hence
\[
\boxed{
	0\to \Gamma(X,\mathcal F')
	\to \Gamma(X,\mathcal F)
	\to \Gamma(X,\mathcal F'')
	\to 0
	\text{ is exact.}
}
\]
