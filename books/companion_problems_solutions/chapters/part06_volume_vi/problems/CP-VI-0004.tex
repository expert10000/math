\begin{exercise}
	Describe \(\operatorname{Spec} k[x]\), where \(k\) is a field.
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
The prime ideals of the PID $k[x]$ are $(0)$ and the maximal ideals $(p(x))$ generated by irreducible polynomials $p$. Thus $\operatorname{Spec} k[x]$ has one generic point $(0)$ and one closed point for every monic irreducible polynomial. The closure of $(0)$ is all of $\operatorname{Spec} k[x]$, while every nonzero prime is closed. If $k$ is algebraically closed, the closed points are precisely $(x-a)$ with $a\in k$.
