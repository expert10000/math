\subsection*{CP-VI-0101: The point at infinity on a Weierstrass cubic}

For the cubic $Y^2Z=X^3+aXZ^2+bZ^3$, show that there is exactly one point with $Z=0$ and verify that it is nonsingular.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Setting $Z=0$ gives $0=X^3$, hence $X=0$. Projective coordinates then force $Y\ne0$, so the unique point is $[0:1:0]$. For $F=Y^2Z-X^3-aXZ^2-bZ^3$, we have $F_Z=Y^2-2aXZ-3bZ^2$, so $F_Z([0:1:0])=1\ne0$. Therefore the point is nonsingular.
