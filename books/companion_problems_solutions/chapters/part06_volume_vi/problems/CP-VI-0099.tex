\subsection*{CP-VI-0099: Picard group law}

Explain why isomorphism classes of line bundles on $X$ form an abelian group under tensor product, with identity $\mathcal O_X$ and inverse $\mathcal L^\vee$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The tensor product of locally free rank-one sheaves is again locally free of rank one. Associativity and commutativity hold up to canonical isomorphism, which is sufficient on isomorphism classes. The structure sheaf is the identity since $\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal O_X\cong\mathcal L$. The dual satisfies $\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal L^\vee\cong\mathcal O_X$ locally, hence globally. Therefore the isomorphism classes form the abelian Picard group $\operatorname{Pic}(X)$.
