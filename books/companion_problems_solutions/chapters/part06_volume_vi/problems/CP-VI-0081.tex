\subsection*{CP-VI-0081: Dimension and chains of irreducible closed subsets}

Show that for a ring $A$, strict chains of prime ideals
\[
\mathfrak p_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq\mathfrak p_r
\]
correspond contravariantly to strict chains of irreducible closed subsets
\[
V(\mathfrak p_r)\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq V(\mathfrak p_0).
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Inclusion of ideals reverses inclusion of vanishing sets. Each $V(\mathfrak p_i)$ is irreducible because $\mathfrak p_i$ is prime. Strictness is preserved in reverse: if $V(\mathfrak p_i)=V(\mathfrak p_{i+1})$, taking radicals gives $\mathfrak p_i=\mathfrak p_{i+1}$. Thus Krull dimension can equally be read as the supremum of lengths of chains of irreducible closed subsets.
