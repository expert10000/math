\begin{exercise}
	Show that
	\[
	k[x]/(x(x-1))\cong k\times k.
	\]
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
The ideals $(x)$ and $(x-1)$ in $k[x]$ are comaximal because
\[
1=x-(x-1).
\]
Their intersection therefore equals their product $(x(x-1))$. The Chinese remainder theorem gives
\[
k[x]/(x(x-1))\cong k[x]/(x)\times k[x]/(x-1)\cong k\times k.
\]
