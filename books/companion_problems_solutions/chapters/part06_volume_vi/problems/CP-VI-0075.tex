\subsection*{CP-VI-0075: Affine fiber products}

Let $A\to B$ and $A\to C$ be ring homomorphisms. Prove that
\[
\operatorname{Spec}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}C
\cong \operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A C).
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

For any scheme $T$ and ring $R$, morphisms $T\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ correspond naturally to ring maps $R\to\Gamma(T,\mathcal O_T)$. By the universal property of the tensor product, ring maps $B\otimes_A C\to\Gamma(T,\mathcal O_T)$ correspond to pairs of ring maps from $B$ and $C$ agreeing on $A$. These are precisely pairs of morphisms $T\to\operatorname{Spec}B$ and $T\to\operatorname{Spec}C$ whose composites to $\operatorname{Spec}A$ agree. This correspondence is natural in the arbitrary test scheme $T$, establishing the stated universal property of the fiber product.
