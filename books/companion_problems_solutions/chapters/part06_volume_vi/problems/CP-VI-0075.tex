\subsection*{CP-VI-0075: Affine fiber products}

Let $A\to B$ and $A\to C$ be ring homomorphisms. Prove that
\[
\operatorname{Spec}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}C
\cong \operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A C).
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

For any scheme $T$, morphisms $T\to\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A C)$ correspond, affine-locally and hence globally, to compatible ring maps from $B$ and $C$ into the coordinate rings of $T$ that agree on $A$. Contravariantly these are exactly pairs of morphisms $T\to\operatorname{Spec}B$ and $T\to\operatorname{Spec}C$ having the same composite to $\operatorname{Spec}A$. Thus $\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A C)$ satisfies the universal property of the fiber product.
