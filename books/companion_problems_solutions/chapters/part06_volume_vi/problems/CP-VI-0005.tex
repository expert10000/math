\begin{exercise}
	Describe \(D(x)\subseteq \operatorname{Spec} k[x]\).
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
By definition $D(x)$ consists of the primes of $k[x]$ not containing $x$. Hence it contains the generic point $(0)$ and every closed point $(p(x))$ with $p$ irreducible and $p\neq x$ (up to associates). If $k$ is algebraically closed, this is $\operatorname{Spec} k[x]$ with the closed point $(x)$ removed.
