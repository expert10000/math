\subsection*{CP-VI-0055: Products and coordinate rings}

For affine algebraic sets $X\subseteq\mathbb A_k^m$ and $Y\subseteq\mathbb A_k^n$, show that
\[
k[X\times Y]\cong k[X]\otimes_k k[Y].
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Write $k[X]=k[x_1,\dots,x_m]/I(X)$ and $k[Y]=k[y_1,\dots,y_n]/I(Y)$. Then
\[
k[X]\otimes_k k[Y]\cong
k[x_1,\dots,x_m,y_1,\dots,y_n]/(I(X)+I(Y)).
\]
The equations in $I(X)$ involve only the $x$-variables and those in $I(Y)$ only the $y$-variables, and their common zero locus is exactly $X\times Y$. This identifies the displayed quotient with the coordinate ring of the product.
