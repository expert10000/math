\subsection*{CP-VI-0092: The projective closure of an affine curve}

Find the projective closure in $\mathbb P_k^2$ of the affine parabola $y=x^2\subseteq\mathbb A_k^2$ and determine its points at infinity.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Use affine coordinates $x=X/Z$, $y=Y/Z$. Homogenizing $y-x^2=0$ to degree two gives
\[
YZ-X^2=0.
\]
Hence the projective closure is $V_+(YZ-X^2)$. Points at infinity have $Z=0$, so the equation becomes $X^2=0$, hence $X=0$. Therefore there is one point at infinity, $[0:1:0]$.
