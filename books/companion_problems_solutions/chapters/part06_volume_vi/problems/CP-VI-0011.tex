\begin{exercise}
	Show that
	\[
	\operatorname{Spec}(k\times k)
	\]
	is disconnected.
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
The prime ideals of a product $A\times B$ are exactly $\mathfrak p\times B$ and $A\times\mathfrak q$. Thus
\[
\operatorname{Spec}(k\times k)=\{(0)\times k,\;k\times(0)\}.
\]
Each point is both open and closed; for example the idempotent $(1,0)$ separates the two components. Hence the spectrum is disconnected.
