\subsection*{CP-VI-0067: Stalkwise detection of zero sections}

Let $F$ be a sheaf of abelian groups on $X$ and $s\in F(U)$. Prove that $s=0$ if and only if every germ $s_x\in F_x$ is zero for $x\in U$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

If $s=0$, every germ is zero. Conversely, if $s_x=0$ for each $x$, then for every $x$ there is a neighborhood $U_x\subseteq U$ on which $s|_{U_x}=0$. The zero sections on the cover $\{U_x\}$ agree on overlaps. By uniqueness in the sheaf axiom, the section glued from them is the zero section on $U$; since $s$ has the same restrictions, $s=0$.
