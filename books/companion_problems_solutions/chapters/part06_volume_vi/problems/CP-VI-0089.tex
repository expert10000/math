\subsection*{CP-VI-0089: Standard affine cover of projective space}

Show that $\mathbb P_k^n=\operatorname{Proj}k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$ is covered by the $n+1$ opens $D_+(x_i)$, each isomorphic to $\mathbb A_k^n$. Describe the transition functions on $D_+(x_i)\cap D_+(x_j)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

A homogeneous prime in Proj cannot contain all $x_i$, so it lies in some $D_+(x_i)$. Each chart is affine with coordinates $u_r=x_r/x_i$ for $r\ne i$. On the overlap where $x_j/x_i=u_j$ is invertible, the $j$-chart coordinates are
\[
v_i=x_i/x_j=u_j^{-1},\qquad v_r=x_r/x_j=u_r/u_j.
\]
These are the usual projective transition functions.
