\subsection*{CP-VI-0071: A morphism induced by a quotient}

Let $A\twoheadrightarrow B=A/I$ be a quotient map. Describe the induced morphism $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ and prove that its image is $V(I)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The induced map sends a prime $\mathfrak q\subseteq B$ to its inverse image in $A$. Primes of $A/I$ correspond bijectively to primes $\mathfrak p\subseteq A$ containing $I$, via $\mathfrak p\leftrightarrow\mathfrak p/I$. Therefore the image is precisely $\{\mathfrak p:I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}=V(I)$. With the induced structure sheaf this is the standard closed immersion $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A$.
