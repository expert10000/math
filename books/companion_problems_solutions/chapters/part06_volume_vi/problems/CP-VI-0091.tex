\subsection*{CP-VI-0091: A projective hypersurface}

Let $F\in k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$ be homogeneous. Show that $V_+(F)\subseteq\mathbb P_k^n$ is a projective scheme and identify its homogeneous coordinate ring.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The homogeneous ideal $(F)$ defines the closed subscheme
\[
V_+(F)=\operatorname{Proj}(k[x_0,\dots,x_n]/(F)).
\]
Thus it is projective by definition, being a closed subscheme of projective space. Its homogeneous coordinate ring is the graded quotient $k[x_0,\dots,x_n]/(F)$.
