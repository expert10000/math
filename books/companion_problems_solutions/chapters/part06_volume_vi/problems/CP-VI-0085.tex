\subsection*{CP-VI-0085: Quasicoherent sheaf from a module}

Let $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $M$ an $A$-module. Define $\widetilde M$ on basic opens by $\widetilde M(D(f))=M_f$. Show that the stalk at $\mathfrak p$ is $M_{\mathfrak p}$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Basic opens $D(f)$ with $f\notin\mathfrak p$ form a neighborhood basis. Therefore
\[
\widetilde M_{\mathfrak p}
=\varinjlim_{f\notin\mathfrak p} M_f
\cong M_{\mathfrak p},
\]
because the colimit localizes $M$ at the multiplicative set $A\setminus\mathfrak p$. This is the module analogue of $\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}=A_{\mathfrak p}$.
