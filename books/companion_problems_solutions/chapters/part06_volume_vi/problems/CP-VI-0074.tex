\subsection*{CP-VI-0074: An open immersion from localization}

Let $f\in A$. Show that the morphism $\operatorname{Spec}A_f\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ induced by localization identifies $\operatorname{Spec}A_f$ with the open subscheme $D(f)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Primes of $A_f$ correspond to primes $\mathfrak p\subseteq A$ disjoint from $\{1,f,f^2,\dots\}$, equivalently to primes not containing $f$. Thus the map is a homeomorphism onto $D(f)$. On a basic open $D(g)\subseteq D(f)$, the corresponding ring is $(A_f)_g\cong A_{fg}$, which agrees with the restriction of the structure sheaf of $\operatorname{Spec}A$. Hence the morphism is an isomorphism onto the open subscheme $D(f)$.
