\begin{exercise}
	Show that
	\[
	\operatorname{Spec} k[x,y]/(xy)
	\]
	is connected but reducible.
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
Put $A=k[x,y]/(xy)$. The ideals $(x)$ and $(y)$ are the two minimal primes, so
\[
\operatorname{Spec} A=V(x)\cup V(y)
\]
is reducible. The two irreducible components meet at the maximal ideal $(x,y)$, hence their union is connected. Equivalently, $A$ has no nontrivial idempotents, so $\operatorname{Spec} A$ is connected.
