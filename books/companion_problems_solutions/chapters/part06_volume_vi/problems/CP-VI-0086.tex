\subsection*{CP-VI-0086: Homogeneous ideals and graded quotients}

Let $S=k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$ with its standard grading and $I\subseteq S$ a homogeneous ideal. Prove that $S/I$ inherits a grading and identify its degree-$d$ part.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Because $I$ is homogeneous, $I=\bigoplus_{d\ge0}I_d$ with $I_d=I\cap S_d$. Define $(S/I)_d=(S_d+I)/I\cong S_d/I_d$. Every class has a representative decomposed into homogeneous components, and homogeneity of $I$ makes this decomposition well-defined modulo $I$. Multiplication satisfies $(S/I)_d(S/I)_e\subseteq(S/I)_{d+e}$, so $S/I$ is graded.
