\subsection*{CP-VI-0073: Residue fields of scheme points}

Let $x\in X$ be a point of a scheme. Define its residue field $\kappa(x)$ and show, when $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $x=\mathfrak p$, that
\[
\kappa(x)\cong\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak p).
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

By definition $\kappa(x)=\mathcal O_{X,x}/\mathfrak m_x$. In the affine case $\mathcal O_{X,x}=A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $\mathfrak m_x=\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}$. Thus
\[
\kappa(x)=A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}\cong(A/\mathfrak p)_{(0)},
\]
which is the fraction field of the domain $A/\mathfrak p$.
