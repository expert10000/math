\subsection*{CP-VI-0070: The stalk of the structure sheaf}

For $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $\mathfrak p\in X$, prove that
\[
\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}\cong A_{\mathfrak p}.
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The basic opens $D(f)$ with $f\notin\mathfrak p$ form a neighborhood basis of $\mathfrak p$. Hence
\[
\mathcal O_{X,\mathfrak p}
=\varinjlim_{\mathfrak p\in D(f)}\Gamma(D(f),\mathcal O_X)
=\varinjlim_{f\notin\mathfrak p}A_f.
\]
The directed colimit localizes $A$ at all elements outside $\mathfrak p$, which is exactly $A_{\mathfrak p}$.
