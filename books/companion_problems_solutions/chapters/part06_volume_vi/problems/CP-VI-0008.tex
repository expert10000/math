\begin{exercise}
	Describe
	\[
	\operatorname{Spec} k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2).
	\]
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
Let $A=k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$. Since $\varepsilon$ is nilpotent, every prime ideal contains $\varepsilon$. The quotient $A/(\varepsilon)\cong k$ has the single prime $(0)$, so $A$ has exactly one prime, namely $(\varepsilon)$. Thus $\operatorname{Spec} A$ is a one-point topological space, but its local ring contains the nonzero nilpotent class of $\varepsilon$; the scheme is nonreduced.
