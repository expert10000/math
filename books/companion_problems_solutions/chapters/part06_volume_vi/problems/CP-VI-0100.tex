\subsection*{CP-VI-0100: Smoothness of a plane cubic}

Assume $\operatorname{char}k\ne2,3$. For the projective cubic
\[
E: Y^2Z=X^3+aXZ^2+bZ^3,
\]
show that $E$ is smooth exactly when $4a^3+27b^2\ne0$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Set $F=Y^2Z-X^3-aXZ^2-bZ^3$. A singular point must satisfy $F=F_X=F_Y=F_Z=0$. In the affine chart $Z=1$, these equations become $y^2=x^3+ax+b$, $2y=0$, and $3x^2+a=0$. Thus $y=0$ and $x$ is a common root of $x^3+ax+b$ and its derivative $3x^2+a$. Such a common root exists exactly when the cubic discriminant $-4a^3-27b^2$ vanishes. The point at infinity $[0:1:0]$ is smooth because $F_Z=Y^2$ there. Hence smoothness is equivalent to $4a^3+27b^2\ne0$.
