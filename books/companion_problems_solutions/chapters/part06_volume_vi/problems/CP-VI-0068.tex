\subsection*{CP-VI-0068: Sheafification preserves stalks}

Let $F$ be a presheaf and $F^+$ its sheafification. State and prove, using germs, why the canonical map $F\to F^+$ induces an isomorphism $F_x\cong F^+_x$ for every $x\in X$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

A section of $F^+$ is locally represented by sections of $F$. Hence every germ of $F^+$ at $x$ has, after shrinking to a neighborhood of $x$, a representative from $F$, proving surjectivity on stalks. If two germs from $F_x$ become equal in $F^+_x$, their sheafified representatives agree on some neighborhood of $x$; by the construction of sheafification this equality is already local in $F$, so the original germs coincide. Thus $F_x\to F^+_x$ is bijective (and respects the relevant algebraic structure).
