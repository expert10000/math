\subsection*{CP-VI-0076: Base change of a closed immersion}

Let $i:X\hookrightarrow Y$ be a closed immersion and $Y'\to Y$ any morphism. Prove that the base change $X\times_Y Y'\to Y'$ is again a closed immersion.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The claim is local on $Y$ and $Y'$. Write $Y=\operatorname{Spec}A$, $X=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$, and $Y'=\operatorname{Spec}B$. Then
\[
X\times_Y Y'\cong\operatorname{Spec}((A/I)\otimes_A B)
\cong\operatorname{Spec}(B/IB).
\]
The map to $\operatorname{Spec}B$ is induced by the quotient $B\twoheadrightarrow B/IB$, hence is a closed immersion.
