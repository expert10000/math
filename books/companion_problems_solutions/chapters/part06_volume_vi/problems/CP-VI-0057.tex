\subsection*{CP-VI-0057: Recovering an affine morphism from its pullback}

Let $X\subseteq\mathbb A_k^m$ and $Y\subseteq\mathbb A_k^n$. Given a $k$-algebra homomorphism $\theta:k[Y]\to k[X]$, show how to construct a morphism $\varphi:X\to Y$ with $\varphi^*=\theta$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Let $\bar y_i\in k[Y]$ be the coordinate classes and choose polynomial representatives $f_i\in k[x_1,\dots,x_m]$ for $\theta(\bar y_i)$. Define $\varphi(a)=(f_1(a),\dots,f_n(a))$. If $g\in I(Y)$, then $g(\bar y_1,\dots,\bar y_n)=0$ in $k[Y]$; applying $\theta$ gives $g(f_1,\dots,f_n)=0$ in $k[X]$, so $\varphi(a)\in Y$. By construction the pullback of each coordinate function is $\theta(\bar y_i)$, hence $\varphi^*=\theta$.
