\begin{exercise}
	Compare topologically
	\[
	\operatorname{Spec} k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)
	\]
	and
	\[
	\operatorname{Spec} k.
	\]
\end{exercise}

\subsection*{Canonical solution}
Both spectra consist of a single point. Indeed $\operatorname{Spec} k$ has the unique prime $(0)$, while $\operatorname{Spec} k[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)$ has the unique prime $(\varepsilon)$. Hence the underlying topological spaces are homeomorphic. They are not isomorphic as schemes: the first is reduced, whereas the second has the nonzero nilpotent $\varepsilon$.
