\subsection*{CP-VI-0078: Fiber over a point}

Let $f:X\to S$ be a morphism and $s\in S$. Define the fiber $X_s$ and, in the affine case $X=\operatorname{Spec}B$, $S=\operatorname{Spec}A$, compute its coordinate ring.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The scheme-theoretic fiber is
\[
X_s=X\times_S\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s).
\]
If $s$ corresponds to $\mathfrak p\subseteq A$, then
\[
X_s\cong\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p)).
\]
This retains nilpotents and residue-field information, so it is finer than merely taking the set-theoretic inverse image.
