\subsection*{CP-VI-0065: Restriction maps and functoriality}

Let $F$ be a presheaf on $X$. For open sets $W\subseteq V\subseteq U$, prove that the restriction maps satisfy
\[
\rho^U_W=\rho^V_W\circ\rho^U_V,
\]
and explain why a presheaf is equivalently a contravariant functor from the poset of open subsets of $X$ to sets (or rings, groups, modules).

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The displayed identity, together with $\rho^U_U=\mathrm{id}$, is precisely the presheaf functoriality axiom. Regard the poset of opens as a category with a unique arrow $V\to U$ when $V\subseteq U$. A contravariant functor sends this inclusion to a map $F(U)\to F(V)$, namely restriction, and functoriality gives exactly the two presheaf identities. Conversely these identities define such a contravariant functor.
