\subsection*{CP-VI-0083: Codimension of a coordinate subspace}

In $\mathbb A_k^n$, let $L=V(x_1,\dots,x_r)$. Compute $\dim L$ and $\operatorname{codim}_{\mathbb A^n}L$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The coordinate ring is
\[
k[L]=k[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(x_1,\dots,x_r)\cong k[x_{r+1},\dots,x_n],
\]
which has dimension $n-r$. Since $\dim\mathbb A^n=n$, the codimension is $r$. Equivalently, the prime ideal $(x_1,\dots,x_r)$ has height $r$.
