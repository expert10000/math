\subsection*{CP-VI-0077: Base change of an open immersion}

Prove that the base change of an open immersion is an open immersion. Verify this explicitly for $D(f)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A$ after base change along $A\to B$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Open immersions are local, so it suffices to check a basic affine open. We have $D(f)=\operatorname{Spec}A_f$. After base change,
\[
\operatorname{Spec}A_f\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}B
\cong\operatorname{Spec}(A_f\otimes_A B)
\cong\operatorname{Spec}B_{\bar f}=D(\bar f)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}B.
\]
Thus the pullback is again an open immersion; arbitrary open immersions follow by covering with basic opens.
