\subsection*{CP-VI-0107: Čech complex for a two-open cover}

Let $X=U\cup V$ and $F$ be a sheaf of abelian groups. Write the first terms of the Čech complex for the cover $\{U,V\}$ and describe $\check H^0$ and $\check H^1$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The complex is
\[
0\to F(U)\oplus F(V)\xrightarrow{\delta}F(U\cap V)\to0,
\]
with $\delta(s_U,s_V)=s_V|_{U\cap V}-s_U|_{U\cap V}$. Its zeroth cohomology is the kernel, namely pairs of sections agreeing on the overlap; by the sheaf axiom this is $F(X)$. Its first cohomology is
\[
\check H^1(\{U,V\},F)=F(U\cap V)/\operatorname{im}\delta.
\]
