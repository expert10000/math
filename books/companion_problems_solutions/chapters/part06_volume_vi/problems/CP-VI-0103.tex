\subsection*{CP-VI-0103: Exceptional coordinate lines of the Cremona map}

For the standard quadratic Cremona transformation, determine the image of the line $x=0$ away from the base points, and similarly for $y=0$ and $z=0$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

On $x=0$ with $yz\ne0$,
\[
[0:y:z]\mapsto[yz:0:0]=[1:0:0].
\]
Thus the line $x=0$ is contracted to the opposite base point. Cyclically, $y=0$ is contracted to $[0:1:0]$ and $z=0$ to $[0:0:1]$. These are the exceptional curves of the birational transformation on $\mathbb P^2$.
