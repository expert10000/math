\subsection*{CP-VI-0088: A standard affine chart of Proj}

Let $S=k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$. Show that
\[
D_+(x_0)\subseteq\operatorname{Proj}S
\]
is isomorphic to $\mathbb A_k^n$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The standard-open formula gives
\[
D_+(x_0)\cong\operatorname{Spec}(S_{x_0})_0,
\]
where the subscript $0$ denotes the degree-zero part. Every degree-zero element is a polynomial in $x_1/x_0,\dots,x_n/x_0$, so
\[
(S_{x_0})_0\cong k[x_1/x_0,\dots,x_n/x_0]\cong k[t_1,\dots,t_n].
\]
Thus $D_+(x_0)\cong\mathbb A_k^n$.
