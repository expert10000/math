\subsection*{CP-VI-0104: Affine charts of the blow-up of the plane}

Describe the blow-up of $\mathbb A_k^2=\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y]$ at the origin using two affine charts, and identify the exceptional divisor in each chart.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The blow-up is the closed subscheme of $\mathbb A^2\times\mathbb P^1$ defined by $xV=yU$. On the chart $U\ne0$, put $v=V/U$; then $y=xv$ and the chart is $\operatorname{Spec}k[x,v]$. The exceptional divisor is $x=0$. On $V\ne0$, put $u=U/V$; then $x=yu$ and the chart is $\operatorname{Spec}k[u,y]$, with exceptional divisor $y=0$. On the overlap $u=v^{-1}$, so the exceptional divisor glues to $\mathbb P^1$.
