\subsection*{CP-VI-0061: A generic point of the affine line}

Let $k$ be a field and $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]$. Identify the generic point of $X$, compute its closure, and explain why it is not closed.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Because $k[t]$ is a domain, $(0)$ is prime. Its closure is $V((0))=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]=X$, so $(0)$ is the generic point. It is not maximal (for example $(0)\subsetneq(t)$), hence it is not closed.
