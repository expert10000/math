\subsection*{CP-VI-0079: Normalization of a cusp}

Let $A=k[t^2,t^3]\subseteq k[t]$. Show that $t$ is integral over $A$, that $k[t]$ is finite over $A$, and that $k[t]$ is the normalization of $A$ in $k(t)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The element $t$ satisfies the monic equation $T^2-t^2=0$ with coefficient $t^2\in A$, so it is integral over $A$. Since $k[t]=A+At$, it is generated by $1,t$ as an $A$-module and is therefore finite. The fraction fields of $A$ and $k[t]$ are both $k(t)$ because $t=t^3/t^2$. The PID $k[t]$ is integrally closed. Hence every element of $k(t)$ integral over $A$ lies in the integral closure, which is contained in $k[t]$; conversely $k[t]$ is integral over $A$. Thus the normalization is $k[t]$.
