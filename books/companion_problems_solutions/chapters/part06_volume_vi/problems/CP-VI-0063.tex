\subsection*{CP-VI-0063: The local ring at a point of the affine line}

For $X=\mathbb A_k^1$ and the closed point $a\in k$, describe $\mathcal O_{X,a}$ explicitly. Determine which rational functions belong to it and compute its maximal ideal and residue field.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The point corresponds to $\mathfrak m_a=(t-a)\subset k[t]$. Hence
\[
\mathcal O_{X,a}=k[t]_{(t-a)}
=\left\{\frac{f(t)}{g(t)}:g(a)\ne0\right\}.
\]
Its unique maximal ideal is generated by $t-a$, consisting of fractions whose numerator vanishes at $a$. Evaluation at $a$ induces the quotient map $\mathcal O_{X,a}\to k$, so the residue field is $k$.
