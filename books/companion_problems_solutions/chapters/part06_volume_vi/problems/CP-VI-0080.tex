\subsection*{CP-VI-0080: Dimension of a polynomial ring in one variable}

Prove that $\dim k[t]=1$ for a field $k$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The chain $(0)\subsetneq(t)$ shows $\dim k[t]\ge1$. Since $k[t]$ is a PID, every nonzero prime ideal is generated by an irreducible polynomial and is maximal. Thus a strict chain of prime ideals can have at most the form $(0)\subsetneq\mathfrak m$, so its length is at most one. Hence $\dim k[t]=1$.
