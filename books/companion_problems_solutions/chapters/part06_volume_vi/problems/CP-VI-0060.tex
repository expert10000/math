\subsection*{CP-VI-0060: Closed points over an algebraically closed field}

Let $k$ be algebraically closed. Show that the closed points of $\operatorname{Spec}k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ are exactly the maximal ideals
\[
(x_1-a_1,\dots,x_n-a_n),\qquad a_i\in k.
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

A point $\mathfrak p$ of a spectrum is closed exactly when $V(\mathfrak p)=\{\mathfrak p\}$, equivalently when $\mathfrak p$ is maximal. By Hilbert's Nullstellensatz every maximal ideal of the polynomial ring over an algebraically closed field is $(x_1-a_1,\dots,x_n-a_n)$ for a unique point $a\in k^n$. Conversely each such ideal has quotient $k$, hence is maximal and defines a closed point.
