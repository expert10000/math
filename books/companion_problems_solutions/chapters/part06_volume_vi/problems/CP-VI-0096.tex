\subsection*{CP-VI-0096: Cartier divisor from a regular function}

Let $f\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$ be a non-zero-divisor on a scheme $X$. Show that $f$ defines an effective Cartier divisor and identify its ideal sheaf.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

On each open set, multiplication by $f$ embeds $\mathcal O_X$ into itself because $f$ is a non-zero-divisor. The image $f\mathcal O_X$ is an invertible ideal sheaf locally generated by one non-zero-divisor. The closed subscheme defined by this ideal is therefore an effective Cartier divisor, with ideal sheaf $\mathcal I_D=f\mathcal O_X$.
