\subsection*{CP-VI-0096: Cartier divisor from a regular function}

Let $X$ be a scheme and $f\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$ a section
whose germ $f_x\in\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a non-zero-divisor for every
$x\in X$. Show that the zero scheme of $f$ is an effective Cartier
divisor and identify its ideal sheaf.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

Multiplication by $f$ is injective on every stalk and therefore
as a morphism of sheaves:
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X\xrightarrow{\,\cdot f\,}\mathcal O_X.
\]
Its image $f\mathcal O_X$ is an ideal sheaf isomorphic to
$\mathcal O_X$, hence invertible. Locally it is generated by the
non-zero-divisor $f_x$, so the closed subscheme cut out by
$f\mathcal O_X$ is an effective Cartier divisor $D$.
The ideal sheaf is
\[
\boxed{\mathcal I_D=f\mathcal O_X.}
\]
The stalkwise hypothesis is essential: the assertion that $f$ is
merely a non-zero-divisor in $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)$ does not by
itself express the required local regularity condition.
