\subsection*{CP-VI-0094: Divisor of a rational function on the projective line}

On $\mathbb P_k^1$ with affine coordinate $t=X_1/X_0$, compute $\operatorname{div}(t)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The function $t$ has a simple zero at $t=0$, corresponding to $[1:0]$. Near infinity use $s=1/t=X_0/X_1$; then $t=s^{-1}$ has a simple pole at $s=0$, corresponding to $[0:1]$. Hence
\[
\operatorname{div}(t)=[0]-[\infty].
\]
Its total degree is zero, as expected for a principal divisor on a proper curve.
