\subsection*{CP-VI-0056: Pullback of regular functions}

Let $\varphi:X\to Y$ be a morphism of affine algebraic sets. Prove that composition defines a $k$-algebra homomorphism
\[
\varphi^*:k[Y]\to k[X],\qquad f\mapsto f\circ\varphi.
\]
Show that $(\psi\circ\varphi)^*=\varphi^*\circ\psi^*$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

A regular function on $Y$ composed with the polynomial coordinate functions of $\varphi$ is regular on $X$. Composition preserves sums, products, and constants, so $\varphi^*$ is a $k$-algebra homomorphism. For $g\in k[Z]$,
\[
((\psi\circ\varphi)^*g)(x)=g(\psi(\varphi(x)))=(\varphi^*(\psi^*g))(x),
\]
so the functor is contravariant.
