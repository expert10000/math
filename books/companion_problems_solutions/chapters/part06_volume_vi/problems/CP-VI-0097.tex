\subsection*{CP-VI-0097: Principal divisors vanish in the class group}

Let $X$ be an integral normal Noetherian scheme. Explain why the divisor class group is
\[
\operatorname{Cl}(X)=\operatorname{Div}(X)/\operatorname{Prin}(X)
\]
and why every principal divisor has trivial class.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The group $\operatorname{Div}(X)$ is the free abelian group on codimension-one integral closed subschemes. Rational functions give the subgroup $\operatorname{Prin}(X)=\{\operatorname{div}(f):f\in K(X)^\times\}$. By definition the class group is the quotient by this subgroup. Hence $\operatorname{div}(f)$ maps to zero for every rational function $f$.
