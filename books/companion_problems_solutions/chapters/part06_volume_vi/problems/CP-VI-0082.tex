\subsection*{CP-VI-0082: Codimension of a hypersurface}

Let $A=k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ and let $0\ne f\in A$ be nonconstant and irreducible. Show that $V(f)\subseteq\mathbb A_k^n$ has codimension $1$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The ideal $(f)$ is prime because $A$ is a UFD and $f$ is irreducible. By Krull's principal ideal theorem, every minimal prime over a nonzero principal ideal in the Noetherian ring $A$ has height at most one. Since $(f)$ is itself a nonzero prime and $(0)\subsetneq(f)$, its height is at least one. Hence $\operatorname{ht}(f)=1$, so the irreducible closed subset $V(f)$ has codimension one.
