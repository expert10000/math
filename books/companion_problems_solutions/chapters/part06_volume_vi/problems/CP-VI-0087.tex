\subsection*{CP-VI-0087: The irrelevant ideal}

For the standard graded ring $S=k[x_0,\dots,x_n]$, let $S_+=(x_0,\dots,x_n)$. Show that a homogeneous prime $\mathfrak p$ represents a point of $\operatorname{Proj}S$ exactly when $S_+\not\subseteq\mathfrak p$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

This is the defining exclusion in $\operatorname{Proj}S$: one takes homogeneous prime ideals that do not contain the irrelevant ideal $S_+$. Equivalently, at least one degree-one coordinate $x_i$ is not in $\mathfrak p$. Hence every Proj point lies in some standard open $D_+(x_i)$, and these opens cover $\operatorname{Proj}S$.
