\subsection*{CP-VI-0102: The standard quadratic Cremona map}

Consider
\[
\phi:\mathbb P^2\dashrightarrow\mathbb P^2,
\qquad [x:y:z]\mapsto[yz:xz:xy].
\]
Find its base points and show that $\phi$ is an involution on the dense open set $xyz\ne0$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

All three coordinates $yz,xz,xy$ vanish simultaneously exactly at $[1:0:0]$, $[0:1:0]$, and $[0:0:1]$, the base points. On $xyz\ne0$,
\[
\phi^2([x:y:z])=[(xz)(xy):(yz)(xy):(yz)(xz)]
=[x^2yz:xy^2z:xyz^2]=[x:y:z].
\]
Thus the rational map is birational and is its own inverse on that dense open set.
