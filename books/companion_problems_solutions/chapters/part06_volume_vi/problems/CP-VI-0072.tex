\subsection*{CP-VI-0072: Gluing two affine lines}

Glue two copies $U=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]$ and $V=\operatorname{Spec}k[s]$ along $D(t)\subset U$ and $D(s)\subset V$ using the isomorphism $s=t^{-1}$. Identify the resulting scheme.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

The overlap rings are $k[t,t^{-1}]$ and $k[s,s^{-1}]$, and the map $s\mapsto t^{-1}$ is an isomorphism. Gluing the two affine charts along these punctured lines produces the standard two-chart construction of $\mathbb P_k^1$: on $D_+(x_0)$ use $t=x_1/x_0$, on $D_+(x_1)$ use $s=x_0/x_1$, and on the overlap $st=1$.
