\subsection*{CP-VI-0069: Sections of the structure sheaf on a basic open}

Let $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $f\in A$. Prove the fundamental affine formula
\[
\Gamma(D(f),\mathcal O_X)\cong A_f.
\]
Explain the case $f=1$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Solution.}

By construction of the affine structure sheaf, a section on $D(f)$ is locally represented by fractions whose denominators avoid the primes under consideration. The universal localization map $A\to A_f$ gives such sections, and the standard gluing argument on a cover by smaller basic opens shows every section comes uniquely from an element of $A_f$. Therefore $\Gamma(D(f),\mathcal O_X)=A_f$. For $f=1$, $D(1)=X$ and $A_1=A$, giving $\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)=A$.
