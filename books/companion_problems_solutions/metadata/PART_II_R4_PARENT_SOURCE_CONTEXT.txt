  2060: \end{enumerate}

  2062: \paragraph{Theory (toolkit).}
  2063: \begin{itemize}\itemsep3pt
  2064: 	\item Dyadic intervals: $I_{n,k}=[k2^{-n},(k+1)2^{-n})$.
  2065: 	\item $\psi_{n,k}$ is supported on $I_{n,k}$ and has zero mean.
  2066: 	\item Dyadic conditional expectations $P_m f$: step functions constant on $\{I_{m,k}\}$ with $P_m f\to f$ in $L^2$ for $f\in L^2$.
  2067: \end{itemize}
  2068: 
  2069: \paragraph{Solution.}
  2070: \emph{(a) Orthonormality.} If $(n_1,k_1)\neq(n_2,k_2)$ then either supports are disjoint (integral $0$) or,
  2071: when $n_1\neq n_2$, the finer wavelet integrates to $0$ on each child of the coarser support, so the integral vanishes.
  2072: Also $\|\psi_{n,k}\|_2^2=\|\psi\|_2^2=1$.
  2073: 
  2074: \smallskip
  2075: \emph{(b) Density on indicators.} For a finite interval $I$, the dyadic step functions $P_m\mathbf 1_I$ converge to
  2076: $\mathbf 1_I$ in $L^2$. Each $P_m\mathbf 1_I$ is a finite combination of $\psi_{n,k}$ (Haar expansion of step functions),
  2077: hence $\mathbf 1_I\in\overline{\mathrm{Span}}\{\psi_{n,k}\}$.
  2078: 
  2079: \smallskip
  2080: \emph{(c) Basis.} Orthonormality from (a) and density from (b) imply $\{\psi_{n,k}\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $L^2(\mathbb{R})$.
  2081: 
  2082: \paragraph{Examples.}
  2083: \begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
  2084: 	\item $f=\mathbf 1_{[0,1)}=\tfrac12(\mathbf 1_{[0,1/2)}+\mathbf 1_{[1/2,1)})$ has a finite Haar expansion on level $n=0,1$.
