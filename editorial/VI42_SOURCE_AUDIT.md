## Mathematical guardrails

1. `CaCl(X) ≅ Pic(X)` is stated for **integral schemes**, where every invertible sheaf has a nonzero rational section from its generic stalk.
2. `Pic(X) → Cl(X)` is stated under **normal Noetherian integral** hypotheses, matching the Cartier-to-Weil framework developed in VI/40–VI/41.
3. `Pic(X) ≅ Cl(X)` is asserted only when every Weil divisor is Cartier, e.g. on locally factorial normal Noetherian integral schemes.
4. Affineness alone is **not** used to infer `Pic(X)=0`.
5. The quadric-cone calculation uses the already proved non-Cartier nature of `L=V(x,z)`; it does not infer `Pic(X)=0` merely from affineness.
6. The punctured cone computation uses regularity/local factoriality plus codimension-two invariance of `Cl`.
7. A nonzero section yields an effective Cartier divisor only under the stated integral/generically nonzero hypotheses.
8. The identification `Pic(X) ≅ H^1(X,O_X^×)` is mentioned only as a preview; no cohomological machinery is assumed here.
9. The plane-cubic map `P ↦ O(P-O)` is introduced only as a bridge; the group-law theorem is deferred to VI/43.
