## New canonical extensions

VI/38 adds the material required to complete the projective arc rigorously:

- the correct domain of a Proj morphism induced by a graded map;
- the irrelevant-ideal/radical criterion for global definition;
- positive-degree homogeneous maps via Veronese regrading;
- base loci and maps defined by same-degree forms;
- affine-local characterization of closed immersions;
- homogeneous quotient closed immersions;
- relative projective space and projective morphisms;
- base-change and composition stability;
- relative very ampleness;
- full ring-theoretic proof of the Veronese closed embedding;
- the Segre product, product-chart theorem, determinantal kernel, and full Segre closed-embedding proof;
- composition of projective morphisms via relative Segre.
