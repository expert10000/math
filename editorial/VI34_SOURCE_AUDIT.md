# VI/34 source audit - Graded Rings

## Legacy support
The project audit identifies `theory-of-algebraic-geometry-4.tex` as containing graded rings, homogeneous primes, the irrelevant ideal, degree-zero homogeneous localization, `Proj S`, its structure sheaf, standard opens, projective space, and closed projective subschemes.

After the VI/33 architecture insertion, the canonical thematic selector `graded ring|homogeneous` belongs to VI/34.

## Retained in VI/34
- graded rings and homogeneous elements;
- homogeneous ideals, radicals, quotients, and primes;
- irrelevant ideal;
- homogeneous localization;
- degree-zero localization.

## Canonical extensions
- graded modules and homogeneous maps;
- degree shifts and graded exact sequences;
- degree-zero localization of modules;
- Veronese subrings;
- weighted gradings;
- affine cones and scaling;
- explicit algebraic bridge to VI/35.

## Deferred
- topology and definition of `Proj S`;
- structure sheaf on `Proj S`;
- geometric standard opens `D_+(f)`;
- projective space and projective subschemes;
- twisting sheaves as globally glued sheaves.

No separately numbered legacy problem is claimed because available migration evidence is thematic rather than problem-ID specific.
