# C01 reader-facing editorial cleanup — provenance note

C01 separates mathematical exposition from internal production metadata.

Reader-facing chapters, problems, exercises, and solutions describe mathematics and pedagogy rather
than repository workflow, source-allocation bookkeeping, corpus ownership, legacy status, dossier
status, migration state, or reconstruction state.

Formal source lineage is preserved in the repository's audit, source-migration, and provenance records.
Those formal records are intentionally excluded from the reader-facing QA.

During C01, bookkeeping-only reader exercises discovered by the exhaustive gate were replaced by
mathematics, including:
- VI/23: base change for fiber products via the universal property;
- VI/29: prime chains, irreducible closed subsets, and Krull dimension;
- VIII/30: clutching maps and bundle classification over spheres.

Support-diagram notes in VIII/18, VIII/25, and VIII/28 were rewritten as ordinary mathematical
exposition.
