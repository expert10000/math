## Mathematical guardrails

1. The UFD criterion is stated for **normal Noetherian domains**, where height-one local rings are DVRs and the Krull intersection formula is available.
2. `factorial` and `locally factorial` are kept distinct. A locally factorial scheme can have nonzero global class group.
3. The localization theorem kills exactly the classes of height-one primes meeting the multiplicative set.
4. Removing codimension at least two preserves the Weil divisor class group on a normal Noetherian integral scheme.
5. The quadric-cone nonprincipality proof rules out rational equations, not merely regular equations: if `div(f)=L`, then `f^2/x` has divisor zero and is a unit; this would force a square to have odd `x`-adic valuation in `k(x,z)`.
6. The cone class-group proof uses localization at `x` to prove generation before using `div(x)=2L` as a relation.
7. The punctured cone is distinguished from `D(x)`: removing the codimension-two vertex preserves the class, while removing the codimension-one divisor `L` kills it.
8. The chapter introduces `Q`-Cartier only as a bridge and explicitly warns that global torsion of `Cl(X)` is stronger than local `Q`-factoriality.
9. The full Cartier/Picard quotient is deferred rather than assumed.
