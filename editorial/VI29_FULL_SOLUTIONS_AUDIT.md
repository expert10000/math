- chain length conventions;
- the prime-chain / irreducible-closed-chain dictionary;
- height as `dim A_p`;
- quotient and localization dimension inequalities;
- invariance under reduction;
- product dimensions;
- localization examples with strict drop and preserved dimension;
- infinite Krull dimension via unbounded finite chains;
- dimension preservation under integral extensions using going up + incomparability;
