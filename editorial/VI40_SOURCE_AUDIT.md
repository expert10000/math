## Reconstruction rule used

chapter source.  Therefore this package does **not** claim verbatim recovery.  The ledger
is used as provenance and scope evidence, and the chapter is a new canonical treatment.

## Mathematical guardrails

1. Cartier pullback is **not** stated for arbitrary morphisms without qualification.
   Pullback is defined when the pulled-back local equations lie in the unit group of the
   total quotient sheaf of the source.
2. For an effective Cartier divisor on a locally Noetherian source, avoiding images of
   associated points is recorded as the practical non-zero-divisor criterion.
3. Cartier-to-Weil is developed on normal Noetherian integral schemes so codimension-one
   local rings are DVRs.
4. The comparison map is proved injective in that normal setting; surjectivity is stated
   under locally factorial hypotheses rather than in general.
5. Effective Cartier divisors require local equations that are non-zero-divisors.  A
   principal ideal generated by a zero-divisor is not enough.
6. The convention is `O_X(D)|_U = f^{-1} O_U` for a local Cartier equation `f`; hence
   `O_X(-D)` is the ideal sheaf of an effective Cartier divisor.
