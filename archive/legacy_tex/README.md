# Legacy TeX Archive

This is the eventual provenance home for historical standalone TeX sources after canonical migration.

**Do not bulk-move `chapters/tex/` here in the architecture commit.** A source may move only after all of its rows in `editorial/SOURCE_MIGRATION.tsv` have been audited and its unique mathematical content has a canonical destination. Until then, `chapters/tex/` remains the preserved working legacy source tree.

Merged exercise snapshots, verified duplicates, and provenance-only variants can be moved earlier once their `DUPLICATE_MAP.tsv` classification is confirmed.
