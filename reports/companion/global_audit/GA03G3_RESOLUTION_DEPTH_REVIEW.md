# GA-03g.3 — Resolution and depth consistency

Curated mathematical review; lexical anchor verification is not proof certification.

## CP-V-0041 — PERIODIC_RESOLUTION

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

For A=k[x,y]/(xy), d1=(x y), d2=diag(y,x), d3=diag(x,y); ranks 1,2,2,... give Tor_0(k,k)=k, Tor_i(k,k)=k^2 for i>=1. Ext^1_A(k,A)=k, Ext^i=0 for i!=1.

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\ker d_1=(y)\\times(x); \\operatorname{Tor}_i^A(k,k)\\cong k^2; \\operatorname{Ext}_A^1(k,A)\\cong k`

## CP-V-0042 — MINIMAL_RESOLUTION_EXISTENCE

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

Repeated minimal free covers are possible since the ring is Noetherian local and each successive kernel is finitely generated; all matrices have entries in the maximal ideal.

Anchors requiring inspection: `K_1:=\\ker; \\mathfrak mF_0; d_2\\otimes_Ak=0`

## CP-V-0043 — BETTI_AND_PROJECTIVE_DIMENSION

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

In a minimal finite-free resolution, differential maps vanish modulo m; beta_i=dim_k Tor_i(k,M)=rank F_i. When pd M is finite, the last nonzero beta index equals pd M.

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\operatorname{Tor}_i^A(k,M); \\operatorname{Tor}_{n+1}^A(k,M)=0`

## CP-V-0111 — SCHANUEL_STABILITY

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT_WITH_DISTINCTION; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

Pullback gives K + Fprime ≅ Kprime + F; stable syzygy equivalence does not imply equality of arbitrary free resolution terms or Betti numbers.

Anchors requiring inspection: `K\\oplus F\u0027\\cong K\u0027\\oplus F; \\cong K\\oplus F\u0027`

## CP-V-0112 — EXPLICIT_SYZYGIES

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEWED

For (x^2,xy,y^2) in k[x,y], the syzygy module is generated by (-y,x,0) and (0,-y,x); distinct from the Koszul resolution of k in CP-V-0079.

## CP-V-0045 — MINIMALITY_EXT_MAP

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

Maps represented by matrices with entries in m induce zero on Ext_A^i(k,-), since m annihilates Ext_A^i(k,A) for the commutative local ring.

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\operatorname{im}f\\subseteq\\mathfrak mF`

## CP-V-0044 — AUSLANDER_BUCHSBAUM

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT_WITH_HYPOTHESES; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

Auslander–Buchsbaum requires nonzero finitely generated M, finite projective dimension, and Noetherian local A. CP-V-0041 has pd_A(k)=infinity, so AB is not applied to that module.

Anchors requiring inspection: `n=\\operatorname{pd}_A M; \\operatorname{depth}K; \\operatorname{depth}M=s-n`

## CP-V-0046 — TOR_LONG_EXACT_SEQUENCE

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

The degreewise tensor sequences are exact because the resolving F_i are free/flat; the connecting map comes from the short exact sequence of complexes.

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\partial_n; F_i\\otimes_A N`

## CP-V-0047 — CYCLIC_TOR

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

The length-one Z-free resolution yields Tor_1^Z(Z/n,N)=N[n], and for N=Z/m the cyclic group of order gcd(n,m).

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\gcd(n,m)`

## CP-V-0048 — INTERSECTION_FORMULA

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

The natural isomorphism Tor_1^A(A/I,A/J)=(I intersection J)/IJ comes from the kernel of I/IJ -> A/J.

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\frac{I\\cap J}{IJ}`

## CP-V-0113 — CYCLIC_EXT

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT_WITH_DOMAIN; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

For positive integers n,m, Ext_Z^1(Z/n,Z/m)=Z/gcd(n,m). The statement should explicitly impose n,m>=1, as in CP-V-0047.

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\operatorname{Ext}^1_; \\gcd(n,m)`

## CP-V-0114 — DERIVED_EXACTNESS

**Assessment:** CONSISTENT; **evidence:** REVIEW_ANCHORS

Tor is the left derived tensor functor, Ext the right derived Hom functor; the long exact sequences encode the respective failure of exactness.

Anchors requiring inspection: `\\operatorname{Tor}_1; \\operatorname{Ext}_R^1`
