## Checked arguments and retained standard dependencies

- Normal cones xy=z^n: their hypersurface rings satisfy S2 and are regular away from the codimension-two vertex, including characteristics dividing n. Thus the R1/S2 normality criterion applies. At (x,z), y is a unit, z is a uniformizer, and ord_D(x)=n. Units of k[x,x^-1,z] are c*x^m, so the complete relation subgroup is nZ, not merely a displayed torsion relation.
- Cone xy=zw: the analogous normality argument and the unit group k^*x^Z give the single relation D+E. The quotient Z^2/Z(1,1) is Z.
- P^1 and P^1 x P^1: the boundary-unit arguments and residue-field degrees give the stated class groups and bidegrees, with rational ruling conventions now explicit.
- The no-map theorem P^n -> P^r for r<n depends on Pic(P^n)=Z and the height bound for an ideal generated by r+1 forms. Its projective zero is a scheme/geometric point, not necessarily k-rational.
- Veronese embeddings use the standard affine-chart description of the Veronese map; the conic XZ-Y^2 is smooth also in characteristic 2. Projective power maps need not be separable; the new chart proof establishes finiteness in every characteristic.
- Quasi-coherent kernels, images, cokernels, pullbacks and affine pushforwards reduce to exact localization, tensor product or restriction of scalars. Coherent claims retain their locally Noetherian hypotheses; the proper-pushforward theorem and the quasi-compact/quasi-separated pushforward theorem are standard dependencies.
- Closed immersions use the standard affine closed-subspace theorem. The global ideal correspondence is reconstructed by localization on common distinguished refinements and gluing. The example quotients, nilpotent lengths and Koszul relation (-y,x) were checked directly.
