# Volume I — full prerequisite and notation repair

This pass extends the earlier five-chapter audit to the full I/01--I/18 dependency chain.

- Formal uses of kernel/image, trace, operator similarity, diagonalizability and SVD-era numerical language were either moved to prior material or explicitly marked as previews.
- The complex inner-product convention is now canonical: linear in the first argument, conjugate-linear in the second.
- $A^*$ denotes conjugate transpose before Chapter 16; Chapter 16 then identifies it with the adjoint matrix.
